### ⭐⭐⭐ How Language Models Organize Task Information
**6/10** · Kaiyang Wan、Yuxia Wang · arXiv (Cornell University) · 2026-10-20 · [原文](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2610.27724)
*入选：能力迁移*

> 📄 **摘要**：Dense linear probes assess task readability from language model (LM) representations, but do not directly reveal how many features suffice for prediction. We measure readout sparsity with \(k^*\), the smallest feature count found by a fixed search protocol that recovers 95% of dense-probe AUROC above chance. We evaluate 19 LMs from five labs on 14 core tasks. Ten base models give the nine reliably readable tasks similar feature counts and nearly the same order. This agreement extends to the selected features themselves, which on three tasks show cross-model response correspondence beyond class-mean differences and a label-correlation-matched null. Instruction-tuned deployment selectively reduces \(k^*\), with both weights and input format contributing. In controlled synthetic experiments across 20 models, successfully learned targets admit single-feature readouts, whereas readable controls require tens of features. Reference-model \(k^*\) is associated with label requirements across eight students, following an empirical scaling of \(N^*\propto(k^*)^{0.65}\). After continual learning, saved heads can fail while linear readability remains high, and recovery requires fewer labels than initial task learning under the tested procedures. Pruning reduces linear readability, with wider pre-pruning readouts generally associated with larger losses. These findings distinguish task readability from readout sparsity and establish their complementary value for predicting learning requirements and diagnosing LM changes.

**一句话**：该研究提出用读出稀疏度 k* 衡量语言模型表示中一个任务需要多少个特征才能被预测，并在 19 个模型 × 14 个任务上考察跨模型一致性与持续学习后的变化。

痛点：稠密线性探针只能说明任务信息“可读”，却不能说明表示中有多少特征真正承载预测信息，也无法刻画任务信息在模型间是否同构。方法：定义 k*（固定搜索协议下恢复稠密探针 95% 超额 AUROC 所需的最小特征数），在 5 家机构的 19 个 LM、14 个核心任务上测量，并做合成对照实验、跨模型特征响应对应分析与持续学习/剪枝实验。结果：基础模型对可读任务给出相近的 k* 与几乎相同的特征排序，所选特征本身也跨模型对应；指令微调会选择性降低 k*；持续学习后保存的头可能失效而线性可读性仍高，且恢复所需标注少于初始学习。

> 🔗 **与课题**：主要支撑⑦评测与⑥训练/后训练中的持续学习子项：k* 提供了一种“内部知识紧凑度/可读性”的量化探针协议，可类比用于检验 GEE 上习得的 workflow 知识是否以紧凑、可线性读出的形式存在于模型内部，从而为“内部可迁移工作流知识”这一中介变量提供可操作度量；跨模型特征响应一致性分析为③能力迁移提供了表示层面的对应性证据范式（源域表示特征在目标模型/目标域是否保持对应）；持续学习后“head 失效但线性可读性仍高”的现象与恢复标注效率，可直接对应工作流知识在跨平台微调（GEE→QGIS）中是否被覆盖、需要多少监督才能恢复。但它不研究任务分解/规划（①）、结构化/DAG 生成（②⑤）、算子 grounding（④），也不绑定 tool/code/workflow 场景，故仅为方法论层面的支撑，非核心竞品。

💡 **启发**：可借鉴 k* 的“最小充分特征数”思路，设计一个“workflow 知识最小探针集”指标：用少量探针方向即可解码模型内部的工作流步骤/依赖信息，以此量化迁移前后的知识保持率；并借用其“head 失效 vs 线性可读性”双重检查来区分 QGIS 微调后的能力覆盖与遗忘。

⚠️ **局限**：纯表示层探针研究，任务均为通用核心任务而非工作流/工具使用，k* 依赖固定搜索协议与探针设定，跨任务可比性和因果解释力有限。

### ⭐⭐⭐ FALCA: Falsification-Aware Reinforcement Learning with Hierarchical Credit Assignment for Long-Horizon Deep Search Agents
**6/10** · Zijun Dou、Kun Luo、Chaofan Li、Shuqi Lu、Zheng Liu · arXiv (Cornell University) · 2026-10-20 · [原文](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2610.28361)
*入选：RL×LLM方法*

> 📄 **摘要**：Long-horizon deep search requires LLM agents to iteratively decompose complex information needs, gather evidence from external sources, and maintain a reliable research state under multiple constraints. A central challenge is that early mistakes can be amplified over the search trajectory: agents may anchor on an incorrect candidate, collect partially supporting evidence, overlook contradictions, and accumulate errors that misguide subsequent reasoning. We propose FALCA, a falsification-aware framework that formulates deep search as a propose-falsify process. FALCA adopts a collaborative Searcher-Falsifier architecture, where the Searcher proposes candidate answers or sub-answers, while independent Falsifiers actively seek counterevidence, check unmet constraints, identify conflicts, and produce concise falsification reports for research-state revision. To strengthen this loop, we introduce falsification-aware training, combining SFT warmup on falsification trajectories with agentic RL based on parallel rollouts and hierarchical credit assignment over research stages, state transitions, and actions. Experiments on five search benchmarks show that FALCA improves long-horizon deep search performance over backbone and harness ablations, while reducing confirmation bias and error accumulation through more effective falsification and research-state revision.

**一句话**：提出 FALCA——一个"提议-证伪"式长程深度搜索智能体框架，用 Searcher-Falsifier 协作架构配合 SFT 预热与分层信用分配 Agentic RL 来抑制早期错误累积。

痛点：长程深度搜索中智能体易锚定错误候选、只收集部分支持证据、忽略矛盾，导致轨迹上的误差放大。方法：将搜索重构为 propose-falsify 循环，独立 Falsifier 主动寻找反证、检查未满足约束并生成证伪报告以修正研究状态，同时以证伪轨迹 SFT 预热加并行 rollout 的 agentic RL（在research stage、状态转移、动作三层做分层信用分配）强化该循环。结果：五个搜索基准上优于骨干与 harness 消融，确认偏差与错误累积下降。

> 🔗 **与课题**：主要支撑环节⑥（训练/后训练）：给出"SFT 预热 + agentic RL + 分层信用分配"的可复用训练配方，其"stage / state-transition / action"三级信用分配天然对应工作流中的阶段—步骤—算子粒度，可用于给 Workflow Reasoning 的中间步骤打 credit；弱相关支撑环节①（工作流推理/任务分解）：以"迭代分解复杂信息需求 + 维护研究状态"体现长程任务分解与状态跟踪，但面向信息检索而非结构化流程规划；弱相关支撑环节⑦（评测）：用确认偏差、错误累积等指标衡量长程轨迹质量，与"工作流推理质量、迁移保持率"的评测思路同构。不含 tool/operator grounding（④⑤）与跨域/跨平台能力迁移（③）内容。

💡 **启发**：可借用其"Falsifier/证伪报告检查未满足约束"的机制，改造为 QGIS 图生成的依赖闭合校验器（未被满足的约束即缺失 link 或断链），并把 stage/state/action 三级信用分配迁移到 workflow 步骤级 credit，用于训练或筛选工作流推理轨迹。

⚠️ **局限**：域限定在深度搜索/信息检索，输出是答案与研究状态而非可执行图结构，且无工具落地与跨平台迁移实验；分层 RL 训练成本高、可复现性依赖 harness 细节。
