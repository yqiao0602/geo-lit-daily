It turns a natural-language business instruction into an executable DAG of subtasks, routes each subtask to the employee best able to handle it, and adapts its plan online whenever a subtask fails. --- ## Highlights * Task decomposition: LLaMA-2-13B-chat generates the ordered subtask list and its precedence structure. ### ⭐⭐⭐ ALF-Surg: Atomic language-guided few-shot surgical phase recognition
**6/10** · Houlong He、Lin Mao、Chengli Song · Progress in Medical Devices · 2026-09-30 · [原文](https://doi.org/10.61189/436470jdqdrx)
*入选：能力迁移*

> 📄 **摘要**：Objective: To enhance the generalization and transferability of visual-language models (VLMs) pre-trained with extensive clinical data for surgical phase recognition (SPR) and workflow analysis tasks, this paper proposes Atomic Language-Guided Few-Shot Surgical Phase Recognition (ALF-Surg), a lightweight framework designed for efficient adaptation of VLMs to diverse procedures and clinical sites under few-shot settings. Methods: We leverage the rich medical knowledge contained in large language models to transform high-level annotations into vision-based atomic action descriptions, thereby injecting rich spatiotemporal cues and instrument-tissue interaction semantics. A fine-grained multimodal fusion module is applied to jointly encode vision–language features, forming stable and discriminative atomic-level and class-level prototypes. Furthermore, we propose a multi-level multimodal matching strategy, which performs video-video and video-text alignment at both atomic and phase levels to ensure robust decision-making. Results: ALF-Surg was extensively evaluated on three multi-institutional and multi-procedure datasets, including Cholec80, BernBypass70, and StrasBypass70. Compared with zero-shot transfer, ALF-Surg achieves significant accuracy improvements using only eight labeled frames per class (1-shot gains of +13.65%, +30.00%, and +35.03%, respectively). Compared with state-of-the-art few-shot baselines, ALF-Surg consistently establishes new performance records across these datasets. Conclusion: Experimental results demonstrate that ALF-Surg achieves robust SPR with minimal supervision across diverse surgical settings.

**一句话**：提出 ALF-Surg 轻量框架，用 LLM 把高层标注转成原子动作描述，并以原子级/类别级多模态原型匹配实现少样本、跨机构跨术式的手术阶段识别迁移。

痛点是视觉-语言模型在大规模临床数据上预训练后，面对新术式、新临床站点时泛化与迁移能力不足，且标注昂贵。方法上用大语言模型将高层标注分解为视觉原子动作描述，注入时空与器械-组织交互语义，再用细粒度多模态融合构建稳定的原子级/类别级原型，并通过原子与阶段两个层级的 video-video、video-text 多级匹配做决策。结果在 Cholec80、BernBypass70、StrasBypass70 三个多机构多术式数据集上，仅用每类 8 帧标注即比零样本迁移提升 13.65%/30.00%/35.03% 准确率，并刷新少样本基线。

> 🔗 **与课题**：支撑环节③（能力迁移）：这是一项在基础模型（VLM）上做源域→目标域迁移的实证研究，且迁移目标是多个异构目标域（多家医院、多种术式），与课题关心的 transfer retention 度量和跨域适配机制直接对应；同时它的'LLM 将高层标注分解为原子动作描述'可类比环节①的任务分解/流程细化思路。支撑环节⑥（训练/后训练）：其少样本原型适配属于轻量适配/PEFT 类手段，可作为迁移保持率的对照实现。但与核心问题的距离在于：它迁移的是视觉感知表征而非工作流推理知识，也不涉及 tool/operator grounding 或结构化图生成（②④⑤）。

💡 **启发**：可借用两点：一是用 LLM 把粗粒度高层标注自动降解为细粒度'原子动作/原子步骤'描述作为迁移监督，这与把 GIS Task 拆成可执行 workflow 步骤的思路同构；二是'原子级+类别级双层原型匹配'和跨多个目标域分别报告提升幅度的实验设计，可直接迁移为 QGIS grounding 的迁移保持率评测协议。

⚠️ **局限**：迁移发生在视觉-语言感知层且强依赖手术视频的器械-组织交互语义，未涉及推理/规划、工具调用或结构化依赖图，跨域结论能否外推到 GIS 工作流知识迁移需谨慎。
