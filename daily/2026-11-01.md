### ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Locating and Closing Information Gaps in Multi-Agent Systems
**7/10** · Yaxin Gong、Chongming Gao、Gangyi Zhang、Chenxiao Fan、Leyang Shen、Jiakai Wang · arXiv (Cornell University) · 2026-10-29 · [原文](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2610.40186)
*入选：工作流×工具推理*

> 📄 **摘要**：Multi-step Web tasks often require several LLM agents to gather and combine evidence. The communication graph of a fixed workflow restricts which agents can read each other's outputs: evidence retrieved by one agent may never reach the agent that needs it, leaving an information gap and an incomplete or incorrect answer. Adding a connection can deliver the missing evidence, but can also lead the receiver to adopt an incorrect upstream conclusion that displaces a correct answer; the final output alone does not separate the two. We propose Selective Probing and Link Insertion for Closing information gaps in multi-agent Execution (SPLICE), which locates information gaps per query and decides what to adopt after adding an edge. After the original workflow runs, SPLICE uses the query and node outputs to locate a possible gap, adds one directed edge from a source to a receiver to deliver the relevant evidence, and re-executes the receiver and affected downstream nodes, producing a candidate answer. The adoption decision reads both the final answers of the two runs and the process changes the edit caused inside the workflow: how far the receiver moved, where its new content came from, and whether the change reached the final answer. A selector trained on these process features chooses between adopting the candidate answer and retaining the original. On five benchmarks, SPLICE raises the task score on Web tool use by 8.0% and the mean score on four reasoning and planning benchmarks by 5.7% relative to the static skeleton, at 1.25 to 1.44$\times$ the skeleton's cost, and transfers zero-shot to four unseen backbones.

**一句话**：SPLICE 在固定多智能体工作流执行后定位“信息缺口”，插入一条有向边并重跑受影响的下游节点，再用过程特征训练选择器决定是否采纳新答案。

痛点：多步 Web 任务中，多个 LLM agent 组成固定通信图；证据可能永远无法到达需要它的节点，导致信息缺口与错误答案，而仅看最终答案无法区分“补上证据后变好”和“被错误上游结论带偏”。方法：SPLICE 用 query 与各节点输出定位缺口，插入 source→receiver 的有向边，重执行该节点及其受影响下游得到候选答案，再用 receiver 偏移距离、新内容来源、改动是否传导到最终输出等过程特征训练选择器，决定采纳候选还是保留原答案。结果：五个基准上 Web 工具使用任务分数提升 8.0%，四个推理规划基准平均分也提升。

> 🔗 **与课题**：支撑环节②和⑦，并部分触及⑤。②Workflow→结构化表示：论文把工作流看成有向通信图（即依赖图），其核心操作是“定位缺失依赖 + 插入链接 + 沿依赖重执行下游”，这与我们关心的依赖闭合、links 图结构是否信息可达的问题同构；⑦评测：它不只看最终答案，还用 receiver 移动幅度、内容来源、是否传导到最终输出等过程特征做正确性判定，可借鉴为工作流推理质量或结构正确性的过程级指标；⑤结构化图生成：边插入+下游重执行相当于一次图结构编辑后的依赖一致性与传播验证，但对象是多 agent 通信图而非 QGIS processes+links，所以只在方法层面对应；①工作流推理：per-query 缺口定位可视为对工作流规划的诊断式修正，但不是任务分解本身。总体属于支撑性通用方法，不涉及 tool/operator grounding 或跨平台能力迁移，故不给 8 分。

💡 **启发**：可直接借鉴“过程特征+选择器”的思路：在 QGIS 图生成后，插入或删除一条 link 并沿依赖重执行下游，用节点偏移、内容来源和是否传导到最终产物来判断该结构修改是否真的修复了依赖缺口，而不是只看最终执行结果。

⚠️ **局限**：面向多 agent 通信图与 Web 任务，节点是 LLM agent 而非可执行算子，插入边后“重执行”的成本与语义与 QGIS process 图的算子落地差异较大。

### ⭐⭐⭐⭐ TaskFrame: Connecting Task-Level Planning with Frame-Level Control for Game Agents
**7/10** · Guangzhi Wang、Junfu Pu、Yiran Wang、Xingyilang Yin、Honghao Chen、Cheng Cheng · arXiv (Cornell University) · 2026-10-29 · [原文](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2610.40276)
*入选：RL×LLM方法*

> 📄 **摘要**：Building general game agents requires both high-level task reasoning and low-latency action control in rapidly changing visual environments. These capabilities favor models with substantially different computational profiles, making them difficult to realize within a single model. In this work, we present TaskFrame, a hierarchical planner-controller framework that connects task-level planning with frame-level keyboard-and-mouse control. A vision-language task planner decomposes a high-level goal into an ordered sequence of atomic instructions and is optimized through supervised learning and dependency-aware GRPO. The hidden states of special goal tokens that terminate each atomic instruction provide continuous goal embeddings that are cached and reused across control steps. A causal Transformer controller combines the embedding of the active goal with recent observations to predict discrete keyboard and mouse-button actions and continuous camera motion at every game step. We train the system in three stages: planner training, controller pretraining, and joint alignment. Experiments in Minecraft show that TaskFrame is competitive with general-purpose vision-language models on instruction decomposition, and that in closed-loop evaluation the agent achieves a higher combat success rate than the evaluated baselines.

**一句话**：TaskFrame 提出分层规划器-控制器框架：一个视觉语言任务规划器把高层目标分解为有序的原子指令序列，再把该序列中目标 token 的隐藏状态作为目标嵌入传给因果 Transformer 控制器，让控制器输出键鼠动作，从而在 Minecraft 中实现从任务级规划到帧级操控的闭环。

痛点：通用游戏智能体既需要高层任务推理，又需要低延迟的帧级动作控制，而这两种能力所需模型的计算特性差异很大，难以在单个模型中同时实现。方法：用 VLM 规划器做指令分解，并采用 SFT 与 dependency-aware GRPO 优化；用终止每条原子指令的特殊 goal token 隐藏状态作为可缓存复用的连续目标嵌入；再由因果 Transformer 控制器结合该嵌入与近期观测，预测离散键鼠动作和连续相机运动，并分三阶段训练：规划器训练、控制器预训练、联合对齐。结果：在 Minecraft 中，指令分解能力与通用 VLM 相当，闭环评测的战斗成功率高于对比基线。

> 🔗 **与课题**：支撑环节①与⑥、⑦。①方面：论文的核心之一就是把高层 goal 分解成有序原子指令序列，即任务分解与步骤规划，属于工作流推理的通用形态；其 dependency-aware GRPO 显示作者显式建模了步骤之间的依赖关系，可对应环节②中的依赖组织（但输出仍是线性序列而非图）。⑥方面：SFT + 依赖感知 GRPO 的三阶段训练（规划器训练、控制器预训练、联合对齐）是绑定 workflow/planning 场景的偏好与强化学习用法，按判定框架算作高相关训练手段。⑦方面：闭环评测用执行成功率（combat success）衡量规划落地效果，可作为执行成功率的评测参考。④⑤方面：goal embedding 作为规划与执行之间的接口，可类比为 workflow 步骤到 operator 的落地接口，但论文未涉及算子选择或图结构生成。综合看，这篇论文属于支撑环节的通用方法档，而非直接研究 GIS 工作流推理或 tool grounding。

💡 **启发**：可借鉴两点：(1) 用“终止每条原子指令的特殊 token 隐藏状态”作为可缓存复用的连续目标嵌入，把工作流步骤以隐式方式传递给下游执行器；这提供了一个“不做显式 L3 语义中间层也能传递步骤语义”的实证机制，正好呼应本课题所关心的“不依赖显式语义中间层”。(2) 用 dependency-aware GRPO 奖励步骤顺序的正确性，可迁移为 QGIS 图生成中依赖闭合性的 reward 设计。

⚠️ **局限**：输出是线性有序指令序列而非 DAG，任务域是游戏键鼠控制，无工具/API grounding 与跨平台迁移实验，迁移保持率无从测量。

### ⭐⭐⭐ From Text to Simulation: Building EPANET Models from Formalized Network Descriptions with a Local Language Model
**6/10** · Michał Gorawski · Water · 2026-10-30 · [原文](https://doi.org/10.3390/w18212721)
*入选：领域×任务*

> 📄 **摘要**：Hydraulic models of water distribution networks are the basis of a digital twin, but they are still built mostly by hand. HydraKG is a software pipeline that constructs an EPANET model from a network description and checks the result against that description. In the tabular path, a deterministic parser reads GIS-style attribute tables. In the verbal path, a local large language model (LLM) reads a formalized natural-language description. The network is then checked by validators, by the EPANET solver and by a comparison with the description, none of which uses the source model; code repairs every fact with a single correct value, and the LLM corrects the rest in up to three rounds before the final model is simulated. On seven networks with 11 to 785 nodes, the tabular path reproduces the junction pressures of the source models, as represented internally, with a root-mean-square error (RMSE) of at most 2.1×10−4 m, which is the rounding limit. On canonical descriptions, the verbal path gave a model equal in value to the description in all 52 runs on six networks, including a real distribution zone. On the larger networks, the first extraction omitted groups of elements in every run, and correction rounds restored them.

**一句话**：提出 HydraKG 流水线：用本地 LLM 将形式化自然语言网络描述转换为 EPANET 水力模型，并通过验证器、求解器比对与多轮修复确保模型与描述一致。

管网水力模型仍主要靠手工构建，痛点在于从描述到可仿真模型的结构化落地容易漏元素、错属性。HydraKG 设两条路径：确定性解析器读 GIS 风格属性表，本地 LLM 读形式化自然语言描述；随后由验证器、EPANET 求解器以及与描述的比对做无源模型校验，单值事实由代码修复、其余由 LLM 最多三轮纠正。在 6 个网络的 52 次运行中，语言路径的输出与描述完全一致；大网络上首轮往往整组漏掉元素，均被修复轮补回。

> 🔗 **与课题**：支撑环节④、⑤、⑦（部分）。④ 算子落地：把自然语言映射到 EPANET 的具体元素类型与属性，属于 NL→领域算子的 grounding；⑤ 结构化生成：输出由节点、管段、连接构成的依赖闭合图，与目标 processes+links 图同构；⑦ 评测：以求解器执行与描述一致性作为无源真值，并统计首轮遗漏率与修复恢复率，是可借用的错误分析协议。方法层面“确定性代码修单值事实 + LLM 修其余 + 有限轮（≤3）修复”是 LLM 结构化输出的通用修复范式，可直接迁移到 QGIS 图的依赖闭合与幻觉算子修复。但全文不涉及工作流推理（①）、任务分解与步骤规划，也不做跨域/跨平台能力迁移（③），且不训练、不微调模型，故达不到 8 分。

⚠️ **局限**：单领域的工程流水线，样本仅 6–7 个网络、无训练与迁移实验，不能作为 LLM grounding 通用能力的证据。

### ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Clearing the FoG: Training KG Agents to Retrieve the Evidence Behind Their Answers
**7/10** · Zongyuan Sun、Shuai Zhao、Yingjun Du、Ke Shu、Ziyue Zhu、Haoran Luo · arXiv (Cornell University) · 2026-10-27 · [原文](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2610.37405)
*入选：能力迁移*

> 📄 **摘要**：Large language models (LLMs) can answer many factual questions correctly, but a correct answer is not necessarily a supported one. For an LLM agent, a knowledge graph (KG) plays two roles: as a source of answers, it supplies facts that the model lacks; as a source of evidence, it supplies the supporting triples that make an answer traceable and auditable. However, existing KG agents treat the KG only as a source of answers: their training data carries answers but not evidence, and their training rewards check the answer but not the retrieved evidence, so agents often answer correctly without retrieving the supporting triples. We propose FoG, a framework that trains KG agents to retrieve the evidence behind their answers. FoG consists of evidence-grounded synthesis, a KG interaction agent, and an evidence-coverage process reward (ECPR): the synthesis recursively hides explicit entities behind verifiable sub-questions, so that every question carries its supporting triples by construction; the agent records the triples returned by the KG; and ECPR credits each supporting triple to the turn that first retrieves it. Experiments on three KGQA benchmarks demonstrate that FoG enables 3B--4B open-source agents to outperform substantially larger baselines in answer accuracy and evidence coverage, with zero-shot cross-KG transfer.

**一句话**：提出 FoG 框架，用“证据锚定式合成 + KG 交互智能体 + 证据覆盖过程奖励（ECPR）”训练 KG 智能体在回答的同时检索出支撑答案的三元组证据。

痛点：现有 KG 智能体只把 KG 当答案来源而非证据来源，训练数据与奖励都只检查最终答案、不检查检索到的支撑证据，导致“答对但不可溯源”。方法：递归把显式实体隐藏在可验证子问题背后，使每个问题天然携带支撑三元组；智能体逐轮记录 KG 返回的三元组；ECPR 把每条支撑三元组归功于首次检索到它的那一轮，作为过程奖励。结果：3B–4B 开源智能体在三个 KGQA 基准上答案准确率与证据覆盖率均超过更大基线，并具备零样本跨 KG 迁移能力。

> 🔗 **与课题**：支撑环节①③⑥⑦但非核心。①：把复杂问题递归拆成可验证子问题，属于任务分解/多步流程规划的训练化形态，可类比 GIS Task→Workflow 的分解；⑥：ECPR 是针对中间步骤（首次检索到某条证据的轮次）的过程奖励，是“中间过程可验证”的训练范式，可直接迁移为“每个 workflow 步骤/算子落地是否正确”的过程奖励；⑦：证据覆盖率（evidence coverage）是一个可量化的中间过程评测指标，与课题的迁移保持率、QGIS 落地可用率、幻觉算子率在形式上同构；③：零样本跨 KG 迁移属于同任务跨知识源迁移，但只是源域→目标域的弱迁移，不涉及 tool→tool 或 code→tool 的能力迁移，因此达不到 8 分档。

💡 **启发**：可借鉴“递归隐藏显式实体、构造带可验证子问题链”的数据合成思路，反向用于生成 GIS 工作流训练数据：把任务目标隐藏、只给可验证的子步骤/依赖约束，使每条样本天然携带步骤级监督信号；ECPR 的“把中间证据归功于首次检索它的轮次”可改造为“把每个 QGIS 算子归功于首次 grounding 正确的步骤”，用作过程奖励与幻觉算子检测。

⚠️ **局限**：证据是 KG 三元组这种天然结构化、可校验的对象，其过程奖励可自动判定；而 QGIS 算子的正确性与依赖闭合需要执行或模拟器验证，成本更高，方法迁移并非零成本。
