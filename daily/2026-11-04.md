*入选：RL×LLM方法*

> 📄 **摘要**：GUI web agents require realistic and interactive environments that remain stable during both evaluation and training. Live website evaluation suffers from changing content, access restrictions, and limited coverage of authenticated workflows, while existing synthetic environments for training often lack functional depth and visual fidelity. We propose WebHarbor, a suite of 153 executable and dockerized website environments that faithfully reconstruct the visual interfaces, content, and functional workflows of real-world web services under deterministic control. Beyond reliable evaluation, WebHarbor provides high-fidelity synthetic web environments for cost-efficient reinforcement learning (RL). We demonstrate that a Qwen 4B-level GUI agent trained with RL in WebHarbor generalizes to unseen tasks and performs competitively with leading open-source GUI web agents across four benchmarks. The code and data are publicly available at https://github.com/aiming-lab/WebHarbor.

### ▫️ Tracing the Scientific Discovery Capacity of LLMs to Their Training Data
**-/10** · Arda Uzunoglu、Mahler Revsine、Michael Schatz、Benjamin Van Durme、Mehrdad Farajtabar、Daniel Khashabi · arXiv · 2026-11-02 · [原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03617v1) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.03617v1)
*入选：能力迁移*

> 📄 **摘要**：Large language models (LLMs) show growing promise for scientific discovery, yet basic questions remain about what enables reliable discovery. How can we trace the scientific discovery capacity of LLMs to their training data? We study this question as one of missing probability mass and develop a theoretical account in which monofacts-the facts observed once during training-set the discovery capacity. In materials science and regulatory genomics, we directly manipulate monofact rates in training sets and find that more monofacts consistently yield more discoveries across two open-weight model families, sampling strategies, and reasoning budgets. Further experiments show the relationship cannot be explained by training-set diversity alone and can transfer across scientific domains when paired with in-domain data. Together, our results identify monofact rate in training data as a key factor governing discoveries in language models.

### ▫️ FedReady: Bounded Agents for Federation Formation
**-/10** · Ziyue Xu、Holger R. Roth、Chester Chen、Peter Cnudde · arXiv · 2026-11-02 · [原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03586v1) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.03586v1)
*入选：领域×任务*

> 📄 **摘要**：Federated learning (FL) runtimes standardize aggregation and deployment, but each new study still requires federation formation: selecting task-eligible sites and preparing their local data and task code for federated training or analysis. This preparation recurs when the target changes, while raw records and site-private mappings are excluded from cross-site FL messages. We present FedReady, a system that performs federation formation with bounded LLM agents. Agents propose shared contracts, local adapters, and task code; the runtime owns workflow state and accepts artifacts only after checks tied to their digests. Candidate site discovery prioritizes recall; local eligibility verification checks task support before site admission. Once participating sites and artifacts are fixed, conventional federated training or analysis proceeds without readiness agents. Across 39 simulated retinal sites and four tasks, discovery reaches recall 1.00 at precision 0.65; local eligibility verification removes all 17 false positives without losing an eligible site-task pair. The participating sites supply 45,575 task-record instances and complete four 100-round jobs, including one with a runtime-generated contract. Across ten patient cohorts, preparation supports seven patient populations and holds endpoints at three sources as unsupported. Comparison with manually prepared reference populations and analysis (the manual reference) gives a maximum survival-curve difference of 0.001133. We also analyze 19 development failures, targeted classification ablations, and standalone and heterogeneous agent configurations. These results establish the feasibility of task-specific data preparation before ordinary FL training or analysis.

### ▫️ PlantVLM-Edge: Quantized Vision-Language Models for On-Device Crop Health Diagnosis
**-/10** · Gaurab Wagle、Bishal Adhikari、Jiajia Li、Xiaolei Huang、Dong Chen · arXiv · 2026-11-02 · [原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03557v1) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.03557v1)
*入选：能力迁移*

> 📄 **摘要**：Timely and accurate crop health diagnosis is essential for sustainable agricultural production, yet accurate diagnosis often requires expert knowledge that is not readily available. Recent vision-language models (VLMs) have demonstrated remarkable capabilities in visual understanding, reasoning, and natural-language interaction, creating new opportunities for intelligent crop health assessment. However, deploying VLMs on resource-constrained devices remains challenging due to their substantial computational and memory requirements. This paper presents PlantVLM-Edge, a quantized vision-language framework for conversational crop health diagnosis that can run entirely on resource-constrained edge devices. PlantVLM-Edge combines agricultural domain adaptation, post-training quantization, and on-device serving with an offline chat interface, allowing growers to ask open-ended questions and receive diagnoses, symptom explanations, and management guidance without cloud connectivity. The framework is backbone-agnostic: we validate it with two different VLM types with varying scale and architecture (Qwen3-VL-2B and LLaVA-1.5-7B), four quantization schemes, and two diagnostic domains (plant diseases and insect pests), and we deploy all variants on an NVIDIA Jetson AGX Orin. These results demonstrate that timely and accurate crop health diagnosis is practical on edge devices, providing a foundation for next-generation crop monitoring and decision support systems.

### ▫️ LLMs Depend on Specialist Tools for Out-of-Distribution Generalization in MS/MS Structural Elucidation
**-/10** · Gabriel Asher、Cailum M. K. Stienstra、Tomo Oga、Devesh Shah Antonio H. de O. Fonseca、Niall O'Connor、Michael Widrich · arXiv · 2026-11-02 · [原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03308v1) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.03308v1)
*入选：能力迁移*

> 📄 **摘要**：A frontier language model was recently reported to solve 1D NMR structural elucidation without tools. We ask whether this capability transfers to tandem mass spectrometry (MS/MS) in the de novo setting, where the model must propose a structure directly from the spectrum without a candidate list or reference library. To separate reasoning from recall, we build a 100-analyte benchmark from MassSpecGym, split by each analyte's textual footprint into in-distribution (well documented in literature) and out-of-distribution (essentially undocumented) arms. We hold the model, harness, and output format fixed and vary only the LLM's access to domain-specific tools. These results suggest that LLMs are not effective at de novo MS/MS structural elucidation on their own, and that further gains depend on the specialist tools available to the agent rather than on the LLM itself.

### ▫️ An Axiomatic Framework for Reinforcement Learning in Open Multi-Agent Systems
**-/10** · Jianhong Wang、Yang Li、Samuel Kaski、Jonathan Lawry · arXiv · 2026-11-02 · [原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03301v1) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.03301v1)
*入选：RL×LLM方法*

> 📄 **摘要**：Open multi-agent systems, where only a subset of agents are controllable, are central to real-world domains such as smart grids, yet lack a principled foundation for reinforcement learning algorithm design. We study this setting through n-agent ad hoc teamwork (NAHT), a recent framework for learning in open multi-agent environments. We propose an axiomatic framework that derives value function learning from the Shapley axioms (Efficiency, Symmetry and Linearity), treating them as structural constraints rather than heuristic design choices. This formulation builds on a novel NAHT decomposition, which characterises the fundamental difference between open and fully controllable multi-agent systems, and establishes a cooperative game-theoretic foundation for learning. We further show that enforcing these axioms on learning individual value functions recovers the Shapley value for Dec-POMDPs, providing a principled bridge between reinforcement learning and classical cooperative game theory. Building on this framework, we introduce Shapley Machines and Banzhaf Machines, two generic algorithmic templates that can be instantiated with standard reinforcement learning methods. When applied to strong base algorithms such as IPPO and POAM, our methods consistently improve learning efficiency and generalisation to unseen agent types and conventions across NAHT benchmark tasks. The code of experiments is published on https://github.com/hsvgbkhgbv/axiomatic-rl-naht.

### ▫️ SQLoom: Set-Contrastive Learning of Syntactic, Semantic, and Logical Dependencies for Text-to-SQL

*入选：RL×LLM方法*

> 📄 **摘要**：Text-to-SQL translates natural language questions into SQL queries, enabling database access without writing SQL. Fine-tuning open-weight large language models (LLMs) is cost-effective, but existing methods treat SQL as ordinary text and optimize token-level likelihood without explicitly modeling its syntactic, semantic, and logical requirements. Thus, a single incorrect alias or comparison operator can invalidate a query while contributing little to training loss. We propose SQLoom, a constraint-aware fine-tuning framework with supervision at three levels: syntactic validity under SQL grammar and database schema, semantic alignment between query decisions and the question, and logical consistency across dependent clause decisions. SQLoom extends supervised fine-tuning with a unified set-contrastive objective favoring sets of correct outputs over minimally different incorrect ones at each level. LLM agents propose this supervision, which a SQL parser and execution on counterexample databases verify. We also release Spider-Refined, a training dataset correcting 378 flawed Spider annotations and enriching database schemas. Experiments on Spider, BIRD, and three perturbed Spider variants show that SQLoom improves execution accuracy for Qwen3-1.7B from 62.9% zero-shot to 88.7% on Spider development, reaching 89.2% on Spider test and 73.4% on BIRD development. It outperforms fine-tuning, reinforcement learning, and agent-based baselines with up to 70 billion parameters. On question perturbations, SQLoom loses at most 0.6 accuracy points versus up to 12.1 for agent-based methods. It generates SQL in a single pass at 0.42s per query, 23$\times$ faster than a GPT-4-based agent method. These results demonstrate the effectiveness of set-contrastive supervision over token-level training.

> 📄 **摘要**：We introduce Axion 1.0, a fully open agent foundation model that combines strong and broad agentic capabilities with a remarkably simple training recipe. *入选：RL×LLM方法*

> 📄 **摘要**：Large language agents for literary role-playing are typically modeled around dialogue generation, leaving characters' non-dialogue behaviors and their dynamic evolution over story time underrepresented. In this paper, we propose ACTOR (Action-Centered Temporal Organization for Literary Role-Playing Agents), an automated framework for constructing such behavior trajectories from raw novel texts. ACTOR extracts dialogue and persona-relevant non-dialogue behaviors, represents each behavior using a Thought-Action-Observation structure, and organizes behavior trajectories according to the internal temporal order of the story. We build a behavior dataset from 143 diverse literary works. For each source behavior, we generate counterfactual behaviors that are contextually plausible yet inconsistent with the character's persona. We find that existing models struggle to reliably infer character-consistent actions from historical behaviors and evolving character states. Across 17,560 evaluation instances, the model trained on ACTOR's preference pairs achieves substantially improved accuracy. This result demonstrates that structured, temporally organized supervision enables models to leverage character profiles and prior behaviors to infer subsequent actions, greatly advancing literary role-playing beyond mere dialogue imitation. ### ▫️ Controlling Sharpening in LLM Post-Training
**-/10** · Simon Matrenok、Nicolas Menet、Nicolas Flammarion、Andreas Krause · arXiv · 2026-11-02 · [原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03146v1) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.03146v1)
*入选：RL×LLM方法*

> 📄 **摘要**：LLM post-training via reinforcement learning has shown consistent improvements in accuracy at the cost of reducing behavioral coverage - a phenomenon known as sharpening that can stall progress on difficult tasks. Repeated sampling objectives such as $\mathrm{pass@k}$ and $\mathrm{max@k}$ are known to mitigate sharpening, but the mechanism behind it remains poorly understood. Moreover, existing approaches for $\mathrm{@k}$ optimization use dedicated algorithms, and no general framework exists for adapting established policy optimization methods from $\mathrm{@}1$ to $\mathrm{@k}$. We derive the optimal policies for KL-regularized $\mathrm{pass@k}$ and $\mathrm{max@k}$ objectives in closed form. Our explicit solutions reveal that $\mathrm{@k}$ optimization naturally redistributes learning pressure based on prompt difficulty, directing it toward harder problems. Crucially, we show that standard $\mathrm{@}1$ objectives achieve these exact same optimal policies through prompt-adaptive regularization or reward transformation. Ultimately, this yields an optimizer-independent explanation for why $\mathrm{@k}$ objectives reduce sharpening on easy tasks while prioritizing success on harder ones. Building on these insights, we introduce $\mathrm{@k}$ Adaptive Pressure for Policy Optimization (KAPPO). Following a one-time reference calibration, KAPPO seamlessly adapts existing $\mathrm{@}1$ methods to $\mathrm{@k}$ via prompt-adaptive regularization or reward transformation, without otherwise altering their training procedures. Experiments with several RL methods on mathematical reasoning and code generation demonstrate that KAPPO provides direct control over sharpening. Crucially, we demonstrate that $\mathrm{@k}$ pressure can be fully realized with single completion training, matching the performance of multi-completion $\mathrm{@k}$ optimizers at a fraction of the cost.

### ▫️ PACERec: Progressive Adaptation of LLM Item Embeddings with Collaborative Evidence for Recommendation

> 📄 **摘要**：Large language model (LLM) embeddings have been increasingly adopted in sequential recommendation to encode static item attributes and alleviate the limitations of item ID embeddings under sparse interactions. However, these embeddings typically capture only static content similarity while neglecting sequential transitions, and since their adaptation quality relies on sparse per-item updates or global geometric calibration, existing methods still struggle to explicitly transfer preceding interaction contexts from frequent to long-tail items under unevenly distributed behavioral evidence, limiting recommendation effectiveness across the catalog. ### ▫️ Bayesian Sequential Optimal Experimental Design for Single-Pixel Imaging using Reinforcement Learning
**-/10** · Marcos Antonio Obando、Tianyuan Wang、Tanja Tarvainen、Tristan van Leeuwen、Felix Lucka · arXiv · 2026-11-02 · [原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03042v1) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.03042v1)
*入选：RL×LLM方法*

> 📄 **摘要**：Adaptive compressed sensing (CS) aims to select the most informative measurements sequentially, which is crucial for many modern imaging systems. This task can be formulated as a Bayesian sequential optimal experimental design (sOED) problem, where each new acquisition is chosen to maximally reduce uncertainty about the unknown image given prior information and subsequent data provided by acquisitions. However, in single-pixel imaging (SPI), the large design space consists of all possible binary illumination patterns. Directly optimizing the Bayesian sOED objective over this space using conventional methods is generally challenging. In this work, we reformulate sOED for SPI as a reinforcement learning (RL) problem. In RL, an agent learns a policy through repeated experimental trials to maximize a reward, here given as the expected information gain, the most complete measure of uncertainty reduction in Bayesian OED. This avoids the need to directly optimize complex information-theoretic objectives during a measurement to obtain the next design. Instead, the result is a learned policy function that directly maps the current posterior belief about the image to the next optimal binary measurement pattern, in a computationally efficient way. Using a simple yet representative SPI imaging scenario, this work evaluates the scalability of the learned policy under two different policy parameterizations. Furthermore, we investigate how performance deteriorates when only approximate posterior information is available or the measurements are impaired by noise, and propose several strategies to introduce additional design constraints motivated by biomedical applications. The numerical experiments demonstrate that the proposed Bayesian sOED-RL framework remains robust and can be extended to more complex, large-scale SPI scenarios.
