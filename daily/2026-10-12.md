### 🔥 ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Automated Construction of Geospatial Analysis Workflows: Recursive Solving of Elementary Modeling Questions Adaptive to Workflow Construction Contexts
**8/10** · Z. T. Chen、Cheng‐Zhi Qin、Liang‐Jun Zhu、A‐Xing Zhu · Transactions in GIS · 2026-10-10 · [原文](https://doi.org/10.1111/tgis.70423)
*入选：领域×任务*

> 📄 **摘要**：ABSTRACT Geospatial analysis workflows are widely used to address complex geographic problems, but their construction is challenging due to dynamic contextual factors like the characteristics of the study area, geo‐models, and datasets during construction process. Current automated approaches typically focus on the workflow executability, neglecting such dynamic influences—defined in this paper as “workflow construction contexts.” This ignorance undermines the rationality and accuracy of workflow modeling. To address this limitation, we propose a new automated approach adaptive to workflow construction contexts, which recursively decomposes the workflow construction process into elementary modeling questions (EQs). This allows the dynamic contexts to be characterized based on pre‐formalized modeling knowledge, enabling the automated construction of appropriate workflows. Implemented in a prototype system with a knowledge graph, our approach is validated through a digital soil mapping case study. The results demonstrate that the proposed approach can successfully construct distinct, context‐specific workflow structures that adaptively match different contexts, thus significantly enhancing the rationality and feasibility of geospatial analysis workflows.

**一句话**：提出一种自适应"工作流构建语境"的地理分析工作流自动构建方法：将构建过程递归分解为基本建模问题（Elementary Questions, EQ），基于预形式化建模知识与知识图谱原型系统实现，并在数字土壤制图案例中验证。

痛点：现有地理分析工作流自动构建方法只关注可执行性，忽略研究区特征、地理模型、数据集等动态"构建语境"，导致建模的合理性与准确性受损。方法：把工作流构建过程递归分解为基本建模问题（EQ），用预先形式化的建模知识刻画动态语境，从而自动构造适配语境的工作流，并以知识图谱原型系统实现。结果：数字土壤制图案例表明，该方法能构建出随语境变化而差异化、可适配的工作流结构，显著提升合理性与可行性。

> 🔗 **与课题**：①强相关：论文的核心就是 GIS Task→Workflow 的任务分解与流程规划，其"递归求解基本建模问题（EQ）"本质是一种显式的工作流推理分解机制，可作为我们 Workflow Reasoning 环节的符号化对照方案。②相关：论文输出的是显式工作流结构（步骤与依赖组织），其"语境→结构"映射思路对 Workflow→结构化表示（依赖/DAG 生成）有参照价值。⑦相关：论文用"合理性/可行性/语境适配度"作为评估工作流构建质量的依据，可为我们设计 workflow reasoning 质量与结构化正确性的评测指标提供借鉴。④⑤相关性较弱：论文停留在通用地理分析工作流层面，没有落到具体算子（QGIS processes/links）的 grounding 与图结构生成，也没有算子级可执行率评估。③基本不涉及：该方法是知识驱动而非模型能力驱动，不研究 GEE→QGIS 或 tool→tool 的跨域能力迁移。总体看，它是"GIS 工作流自动构建"这一核心问题上的非神经/知识图谱路线竞品与基线，可支撑我们论证 LLM 路线的必要性与评测维度，但不构成对"模型内部可迁移工作流知识"这一关键假设的支撑。

💡 **启发**：可直接借用其"递归分解为基本建模问题（EQ）"的分解粒度与层级作为 LLM 工作流推理的中间结构/提示模板；把"工作流构建语境"（研究区、模型、数据）形式化为条件输入，用于检验模型工作流推理是否具备上下文自适应能力。

⚠️ **局限**：属知识工程/知识图谱驱动的符号方法，依赖人工预形式化建模知识，未使用 LLM 或基础模型，也未涉及跨平台能力迁移与 QGIS 算子落地，因此不能作为"工作流推理能力可迁移"的证据，只能作为路线对照与评测维度参考。

### ⭐⭐⭐ DuplexAgent-RSI: Recursive Harness Improvement for Full-Duplex Voice Agent Collaboration
**6/10** · Yingda Shen、Yuxiang Wang、Kunyu Feng、Qinke Ni、Jiaqi Li、Minghao Hsu · arXiv (Cornell University) · 2026-10-08 · [原文](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2610.11299)
*入选：工作流×工具推理*

> 📄 **摘要**：Voice agents are converging on a collaboration pattern: a full-duplex interaction model stays on the live channel as the entry to the conversation, while search, reasoning, and coding are handled through asynchronous delegation. A duplex model supports continuous listening and speaking, but complex reasoning and tool use may exceed its capabilities. A coding agent can plan and execute extended tasks, but its sequential interface is a poor fit for live conversation. Combining them requires a harness that coordinates task acceptance, progress, cancellation, replacement, and result delivery while keeping the conversation responsive. Existing harnesses often rely on coupled heuristics, making them difficult to improve systematically from evidence. We present DuplexAgent, a full-duplex collaboration system whose harness expresses this workflow as six editable modules, and Duplex-Harness-RSI, a closed loop that revises them from interaction traces. A simulator automatically generates timed test conversations, runs the system, and produces failure traces that identify the collaboration modules requiring repair. Reasoning LLMs and coding agents in the delegation pool also serve the improvement loop: the Exam Planner selects the next tests from observed weaknesses and the repair archive, and the Harness Editor proposes targeted module changes. The capabilities that serve the user thus also improve the system's coordination. Experiments on intelligence, agentic, and duplex benchmarks show that DuplexAgent combines continuous interaction with difficult reasoning and complex task execution, achieving stronger spoken-knowledge and executable-tool scores than the compared delegated systems while maintaining strong interruption response. A harness ablation further shows that this modular, verifiable loop outperforms the initial harness and repeated editing that lacks its diagnosis and repair archive.

**一句话**：提出 DuplexAgent 全双工语音智能体协作系统与 Duplex-Harness-RSI 闭环，把协作流程写成六个可编辑 harness 模块，并依据交互失败轨迹自动选测、自动改模块。

痛点：全双工语音模型响应快但复杂推理与 tool use 能力不足，编码智能体擅长长任务却接口串行，两者协作需要 harness 协调任务受理、进度、取消、替换与结果交付，而现有 harness 依赖耦合启发式、难以从证据里系统改进。方法：把该协作工作流显式表达为六个可编辑模块，并用 Duplex-Harness-RSI 闭环从交互轨迹中修订模块——模拟器自动生成带时序的测试对话并产出失败轨迹定位待修复模块，Exam Planner 依据观察到的弱点与修复档案挑选下一批测试，Harness Editor 提出定向模块改动。结果：在 intelligence、agentic、duplex 基准上做了验证（摘要被截断）。

> 🔗 **与课题**：支撑环节①：harness 显式协调 task acceptance、progress、cancellation、replacement、result delivery，是任务分解与流程编排层面的工程化研究；支撑环节⑤：把协作工作流显式拆成六个可编辑模块，属于工作流的结构化表示（虽非 DAG/processes+links 形式）；支撑环节⑦：模拟器自动生成定时测试对话、产出失败轨迹来定位需修复模块，是一套可复用的自动评测与错误归因协议。部分触及⑥：从交互轨迹闭环改进系统，但改的是 harness 模块（提示/逻辑）而非模型权重，属持续改进的编排侧而非训练侧。与②③④（跨平台能力迁移、QGIS operator grounding、结构化图生成）无对应关系。

💡 **启发**：可偷“失败轨迹→定位需修复模块→Exam Planner 自动生成下一批针对性测试→Harness Editor 定向改动”的闭环评测-修复机制，把它移植到 GEE→QGIS 工作流迁移的评测与错误归因（如幻觉算子率、依赖不闭合的自动定位）。

⚠️ **局限**：改进对象是 harness 编排模块而非模型权重，且场景是实时语音对话，与离线结构化 workflow/QGIS 图生成的迁移设定差距较大，结论外推需谨慎。

### ⭐⭐⭐ Optical design in the age of AI: cloud-native simulation, LLM automation, and real-world camera rendering
**6/10** · Ori Sarfaty、Pavel Shapirov、Haim Suchowski · 预印本 · 2026-10-09 · [原文](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.3114740)
*入选：能力迁移*

> 📄 **摘要**：The emergence of Physical AI is increasing the need for scalable, physically accurate, and sensor-faithful optical simulation. This work presents an AI-native optical engineering framework that combines chat-based large language models (LLMs) for engineering reasoning and workflow orchestration with 3DOpix’s PhotonCore, a GPU-accelerated physics engine for quantitative optical simulation and analysis. The framework comprises three pillars. First, programmable PhotonCore simulation through an extensive API interface for design, tolerancing, parameter exploration, and AI-workflow integration. Second, a Chat/LLM layer that interprets natural-language objectives, constructs and modifies optical systems, invokes PhotonCore, and iterates over computed results while physical validity remains determined by the simulation engine, and third is the introduction of the Ray Translation Module that transfers precomputed optical behavior into rendering and sensor environments for scalable generation of sensor-faithful synthetic optical data. The same infrastructure couples forward simulation with inverse learning for estimating alignment, calibration, and other latent physical states from measured images. Together, these capabilities establish a foundation for a new generation of AI-native optical engineering, marking a paradigm shift in which optical simulation evolves from a standalone design tool into a shared computational infrastructure for intelligent imaging and perception systems.

**一句话**：提出一个 AI 原生的光学工程框架，用聊天式 LLM 做工程推理与工作流编排，通过 API 驱动 GPU 物理仿真引擎 PhotonCore，并引入 Ray Translation Module 把光学行为迁移到渲染/传感器环境生成合成数据。

痛点：Physical AI 需要可扩展且物理精确的光学仿真，而传统光学设计工具难以被自然语言驱动与自动化编排。方法：构建三层框架——PhotonCore 的可编程 API、解释自然语言目标并构造/修改光学系统、调用引擎、基于结果迭代的 Chat/LLM 层（物理正确性由仿真引擎兜底），以及把预计算光学行为迁移到渲染与传感器环境的 Ray Translation Module。结果：同一套基础设施把正向仿真与逆向学习（从实测图像估计对准、标定等隐变量）耦合起来，宣称光学仿真从独立设计工具演变为智能成像的共享计算基础设施。

> 🔗 **与课题**：主要支撑环节④（Workflow→Operator 落地）：LLM 把自然语言目标转成对 PhotonCore API 的调用序列，且明确'物理有效性由仿真引擎判定'，与我们要研究的 QGIS processes+links 落地中'算子是否真实存在、参数是否合法'的 grounding 约束同构。其次支撑①（任务分解/工作流推理）：Chat 层负责解释目标、构造与修改系统、调用引擎并基于计算结果迭代，是典型的'任务→工作流→执行→回读结果'闭环。与③（能力迁移）有远距离关联：Ray Translation Module 是把已算出的光学行为迁移到另一套渲染/传感器环境的跨环境复用，但其迁移对象是物理行为而非模型能力，不构成 GEE→QGIS 式的能力迁移证据。整体属于通用方法/系统范式层支撑，不是对本课题核心问题的直接实证。

💡 **启发**：可借鉴其'LLM 只负责编排与推理、正确性由领域引擎裁决'的分工设计，以及把领域引擎封装成丰富 API 再让 LLM 迭代调用-回读-修正的闭环，可直接映射为我们的 QGIS 算子 API + 校验器（processes/links 合法性由 QGIS 侧判定）。

⚠️ **局限**：偏系统/框架宣称类预印本，缺少对 LLM 编排正确率的量化评测（幻觉算子率、执行成功率、跨任务泛化），也没有与基线 prompt/人工设计的对照实验。

### ⭐⭐⭐ SAIL: Scientific Agentic Intelligence via a Science-Aware Loop
**6/10** · SAIL Model Team、Boyuan Sun、Bryan Dai、Che Liu、Chi Liu、Derek Li · arXiv (Cornell University) · 2026-10-08 · [原文](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2610.11451)
*入选：RL×LLM方法*

> 📄 **摘要**：We introduce SAIL, an open model with 35B total and 3B active parameters for literature research, scientific coding, and multi-step research workflows. SAIL is developed through a science-aware improvement loop: agents built on frontier AI models analyze its task failures and construct training tasks that address the underlying capability gaps. The diagnosis examines search and evidence selection in literature tasks, scientific assumptions and reasoning in coding, and planning and revision in longer investigations. The agents draw on paper collections and scientific code repositories to build problems, interaction trajectories, and executable tasks with the required environments and tools. We repeat this loop over multiple development cycles and train SAIL through supervised fine-tuning, specialist training, multi-teacher on-policy distillation, and agentic reinforcement learning. SAIL achieves competitive performance across scientific research tasks with substantially fewer parameters than leading open-weight models.

**一句话**：提出 SAIL（35B 总参/3B 激活的开源科学智能体模型），用“科学感知循环”——让前沿模型驱动的智能体诊断自身失败并反向构造训练任务——再经 SFT、专家训练、多教师 on-policy 蒸馏与智能体 RL 迭代提升，覆盖文献研究、科学代码与多步研究工作流。

痛点：科学智能体在文献检索与证据筛选、科学代码假设推理、长程研究的规划与修订上存在能力缺口，且缺乏针对性训练数据。方法：构建“科学感知改进循环”，由前沿模型智能体对失败做分环节诊断，并从论文库与科学代码库自动合成问题、交互轨迹与带环境/工具的可执行任务，再用 SFT、专家训练、多教师蒸馏与 agentic RL 多轮训练。结果：在科学任务上以远小于主流开源模型的参数取得有竞争力的表现。
