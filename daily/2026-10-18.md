### ⭐⭐⭐ Head Start or Process? Decomposing How an Experience-Acquired Abstraction Shapes Later Learning in a Small Language Model
**6/10** · Sam Ramdan · Zenodo (CERN European Organization for Nuclear Research) · 2026-10-17 · [原文](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23432799)
*入选：能力迁移*

> 📄 **摘要**：When a network that has acquired an abstraction A learns a second abstraction B faster, does A supply information or change the process by which B is learned? We decompose a gradient learner's function-space update into starting predictions, evidence and tangent kernel, and isolate the kernel channel with an output-matched learner: A's network with a fixed stop-gradient offset, so that it starts B with the naive model's predictions. We apply it to rank-8 adapters on Qwen3-1.7B that learned a hidden two-cue conjunction from outcome-labelled episodes. The compact layer-24 mediator of A's transfer is the answer-letter readout (one adapter tested fully, five on the training domains with fewer candidates). A's raw advantage is a head start plus a network effect that operates only from A's own saturated predictions; from matched starts, A's network gives no detectable mean speed-up. Near-inert replicates of the base learner differ by up to 0.28 in mean held-out balanced accuracy, so single-run comparisons, including our preregistered ones, are dominated by trajectory noise. Against replicated references (post hoc), the mean kernel-channel benefit is below +0.02 at the onset of learning on a task whose rule A does not share, and below +0.09 on a task that extends A, where the scaffold question stays open. Two post hoc observations suggest the method is not blind: all three shuffled-outcome adapters slowed matched-start learning (not significant at the adapter level), and one of four kernel statistics ranked the six adapters' matched-start advantage (Spearman 0.94 on both tasks, uncorrected). The preregistered positive control failed calibration, so the frozen verdict is uninterpretable. Files. head-start-or-process.pdf (the paper); head-start-or-process-source.zip (LaTeX source and the scripts that write every table and figure from the results); preregistrations.zip (the three preregistrations, the SHA-256 record, the deviation log and the frozen forecast); code-and-data.zip (code, the ten A adapters, control adapters, every cell's results, logs and analyses; MANIFEST.json gives the SHA-256 of every file). Code and learned adapters. The code, preregistrations, numeric results and paper source are also at github.com/Somebodyhere101/head-start-or-process. Every learned B adapter (about 2 GB) is attached to its release v1.0 rather than to this record, as b-adapters-part01-of-09.zip to part09, with SHA-256 sums in the release notes. Registered forecast. The F_sign forecast for the four held-out adapters is a separate record, doi:10.5281/zenodo.23432798, published before this preprint. Use of AI assistance. Substantial AI assistance was used throughout this project. The question of whether an experience-acquired abstraction changes the process by which a later abstraction is formed, rather than only supplying information, is the author's. An AI system (Claude, Anthropic), working under the author's direction, audited the earlier studies, reviewed the literature, designed the study, wrote the preregistrations and code, ran the experiments and analyses, and drafted the paper. A separate instance of the same system reviewed the draft; that review led to the post hoc analyses E3 and E4. The preregistrations record that the author delegated the design decisions to the AI system. The author directed the work and is responsible for its content.

**一句话**：该研究用输出匹配的学习器分解小型语言模型（Qwen3-1.7B rank-8 adapter）中已习得抽象A对后续学习抽象B的迁移，并区分“起点预测优势”与“网络/核通道效应”。

痛点：在迁移中，A究竟是提供信息，还是改变B的学习过程，难以区分。方法：把梯度学习器的函数空间更新分解为起点预测、证据与tangent kernel，并用带固定stop-gradient偏移的输出匹配学习器隔离kernel通道，在隐藏两线索合取任务的outcome-labeled episodes上训练与测试。结果：A的优势主要是head start，外加一个仅在A自身饱和预测上生效的网络效应；匹配起点后无可检测的平均加速，且近惰性复本间held-out平衡准确率差异高达0.28，使单次run比较（含预注册）被轨迹噪声主导。

> 🔗 **与课题**：主要支撑环节③（能力迁移，机制层）：它提供了区分“迁移是信息搬运（head start）”与“迁移改变学习过程（kernel/network 通道）”的可操作实验分解，可直接类比用于检验本课题的核心假设——GEE→QGIS 的工作流推理迁移究竟是显式 L3 语义中间层在供信息，还是模型内部工作流知识改变了目标域的学习/推理过程；其 output-matched 对照学习器思路可改造为 transfer retention 评测中的“起点信息量控制”基线；对环节⑦评测也有方法警示（重复次数、轨迹噪声）。但不涉及 tool/operator grounding、结构化 DAG 生成或 workflow 表示，属于通用迁移机制研究而非工具迁移实证，故不进 8-10 档。

💡 **启发**：可借用“输出匹配+固定偏移隔离特定通道”的对照设计，在 GEE→QGIS 迁移实验中分离“知识/信息供给”与“推理过程改变”，量化 transfer retention 中超出起点优势的部分；同时提示迁移评测必须多 seed 重复并用分布比较，而非单次对比。

⚠️ **局限**：合成抽象任务、1.7B 小模型、单一训练域，关键结论多为 post hoc 且不显著，近惰性复本噪声极大，外推到 tool/workflow 级迁移需谨慎。

### ⭐⭐ Divergence-Routed On-Policy Distillation for Language Models
**5/10** · Tiantian Peng、Guannan Lv、Hongyang Li、红秋 何、Hang Cheng、Hongjian Dou · arXiv (Cornell University) · 2026-10-14 · [原文](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2610.19572)
*入选：能力迁移*

> 📄 **摘要**：On-policy distillation (OPD) transfers knowledge from a teacher model to a student along student-generated trajectories, typically by minimizing the reverse Kullback-Leibler (RKL) divergence. However, RKL can provide weak corrective signals for teacher-supported tokens to which the student assigns very low probability. This raises two questions: which positions are most consequential for the behavioral gap between the student and teacher, and what additional supervision is needed at these positions? Through cross-sampling, we find that divergence-based routing more effectively pinpoints positions contributing to the teacher-student performance gap than entropy-based routing. Further analysis reveals teacher-mode under-coverage at high Jensen-Shannon (JS) divergence positions, where standard OPD increases the large teacher-student probability gaps. Motivated by these findings, we propose Divergence-Routed On-Policy Distillation (DrOPD). DrOPD retains RKL supervision at all positions and selectively adds forward Kullback-Leibler (FKL) divergence supervision at positions with high JS divergence. Experiments across six math benchmarks show that DrOPD improves over standard OPD and entropy-based routing. DrOPD also reduces severe under-coverage cases by 37.9 percent relative to OPD, supporting targeted FKL as an effective complement to OPD.

**一句话**：提出 DrOPD，一种按散度路由的 on-policy 蒸馏方法：在所有位置保留用 RKL（反向 KL）做监督，只在高 JS 散度位置额外加入 FKL（前向 KL）监督，以弥补教师模式覆盖不足。

痛点：标准 on-policy 蒸馏只用反向 KL，对于学生概率极低、但教师支持的 token 提供的纠正信号很弱；同时学生与教师差异最大的位置又存在教师模式覆盖不足（teacher-mode under-coverage）的问题。方法：先通过交叉采样比较，发现按散度路由比按熵路由更能定位造成师生性能差距的位置，再据此提出 DrOPD，在 JS 散度高的位置选择性补充 FKL 监督。结果：在六个数学基准上优于标准 OPD 和基于熵的路由，严重覆盖不足的情形相对 OPD 减少 37.9%。

> 🔗 **与课题**：只支撑环节⑥（训练/后训练）中的蒸馏子项，属通用方法层：它讨论在“学生自身生成的轨迹”上做师生知识传递，以及如何选择监督位置，这与本课题里用蒸馏把 GEE 工作流推理能力固化进学生模型、并尽量保持迁移保持率（transfer retention）的诉求有间接对应；但它既没有触及工作流推理、结构化 DAG 生成，也没有任何 tool、code、workflow、GIS 场景的实证，因此与③能力迁移不构成直接证据，不能计入 8-10 档。

💡 **启发**：可借鉴“按散度路由选择监督位置”的思路：在 GEE→QGIS 迁移训练中，用师生散度（JS/RKL）定位能力断裂的 token 位置（如算子名、参数、依赖连边），并在这些位置补加前向 KL 或定向 SFT 监督，而不是在全序列上平均用力。

⚠️ **局限**：实验全部局限于数学推理基准，未验证在工具调用、代码生成、结构化输出等场景下的有效性；其“高散度位置等于关键位置”的假设在长程依赖的图结构生成任务上是否成立仍未知。

### ⭐⭐⭐ RCCM: Diagnosing Gains and Harms in Reasoning-Prompt Transfer
**6/10** · Yuqi Zhang、Di Zhang · arXiv (Cornell University) · 2026-10-14 · [原文](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2610.19872)
*入选：能力迁移*

> 📄 **摘要**：A reasoning prompt can improve one deployment and degrade another. RCCM (Reasoning-Control Capability Mismatch) examines these reversals by pairing answers to the same questions with records of how generation ends. Across five open-weight deployments and nine strategies, aggregate accuracy hides substantial question-level gains and harms; a common-budget MATH500 evaluation examines transfer on a separate sample. On 60 additional questions, concise reasoning improves Phi's automatic answer matching at a 2,048-token cap across three collections, but sharply reduces Qwen's. Author-reviewed final-answer commitments give a smaller, statistically unresolved Phi gain. Reserving an answer stage also improves matching, with additional input and call costs. These results show why a prompt benefit must be interpreted alongside its execution and evaluation: matching a reference answer need not mean delivering it, and an apparent compression benefit may admit another explanation. RCCM records this evidence for reproducible transfer comparisons.

**一句话**：RCCM 提出一种诊断框架，用于考察同一推理提示在不同模型部署上带来的收益与损害反转（同一问题上有的提升、有的下降），并记录生成结束方式等执行证据，以支持可复现的迁移比较。

痛点是：聚合准确率掩盖了迁移中问题级的收益与损害，而且'匹配参考答案'不等于'真正交付答案'。方法是将同题答案与生成终止记录配对，在五个开源权重部署、九种策略以及 MATH500 固定预算下比较，另加 60 题细粒度检验。结果显示，简洁推理在 2048 token 上限下显著提升 Phi 的自动答案匹配，却大幅降低 Qwen 的表现；预留答案阶段虽有提升，但带来额外输入和调用成本。

> 🔗 **与课题**：主要支撑⑦评测：它提供了迁移场景下'问题级收益/损害诊断'与'执行证据记录'的评测协议，这正是本课题'迁移保持率（transfer retention）'所需的细粒度测量思路，而不是只看聚合成功率的做法。也部分触及③能力迁移：它研究的正是跨部署/跨模型的迁移（源域→目标域的实证对照），但对象是提示而非习得的工作流知识，因此不足以升到高相关档。关键借鉴在于它区分'表面匹配'与'实际交付'，与本课题区分'结构正确'与'依赖闭合、可执行图'的判据同构。

💡 **启发**：可借鉴'答案匹配 + 生成终止/执行记录'双轨记录法：在 GEE→QGIS 迁移评测中同时记录图结构匹配率与真实执行成功率，并逐题报告增益/损害方向，而非只报平均分。

⚠️ **局限**：研究对象是推理提示的跨模型迁移与答案匹配指标，未涉及工作流推理、工具或算子 grounding，也不含结构化图生成，迁移结论不可直接外推到 workflow 知识迁移。

### ⭐⭐ Taking the PULSE of Medical Reasoning: Step-Level Retrieval Timing for Knowledge-Grounded Inference
**5/10** · Danyang Zhao、Sendong Zhao、Yanrui Du、Haochun Wang、Ming Ma、Wenhao Li · arXiv (Cornell University) · 2026-10-14 · [原文](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2610.20292)
*入选：能力迁移*

> 📄 **摘要**：Reliable medical reasoning requires LLMs to dynamically access external biomedical evidence to improve factual trustworthiness and reasoning accuracy. However, indiscriminate retrieval can introduce noise and latency, raising a critical retrieval-timing problem: at each reasoning step, the system must decide whether the model's internal knowledge is reliable or external evidence is needed. We propose PUlSE, a lightweight probing method that uses the model's internal representations as intrinsic signals for step-level retrieval control. Our posterior trajectory analysis reveals that erroneous steps exhibit substantially higher inter-layer volatility than correct ones, suggesting unstable internal knowledge. Based on this finding, PUlSE trains a compact probe on inter-layer geometric features to detect factual errors upon step completion, and uses it as an adaptive trigger for retrieval and step regeneration. Across four medical benchmarks, PUlSE-timed retrieval matches exhaustive per-step retrieval in accuracy while reducing retrieval calls by 72%, and can transfer to an unseen legal domain, indicating potential domain-agnostic generality. Overall, our study provides an initial step toward shifting retrieval-trigger design from surface-level confidence estimates to internal activation dynamics for adaptive access.

**一句话**：提出 PULSE，用模型层间几何特征的轻量探针判断每一步推理的内部知识是否可靠，从而按步自适应触发外部检索与步骤重生成。

痛点：推理中无差别逐步检索会引入噪声与延迟，关键是如何在每一步判断该靠内部知识还是外部证据。方法：后验轨迹分析发现错误步骤的层间波动显著高于正确步骤，据此在步骤完成时用层间几何特征训练紧凑探针检测事实性错误，并作为检索/重生成的触发器。结果：在四个医学基准上达到逐步穷举检索的精度而检索调用减少 72%，并可迁移到未见过的法律领域。

> 🔗 **与课题**：主要支撑⑦评测：提供"步级"质量判定/错误检测协议（内部激活信号作探针），可借鉴为工作流中间步骤（任务分解、依赖组织）的正确性判据与幻觉算子早期拦截信号；与①弱相关（步骤级推理控制而非工作流规划本身），其"跨域迁移到法律"仅是探针泛化，不构成③能力迁移（tool→tool/跨平台）证据；未涉及④grounding、⑤结构图生成、⑥训练主轴。

💡 **启发**：可把"层间波动/内部表示不稳定性"这类免标注的内部信号，做成工作流中间步骤（尤其在生成 QGIS process+link 前）的廉价校验器，用于决定是否回退重生成或触发外部算子检索，从而降低对显式 L3 语义中间层的依赖。

⚠️ **局限**：任务域为医学/法律问答式检索增强，非工具调用或工作流图生成；探针依赖特定模型内部表征，跨模型/跨任务的可移植性未验证。

### ⭐⭐⭐ Explorer-Zero: Improving Self-Play for Long-Horizon Tasks with Decomposable Verifiable Rewards
**6/10** · Tianyu Liu、Davide Belli、Bence Major、Arash Behboodi、Tribhuvanesh Orekondy · arXiv (Cornell University) · 2026-10-14 · [原文](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2610.19868)
*入选：RL×LLM方法*

> 📄 **摘要**：Training LLM agents on long-horizon interactive tasks with reinforcement learning typically relies on human-annotated tasks and reliable verification, both expensive to construct for stateful environments. Self-play offers an alternative, but must generate achievable, compositional tasks and provide feedback beyond binary outcomes. We propose Explorer-Zero, a self-play framework that jointly generates tasks and fine-grained, verifiable rewards through environment interaction. A learned proposer explores the environment, constructs tasks, and records intermediate and final states as verification targets. These checkpoints reward partial completion, while alternating proposer--solver training adapts the generated curriculum. We show theoretically that subtask rewards reduce collapse among unsuccessful rollouts when they complete different numbers of subtasks. Without human-annotated training tasks, Explorer-Zero outperforms the evaluated self-play baselines across ALFWorld, ScienceWorld, and AppWorld; on AppWorld, our 30B agent reaches 59.67% task success, approaching 62.65% for RL on human-annotated tasks. Further analyses support task-relevant composition and benefits on complex tasks, while self-play initialization improves subsequent human-data RL across all three benchmarks.

**一句话**：提出 Explorer-Zero 自博弈框架：让一个可学习的 proposer 与环境交互，自动生成任务，并把中间与最终环境状态记录为可验证的奖励目标，通过 proposer–solver 交替训练实现长时程任务的细粒度奖励学习。

痛点：长时程交互任务的 RL 训练依赖人工标注任务和可靠验证器，二者在状态化环境中构造代价高，且二元奖励反馈过于稀疏。方法：proposer 探索环境并构造可完成、可组合的任务，将中间与最终状态作为 checkpoint 形成可分解的可验证奖励以奖励部分完成，proposer 与 solver 交替训练自适应调整任务难度课程，并从理论上说明子任务奖励能缓解不同子任务完成数的失败 rollout 之间的坍缩。结果：在无人工标注训练任务的情况下，ALFWorld/ScienceWorld/AppWorld 上优于自博弈基线，AppWorld 上 30B agent 达 59.67% 成功率（人工标注 RL 为 62.65%），且自博弈初始化可提升后续人工数据 RL。

> 🔗 **与课题**：主要支撑环节⑦（评测）与①（任务分解），次要支撑⑥（训练）。⑦：用环境中间状态当 checkpoint，把最终二值成败拆成步骤级可验证的部分得分，这正对应我们要测的“工作流推理质量/步骤级正确性/执行成功率”，是一套可直接借用的细粒度评测/奖励协议。①：proposer 构造的是“可完成的组合式子任务序列”，本质是把长时程任务自动分解为有依赖的步骤，可作为工作流任务分解的自动课程来源。⑥：自博弈课程 + proposer–solver 交替训练属于训练/后训练手段（储备池），且绑定在 tool/agent 环境（AppWorld 为 API/工具调用）而非纯算法改进，因此不算纯 RL 变体。但论文未涉及跨域/跨平台能力迁移（③）与算子落地 grounding（④），也未研究结构化图生成（②⑤），因此不进入 8-10 档。
