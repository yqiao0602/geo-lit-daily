### 🔥 ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Is Persona Generalization Predictable?
**8/10** · Olina Mukherjee、Arnav Goel、Aditi Raghunathan、Jacob Mitchell Springer · arXiv · 2026-11-09 · [原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12864v1) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.12864v1)
*入选：能力迁移*

> 📄 **摘要**：Persona generalization occurs when fine-tuning a language model in one domain induces behavioral changes in others -- for example, the broadly unsafe behavior observed in emergent misalignment. We discover a surprising "on-the-line" phenomenon in persona generalization: in-domain and out-of-domain persona rates across various fine-tuning hyperparameters follow an approximately probit-linear relationship, and the specific probit-linear relationship is surprisingly shared even across model families and model sizes, including Qwen and Llama. Training choices that leave the set of trainable parameters unchanged, such as learning rate, weight decay, and training duration, largely move models along the same line, reducing transfer mainly by weakening in-domain learning. Changing which layers are fine-tuned, however, can change the slope. For unsafe behavior, fine-tuning only later layers yields shallower slopes, retaining strong in-domain learning while reducing out-of-domain misalignment. This "on-the-line" phenomenon unifies unexpected, isolated observations of persona generalization within a quantitative framework for predicting and controlling behavioral transfer.

**一句话**：该论文发现并量化了微调引发的"人格泛化"现象：域内微调率与域外行为变化率之间呈近似 probit 线性关系，该关系跨模型家族与规模共享，且可通过选择微调层改变斜率来调控迁移。

痛点：在一个领域微调语言模型会诱使其他领域行为改变（如涌现性错位），这种跨域行为迁移此前难以预测与控制。方法：系统扫描学习率、权重衰减、训练时长等超参，以及不同层组合的微调设置，拟合域内人格率与域外人格率的定量关系。结果：超参变化只让模型沿同一条 probit 直线移动（主要通过削弱域内学习来降低迁移），而改变微调层可改变斜率——只微调后层能在保持域内学习的同时显著减少域外错位，从而把行为迁移变成可预测、可控制的量。

> 🔗 **与课题**：支撑环节③（源域→目标域能力迁移）：论文正是研究"域内微调如何迁移到域外"的实证规律，并提出该迁移可用一条共享的 probit 曲线预测，与我们把 GEE 工作流推理迁移到 QGIS grounding 的核心问题同构。支撑环节⑦（评测）：给出"域内保持率—域外迁移率"的定量映射，可直接改造为 transfer retention 指标与迁移保持率的预测基线。支撑环节⑥（训练/后训练）：揭示学习率、权重衰减、训练时长等超参只沿曲线滑动，而层选择（哪些参数可训练）才是改变迁移斜率的结构性杠杆，为 DAPT/SFT/LoRA 的层配置提供依据。注意其对象是安全/人格行为而非工作流知识，属于迁移"可预测性"的方法论支撑而非工作流推理本身的实验。

💡 **启发**：可把"域内保持率 vs 域外迁移率"的 probit 线性拟合直接搬来做 GEE→QGIS 迁移保持率分析：检验我们的 workflow reasoning 迁移是否也落在一条共享直线上，并用斜率作为"迁移效率"标量；同时借用"只微调后层保域内、抑域外漂移"的策略，在保留 GEE 工作流推理能力的同时抑制向 QGIS 的幻觉算子漂移。

⚠️ **局限**：研究对象是人格/安全类行为的黑箱泛化，指标是全量表人格率而非任务正确率，未涉及工作流、工具调用等结构化技能的可执行性迁移。

🚨 **为什么必须重视**：它正面回答了"能力迁移能否被预测与控制"这一与本课题撞车的科学问题，并给出可量化的 transfer retention 拟合协议；若我们的 GEE→QGIS 迁移实验缺少此类迁移律基线，迁移度量会被质疑缺乏理论依据。其"层选择改变迁移斜率"的结论还可直接指导我们的 PEFT 配置实验设计。

**▸ 建议：精读**

### ⭐⭐ Interpretability Is Not Model-Specific
**5/10** · Marcus Armstrong、Navid Ayoobi、Pradham Mummaleti、Alexander Chulzhanov、Arjun Mukherjee · arXiv · 2026-11-09 · [原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12675v1) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.12675v1)
*入选：能力迁移*

> 📄 **摘要**：Interpretability tools are usually built for one model at a time: a refusal direction, a truthfulness probe, or a steering vector is found with labeled data inside the model it explains. We ask whether such tools can be carried to other models through a map between residual streams fit only on unlabeled prompts. We transfer a refusal direction, tested causally by addition and ablation, and two read-out tools, a correctness direction and a sentiment probe, across 110 pairs of models from five families and 1B to 72B parameters and between base and instruction-tuned models, with controls for specificity and five seeds. How much transfers depends on what the map was fit on. A map fit on ordinary questions recovers 54\% of the target's own refusal steering and 16\% to 77\% of its correctness gain; adding unlabeled prompts from the region where the tool operates raises these to 102\% and 88\% to 98\%, while control directions recover at most 1\%. We prove that a linear map behaves this way whenever two models correspond linearly on the data it covers, and a label-free coverage score derived from this result tracks recovery across more than 16,000 runs and explains earlier reports that maps between models fail across domains. Carried read-out tools outperform anything the target's unlabeled representations yield. Interpretability results, once found, can be reused across models at the cost of a few unlabeled prompts.

**一句话**：论文利用仅在无标注提示上拟合的残差流线性映射，把拒绝方向、正确性方向、情感探针等可解释性工具从一个模型迁移到另一个模型，并在 110 对模型上做了因果验证。

痛点：可解释性工具（拒绝方向、真实性探针、steering vector）通常依赖目标模型内部的标注数据，模型更换后就不能通用。方法：只在无标注提示上拟合两个模型残差流之间的线性映射，借此把工具搬到目标模型，并通过相加和消融来因果检验；实验覆盖 5 个模型家族、1B–72B、base 与 instruct 之间共 110 对模型，并设置特异性对照和 5 个随机种子。结果：映射所用提示的覆盖范围决定迁移效果，在工具作用区域加入无标注提示后，恢复率从 54%/16–77% 提升到 102%/88–98%，对照方向至多恢复 1%；作者还给出一个无标注覆盖度分数，用来预测跨 16000+ 次运行的恢复情况。

> 🔗 **与课题**：主要支撑环节③（能力迁移），但迁移的是“可解释性读出工具或方向”，并非工作流推理能力：它给出跨模型迁移可行性的边界条件，即源域与目标域在线性对应时迁移成立；还给出一个无标注覆盖度指标，用来量化迁移保持率。这可以作为我们度量 GEE→QGIS 迁移保持率（transfer retention）时区分“表征对齐”与“能力习得”的方法论参照；对环节②⑤（结构化图生成）和①④（workflow 推理、算子 grounding）都没有直接涉及；对⑥⑦只有很弱的间接意义，即提供评估协议层面的可迁移性分数设计思路。

💡 **启发**：可以借鉴“用无标注数据拟合源域与目标域之间的映射，再按覆盖度打分”这一思路：迁移前先估计目标域（QGIS 算子空间）在源域（GEE 工作流空间）表示中的覆盖度，从而预测迁移保持率；把“加工具作用域内的无标注提示”换成“加入 QGIS 算子语料或工作流片段”，作为提升迁移的无监督手段。

⚠️ **局限**：研究对象是线性可读方向的跨模型复用，依赖两模型在线性对应这一强假设，因此不能直接外推到跨平台的技能或工作流知识迁移；并且任务目标并非 LLM 能力迁移本身。

**▸ 建议：略读**

### ⭐⭐ PACE-ECG: Patient-Aware Contrastive ECG Pretraining; Leveraging Longitudinal Structure for Transferable Representations
**5/10** · Riccardo Cavarra、Lupo Lovatelli、Shaheim Ogbomo-Harmitt、Jakub Grzelak、Adelaide De Vecchi、Andrew King · arXiv · 2026-11-09 · [原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12307v1) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.12307v1)
*入选：能力迁移*

> 📄 **摘要**：Foundation models for physiological signals are commonly evaluated on in-domain interpretation tasks, but their ability to transfer to longitudinal clinical prognosis across healthcare systems remains uncertain. We developed PACE-ECG, an ECG pretraining framework that combines contrastive learning across temporally neighbouring recordings from the same patient with clinically guided multitask supervision. We evaluated frozen ECG representations for post-myocardial-infarction all-cause mortality and incident heart failure prediction across MIMIC-IV and two external HEEDB cohorts, comparing the proposed representation with DeepECG-SSL, a state-of-the-art model, and two control baselines. Across both downstream tasks, the proposed model outperformed DeepECG-SSL in-domain (AUROC +1.9% for mortality and +4.0% for heart failure) and on external populations in HEEDB-Boston (+5.6% for mortality +10.3% for heart failure) and HEEDB-Atlanta (+6.9% for mortality +11.0% for heart failure) while using an encoder 41 times smaller and a fraction of the pretraining data. A matched ablation showed that patient-temporal pairing improved external mortality and heart failure AUROC over generic self-supervision by 7.0% and 9.4% on HEEDB-Boston and 8.9% and 9.3% on HEEDB-Atlanta. These findings indicate that pretraining-objective design greatly influences downstream transfer alongside model and data scale, and that in-domain performance alone is insufficient to establish the clinical generalisability of ECG foundation representations.

**一句话**：提出 PACE-ECG，用患者时序相邻心电图对比学习结合临床多任务监督进行预训练，并在多队列上评估冻结表征的跨机构迁移。

痛点是生理信号基础模型多在域内评测，跨医疗系统的纵向预后迁移能力不明。方法是对同一患者时间相邻记录做对比学习，并加入临床引导的多任务监督。结果在 MIMIC-IV 与两个外部 HEEDB 队列上优于 DeepECG-SSL，且匹配消融表明患者-时序配对目标显著影响外部迁移。

> 🔗 **与课题**：弱相关，主要触及③能力迁移与⑦评测：论文用外部队列验证和匹配消融量化预训练目标对下游迁移的影响，并提供冻结表征探针式评估思路；但不涉及 LLM/基础模型上的工作流推理、tool/operator grounding、结构化 DAG 生成或 QGIS 图生成。

💡 **启发**：可借用其“匹配消融 + 多外部目标域验证”的协议来量化迁移保持率，并用冻结表征探针区分预训练目标贡献与模型/数据规模贡献。

⚠️ **局限**：研究对象是 ECG 临床预后预测，不是 LLM 工作流知识或工具落地，迁移结论不能直接外推到 GEE→QGIS 的能力迁移。

### 🔥 ⭐⭐⭐⭐ DeltaUI: Framework-Normalized UI State Transition Modeling for Multi-Task Front-End Engineering
**8/10** · Jiaye Lin、Z. C. Guan、Huanyao Zhang、Zonghao Ying、John Yuehan Zhang、Bohan Zeng · ACM International Conference on Multimedia (ACM MM) · 2026-11-06 · [原文](https://doi.org/10.1145/3767308.3835449)
*入选：能力迁移*

> 📄 **摘要**：Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) are emerging as a foundation for automated front-end engineering because they enable joint reasoning over screenshots and natural-language instructions. However, most front-end assistants treat this workflow as a set of isolated code-generation tasks, directly decoding framework-specific syntax for each request. This direct-decoding paradigm leaves three limitations unresolved: (i) it provides no transferable prediction target across tasks and frameworks, (ii) it offers no stable representation for localized edits and repairs, and (iii) it provides no auditable path from multimodal input to executable outcomes. In this paper, we propose DeltaUI, a unified framework that recasts generation, editing, and repair as predictions of transitions in a shared, framework-normalized UI state space, modeling UI change rather than directly decoding target code. DeltaUI begins by parsing heterogeneous front-end artifacts into a typed UI state graph that separates shared UI semantics from framework-specific surface syntax. It then uses a multimodal transition model to predict structured state operations by encoding the source state and multimodal evidence (e.g., screenshots, instructions, and issues), preserving explicit source-to-update correspondence. A deterministic framework-conditioned realizer converts the resulting state into target-framework code. Finally, an execution-aware critic drives a bounded refinement loop using compiler and render feedback, ensuring the full path remains auditable. Extensive experiments on DesignBench and Design2Code demonstrate that modeling UI-state transitions yields consistent gains over direct code-decoding baselines across multiple MLLM backbones while retaining effective transfer to real-world webpages. The code and implementation of DeltaUI are provided in the supplementary material.

**一句话**：提出 DeltaUI，把前端生成/编辑/修复统一建模为在"框架归一化 UI 状态图"上的状态转移预测，再用确定性 realizer 落成目标框架可执行代码。

痛点：现有前端助手把工作流当作一堆孤立的代码生成任务、直接解码框架特定语法，导致缺少可跨任务/跨框架迁移的预测目标、缺少支持局部编辑修复的稳定表示、也缺少从多模态输入到可执行结果的可审计路径。方法：先把异构前端产物解析为带类型的 UI 状态图（分离共享 UI 语义与框架表面语法），用多模态转移模型在源状态+截图/指令/issue 条件下预测结构化状态操作，再由框架条件化的确定性 realizer 转成目标框架代码，最后用 execution-aware critic 结合编译器/渲染反馈驱动有界精修循环。结果：作者声称实现了跨框架归一化的统一建模与可审计的生成-编辑-修复闭环（摘要截断，未见量化迁移保持率）。

> 🔗 **与课题**：支撑环节⑤③④⑦，且是 Route-2 核心假设的直接对照方案。⑤结构化生成：typed UI state graph + 结构化状态操作预测，与我们要求的 processes+links 依赖闭合图同构——图是一等公民而非自由文本解码。③能力迁移："framework-normalized 共享状态空间提供跨任务、跨框架可迁移的预测目标"，正是 GEE→QGIS 跨平台共享表示问题的同构表述（源域语法→归一化语义→目标域语法）。④grounding/落地：确定性的 framework-conditioned realizer 把抽象状态映射为具体框架代码，等价于把工作流步骤 ground 到具体 QGIS operator，是"模型不直接吐算子名、由确定性落地器负责"这一设计的关键外部证据。⑦评测：execution-aware critic + 编译器/渲染反馈的有界精修，可对应执行成功率与幻觉算子率指标及修复循环设计。最关键的对照价值：它主张必须引入显式归一化中间层才能迁移，而 Route-2 假设不需要显式 L3 语义层即可迁移——这篇构成"加中间层"一组的天然基线。

💡 **启发**：把"归一化状态空间 + 确定性 realizer + 执行 critic"三件套改造成 Route-2 的对照组（显式中间层 vs 无中间层），直接测迁移保持率差异；其"分离共享语义与平台表面语法"的分层 schema 也可借来设计 QGIS operator 的类型化算子描述。

⚠️ **局限**：前端 UI 场景与 GIS 工作流差异显著，且摘要未给出跨框架迁移保持率的量化实证，归一化层的构建成本与框架覆盖度是否可扩展存疑。

🚨 **为什么必须重视**：它与核心科学问题正面冲突：若显式框架归一化中间层确实带来跨框架迁移增益，则"不依赖显式 L3 语义层也能迁移"的立论需要被证伪或限定条件，必须立即作为对照组基线纳入实验设计。同时其确定性 realizer + execution critic 的组件化管线可直接改造复用，属于方法层面可借鉴的竞品动向。

### ⭐⭐ Generative AI and Search Engine Orchestration: A User-Controlled Mode Selection and Adaptive Orchestration Framework
**5/10** · Jincheng Zhang · Zenodo (CERN European Organization for Nuclear Research) · 2026-11-07 · [原文](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24032638)
*入选：工作流×工具推理*

> 📄 **摘要**：The increasing availability of generative artificial intelligence (GenAI) systems and conventional search engines has created two major but often disconnected approaches to accessing and processing internet information. Generative AI systems support content creation, explanation, reasoning, summarization, and other language-intensive tasks, whereas conventional search engines provide access to indexed webpages, original sources, official websites, and navigable information resources. Although these capabilities increasingly overlap, users are still frequently required to determine which interface or tool should be used for a particular task. This separation creates additional interaction costs, complicates multi-stage information workflows, and may lead to inappropriate tool selection when user intentions are ambiguous or when a task requires several complementary capabilities. This paper proposes a user-controlled mode selection and adaptive orchestration framework within Generative AI and Search Engine Orchestration (GASEO). Unlike an exclusively automatic routing architecture, the proposed framework incorporates an explicit user-facing mode selection mechanism alongside automatic orchestration. Users may select Generative AI mode, Search Engine mode, Hybrid mode, or other available modes and capabilities introduced through an extensible mode registry. When the user selects a mode, the system treats that selection as an explicit execution preference or constraint rather than merely another weak signal in intent classification. When the user chooses automatic orchestration, the system determines the appropriate execution strategy based on inferred intent, task composition, information freshness, source requirements, capability availability, and routing uncertainty. The framework further supports user overrides, mode switching, controlled hybrid execution, and the preservation of original search sources and generated content as distinguishable output components. The central contribution is a conceptual architecture for combining user-directed execution, system-directed orchestration, and extensible execution modes within a unified internet task interface. The paper formalizes the relationship between user control and automatic routing, proposes a mode-selection policy, describes interaction and execution workflows, and develops a testable evaluation agenda. It also distinguishes explicit user preference from inferred intent and separates mode selection from the internal orchestration of tools within a selected mode. The proposed framework is presented as a research architecture rather than an empirically validated system. Its potential contribution lies in investigating whether a unified interface can reduce the need for users to understand the technical distinctions between internet information-processing capabilities while preserving their ability to determine how their requests are executed.

**一句话**：提出 GASEO 框架：在生成式 AI 与搜索引擎之间提供用户可控的模式选择与自适应编排机制

痛点是生成式 AI 与传统搜索引擎能力重叠但彼此割裂，用户需自行判断该用哪个工具，在多阶段信息工作流中产生额外交互成本，并且意图模糊时容易选错工具。方法上提出 GASEO（Generative AI and Search Engine Orchestration）框架，在自动路由之外加入显式的用户模式选择机制（GenAI 模式、搜索引擎模式、混合模式以及可扩展模式注册表），并把用户选择视为显式执行偏好或约束。结果层面以框架设计与机制论证为主，未见大规模实证或量化评测。

> 🔗 **与课题**：与课题的关联属于通用方法层：对应环节③（能力迁移/工具选择）与⑦（评测）较弱意义上的支撑，即把多工具（GenAI 与搜索）的路由、模式选择与用户约束纳入统一编排；也间接呼应环节②（把任务组织成结构化执行流程）与环节⑤的“显式约束注入”思路。但它研究的是人机交互层面的模式选择与系统编排，而非 LLM 内部工作流知识的跨平台迁移，也没有 GEE→QGIS 或 tool→tool grounding 的实证、迁移保持率、幻觉算子率等指标，因此不足以进入 8-10 分档。

💡 **启发**：把“用户显式选择”建模为硬约束而非软偏好注入编排器的做法，可借用于 QGIS grounding 阶段：把平台/算子族约束显式写入规划条件，抑制幻觉算子；可扩展 mode registry 的思路可类比为算子注册表驱动的候选约束。

### ⭐⭐⭐ Agentic-OPD: Recovering Forgotten Agent Capabilities via On-Policy Distillation for Agentic Tasks
**6/10** · Tingcheng Bian、Kangye Gong、Jinchang Luo、MingQuan Cheng、Feiran Liu、Ni Li · arXiv · 2026-11-09 · [原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12799v1) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.12799v1)
*入选：能力迁移*

> 📄 **摘要**：Reinforcement learning is now the mainstream paradigm for training LLMs on agentic tasks, yet standard practice keeps only the best-validation checkpoint and discards the rest. **一句话**：该工作提出 Agentic-OPD，把 GRPO 训练中丢弃的中间 checkpoint 当作无需额外成本的教师池，用 on-policy 蒸馏恢复最佳 checkpoint 丢失的 agent 能力。

痛点：在 LLM agent 的 RL 训练中，通常只保留在验证集上表现最好的 checkpoint，但其实不同 checkpoint 各自解决过不同问题，取并集后覆盖的问题范围远超单个 checkpoint，因此被丢弃的中间 checkpoint 也包含可能被浪费的解题能力。方法：以最佳验证 checkpoint 为学生，所有被保存的 checkpoint 都作为冻结教师，用 HCN（Highest Coverage Next）按学生对未解题的加权覆盖率动态选教师，并用 LAD（Locality-Aware Distillation）只在学生失败且教师更优的 rollout 上蒸馏。结果：在五个 agentic 领域、24 个基准和任务上验证了能力覆盖恢复的有效性。

> 🔗 **与课题**：主要支撑环节⑥（训练/后训练中的蒸馏与持续学习）：它把蒸馏用作从同一次训练运行内部回收能力的手段，与本课题把蒸馏/持续学习当作候选解释框架的定位一致。同时与环节③有弱相关：它研究的是“能力保持与迁移保持”（学生能否保留教师已具备的能力），其“覆盖并集 vs 单 checkpoint”的度量思路可直接类比 transfer retention（迁移保持率）的评测设计。但它不涉及 GIS workflow reasoning、tool/operator grounding、结构化图生成，迁移维度是同一运行内 checkpoint→student，而非跨域/跨平台（GEE→QGIS）的能力迁移，故未达 8 分。

💡 **启发**：可借鉴“checkpoint 覆盖并集”的度量视角：在多阶段训练（GEE 源域 SFT → QGIS 目标域适配）中保留多个中间 checkpoint，测各阶段在 QGIS grounding 上的解题并集，量化能力丢失与保持（transfer retention）；LAD 的“只在学生失败且教师更优处蒸馏”可作为跨平台适配时避免覆盖已掌握 QGIS 知识的正则化做法。

⚠️ **局限**：该方法依赖 RL 训练中保存多个 checkpoint，且迁移仅限同一模型、同一任务分布内的能力回收，跨平台/跨工具的泛化未被验证。
