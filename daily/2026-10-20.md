### ⭐⭐⭐ "Supplementary Source Code and Inference Engine for Algorithmic Epistemic Calibration (AEC) in Large Language Models"
**6/10** · Rasika Garbhe · IEEE DataPort · 2026-10-18 · [原文](https://doi.org/10.21227/4hty-ft80)
*入选：RL×LLM方法*

> 📄 **摘要**："Large language models (LLMs) executing multi-step agentic workflows are susceptible to epistemic drift, frequently generating ungrounded intermediate states while maintaining high output probability (overconfidence). Static prompt engineering and offline Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF) do not provide runtime calibration during autoregressive decoding. We introduce Algorithmic Epistemic Calibration (AEC), an inference-time control framework that dynamically modulates reasoning steps and verification thresholds using a self-referential consistency metric derived from information geometry. By mapping the latent generation trajectory onto a statistical manifold equipped with the pullback Fisher Information Metric, AEC continuously quantifies local epistemic divergence exclusively through the final language modeling head. We prove theoretically that bounding the cumulative Fisher information distance via a curvature-modulated contraction mapping prevents epistemic collapse, while introducing partitioned attention masks to handle exogenous semantic shocks from external APIs. Empirical evaluations across ALFWorld, WebShop, and HotpotQA using a 70B-parameter architecture demonstrate that AEC reduces trajectory failure rates to a mean of 11.0% and decreases wasted computational tokens by 41.2% compared to post-hoc self-refinement baselines, establishing a low-overhead paradigm for autonomous self-verification."

**一句话**：提出AEC推理时控制框架，用基于Fisher信息几何的自指一致性度量动态调节多步agent工作流的推理步数与验证阈值，抑制认知漂移。

痛点：LLM执行多步agentic工作流时会出现'认知漂移'，生成无依据的中间状态却保持高置信度，而静态提示与离线RLHF无法在解码过程中提供运行时校准。方法：将潜层生成轨迹映射到带pullback Fisher信息度量的统计流形上，仅通过最终语言模型头量化局部认知散度，并以曲率调制的收缩映射约束累积Fisher信息距离，同时用分区注意力掩码处理外部API带来的语义冲击。结果：在ALFWorld、WebShop、HotpotQA上用70B模型评测，轨迹失败率均值降至11.0%，无效token消耗较事后自反思基线减少41.2%。

> 🔗 **与课题**：支撑环节①与⑦为主：①方面它直接研究多步agent工作流运行中的中间状态可靠性与步数自适应控制，属于工作流推理的过程控制；⑦方面它提供轨迹失败率、无效token占比等可迁移的过程级评测指标，可用于衡量工作流推理质量与幻觉步骤率。但它不涉及②结构化/依赖图生成、③跨域跨平台能力迁移、④operator grounding、⑤processes+links图生成，⑥也非其重点（推理时方法而非训练后训练）。因此属于'通用方法支撑'而非核心撞车。

💡 **启发**：可借用其'轨迹级自指一致性度量+动态验证阈值'作为我们workflow推理阶段的在线依赖闭合/幻觉步骤检测信号；失败率与浪费token的度量口径可直接移植到评测环节。

⚠️ **局限**：方法依赖信息几何理论推导与70B规模实验，未验证跨域迁移与结构化图落地，且分区注意力掩码需侵入式修改推理引擎，复现与在我们QGIS场景的适配成本较高。

### ⭐⭐ RSIFlow: Adaptive Self-Improvement Workflows with an Evolving Meta-Agent
**5/10** · Huan Zhang、Mingju Chen、Dongxu Zhou、Can Lv、Sen Cui、Faguo Wu · arXiv (Cornell University) · 2026-10-15 · [原文](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2610.21791)
*入选：工作流×工具推理*

> 📄 **摘要**：Large language model (LLM) agents have demonstrated strong capabilities in complex tasks, including code generation, tool use, and multi-hop question answering. Existing self-improvement methods enhance agent performance through model optimization, harness evolution, and executable artifact refinement, while recursive self-improvement (RSI) further extends optimization to the improvement mechanisms themselves. However, adapting self-improvement workflows to evolving performance bottlenecks while improving workflow orchestration through execution feedback remains a key challenge. We propose \textbf{RSIFlow}, a \textbf{R}ecursive \textbf{S}elf-\textbf{I}mprovement framework that integrates adaptive work\textbf{flow} orchestration with meta-agent capability evolution. RSIFlow diagnoses performance bottlenecks from task-agent trajectories and environmental feedback to dynamically select component-specific improvement workflows. The meta-agent then distills workflow outcomes into component-specific skills and cross-component principles, evolving its own harness to improve subsequent workflow orchestration. Experiments across six benchmarks and two backbone models show that RSIFlow achieves the SOTA average performance on all benchmarks. Ablation studies further demonstrate the contributions of adaptive workflow orchestration and meta-harness evolution. Code is available at https://github.com/1HUAN1/RSI-Flow.

**一句话**：提出 RSIFlow 递归自改进框架，用元智能体从执行轨迹诊断瓶颈、动态编排组件级改进工作流，并把工作流结果蒸馏为技能与跨组件原则。
