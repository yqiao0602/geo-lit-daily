### ⭐⭐⭐⭐ TaskFrame: Connecting Task-Level Planning with Frame-Level Control for Game Agents
**7/10** · Guangzhi Wang、Junfu Pu、Yiran Wang、Xingyilang Yin、Honghao Chen、Cheng Cheng · arXiv · 2026-10-29 · [原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40276v1) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.40276v1)
*入选：RL×LLM方法*

> 📄 **摘要**：Building general game agents requires both high-level task reasoning and low-latency action control in rapidly changing visual environments. These capabilities favor models with substantially different computational profiles, making them difficult to realize within a single model. In this work, we present TaskFrame, a hierarchical planner-controller framework that connects task-level planning with frame-level keyboard-and-mouse control. A vision-language task planner decomposes a high-level goal into an ordered sequence of atomic instructions and is optimized through supervised learning and dependency-aware GRPO. The hidden states of special goal tokens that terminate each atomic instruction provide continuous goal embeddings that are cached and reused across control steps. A causal Transformer controller combines the embedding of the active goal with recent observations to predict discrete keyboard and mouse-button actions and continuous camera motion at every game step. We train the system in three stages: planner training, controller pretraining, and joint alignment. Experiments in Minecraft show that TaskFrame is competitive with general-purpose vision-language models on instruction decomposition, and that in closed-loop evaluation the agent achieves a higher combat success rate than the evaluated baselines.

**一句话**：提出 TaskFrame 分层规划器-控制器框架，用视觉语言任务规划器把高层目标分解为有序原子指令序列，并把目标 token 隐状态作为连续目标嵌入交给因果 Transformer 控制器做帧级键鼠控制。

痛点：通用游戏智能体既需要高层任务推理又需要低延迟动作控制，单一模型难以兼顾两种差异极大的计算剖面。方法：规划器用监督学习 + 依赖感知 GRPO 优化，把目标分解为有序原子指令，并以终止指令的特殊 goal token 隐状态作为可缓存复用的连续目标嵌入；控制器结合该嵌入与近期观测预测离散按键/鼠标与连续视角动作；三阶段训练（规划器训练、控制器预训练、联合对齐）。结果：在 Minecraft 上指令分解能力与通用 VLM 相当，闭环评测战斗成功率优于基线。

> 🔗 **与课题**：支撑环节①任务分解/流程规划：论文核心就是把高层目标分解成有序原子指令序列，属于典型的 workflow reasoning（步骤规划 + 顺序依赖），可对照“GIS Task→Workflow”的分解粒度与顺序正确性设计。支撑环节②结构化表示中的依赖组织：规划目标显式建模原子指令间依赖，并用 dependency-aware GRPO 奖励顺序/依赖正确性，这与“依赖闭合的工作流图”约束直接同构。支撑环节⑥训练/后训练：给出 SFT→预训练→联合对齐的三阶段配方，以及把结构正确性写成 RL 奖励的做法（RL 绑定 workflow 场景，按规则算高相关）。弱支撑环节④grounding：抽象指令通过 goal embedding 接口落到具体动作空间，是“上层语义→下层算子”的分层落地范式，但动作是游戏键鼠而非 tool/operator，也无 QGIS 类图结构生成。不涉及环节③跨域/跨平台能力迁移（规划器到控制器是管线内表示复用，非跨域能力保持）与⑤结构化图生成。

💡 **启发**：可直接借鉴“依赖感知奖励（dependency-aware GRPO）”的设计：把相邻步骤的依赖成立性、顺序合法性作为可验证奖励信号，用于训练/评测工作流推理的依赖闭合度；另外“终止 token 隐状态作为可缓存目标嵌入”为分层架构中上层计划向下层执行器的传递提供了一种轻量接口，可类比把 workflow 计划的表示传给 QGIS 算子选择模块。

⚠️ **局限**：场景为游戏视觉控制，规划器与控制器耦合在闭环动作空间，缺少 tool/API 级别的算子落地与图结构输出，迁移到 GIS 工作流需重新定义状态与奖励。

### ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Reasoning Patterns for Efficient AI Coding: An Empirical Study
**7/10** · Zhe Yin、Yuling Shi、Xiaodong Gu、Beijun Shen · arXiv (Cornell University) · 2026-10-26 · [原文](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2610.34943)
*入选：领域×任务*

> 📄 **摘要**：Large Reasoning Models (LRMs) generate long reasoning traces that expose intermediate inference behavior, offering opportunities to study and control reasoning in AI coding. However, the underlying patterns in these traces and their potential for analyzing and guiding coding models remain poorly understood, limiting systematic characterization and inference-time control of LRM reasoning. We conduct an empirical study of reasoning patterns across four representative coding tasks. We transform reasoning traces into structured action sequences and mine recurring fragments using within-task support, cross-task contrasts, and valid--invalid support contrasts. We characterize four general reasoning patterns by coding agents: design-to-implementation for code generation, state tracing for execution reasoning, evidence--fix--recheck for debugging, and semantic mapping for translation. We further identify positive and negative patterns associated with valid and invalid reasoning traces, respectively. Positive patterns reflect verification-driven convergence, whereas negative patterns reflect repeated design revision, reflection, and knowledge recall without closure. To demonstrate their practical utility, we evaluate three pattern-based applications: task identification, pattern-guided prompting, and pattern-aware early stopping. Features capturing general reasoning patterns enable accurate task identification from reasoning traces without accessing task descriptions or final answers. Prompting guided by positive and negative patterns improves the overall accuracy of downstream tasks, while negative pattern-aware early stopping substantially reduces API-reported token usage with only a modest decrease in pass@1. All three applications also generalize to previously unseen models, showing that reasoning patterns can support both the analysis and control of LRM behavior in AI coding.

**一句话**：该实证研究把大型推理模型在四类编码任务上的推理轨迹转成结构化的动作序列，并挖掘、刻画可复用的正向与负向推理模式。

痛点：大型推理模型（LRM）的推理轨迹很长，其中隐含的推理模式尚未被系统理解，也难以在推理时加以控制。方法：作者将推理轨迹转化为结构化动作序列，用任务内支持度、跨任务对比以及有效与无效轨迹的对比来挖掘反复出现的推理片段，归纳出四种通用模式（design-to-implementation、state tracing、evidence-fix-recheck、semantic mapping），并区分出正向模式与无效推理对应的负向模式。结果：基于这些模式实现了三项应用——任务识别、模式引导提示、模式感知提前停止，而仅凭通用推理模式特征就能在没有任务描述和最终答案的情况下准确识别任务。

> 🔗 **与课题**：支撑环节②⑦（部分支撑①）：最直接的是把自由文本推理轨迹转成结构化动作序列，这一手法可用来观察模型在 GEE workflow 上的推理轨迹，并判断其步骤组织是否符合依赖闭合；其次是正向模式与负向模式（verification-driven convergence 与 repeated revision/reflection without closure）的判据，可直接迁移为工作流推理质量与幻觉算子率的分析维度，用于评测模型在 QGIS 落地前的规划是否收敛、是否反复重规划；模式引导提示与模式感知提前停止属于推理时控制，可作为 SFT 之外的轻量干预基线。但它限于编码任务，不涉及跨平台迁移与算子 grounding，因此只属通用方法层支撑。

💡 **启发**：可借鉴“把推理轨迹切成结构化动作序列，再用有效轨迹与无效轨迹的对比挖掘正负片段”的协议，用于刻画模型从 GEE workflow 迁到 QGIS 时的步骤模式，以及设计“没有闭环”的负向模式检测器（可作幻觉算子、循环重规划的信号）。

⚠️ **局限**：仅在四个编码任务上做观察性模式的实证研究，模式分类缺乏与执行成功率、结构正确性的因果验证，跨任务推广性有限。

**▸ 建议：精读**

### ⭐⭐⭐ KARMA: Training-Free Knowledge-Atomic Harness Evolution for Industrial Advertising Agents
**6/10** · Xinhua Miao、Yuanzhen Rong、Weiru Zhang、Xiaoyi Zeng · arXiv · 2026-10-29 · [原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39884v1) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.39884v1)
*入选：能力迁移*

> 📄 **摘要**：Industrial advertising agents must continually adapt their decision knowledge to changing product offerings, traffic patterns, and operating policies. We present KARMA (Knowledge-Atomic, Regression-Gated Modular Adaptation), a training-free framework that evolves six typed knowledge modules while keeping model weights and executable harness code frozen. During our evolution, a deterministic Trace Verifier maps observed errors to candidate modules, and a Critic compares successful and failed trajectories to guide a tool-constrained Evolver. The Evolver applies atomic knowledge edits, which a fixed validation gate retains only when their measured gain reaches a threshold; otherwise, the previous knowledge state is restored. We evaluate KARMA with three LLM families on cases sampled from a corpus of approximately half a million real-world operation logs across six e-commerce domains. Across 18 domain--model settings, KARMA raises the average hold-out score from 30.19 to 39.15 (+8.96 points, +29.7%). Ablations show that removing either successful or failed trajectories more than halves the average hold-out gain. Evolved knowledge also transfers across domains without further adaptation, yielding an average hold-out gain of 8.54 points across all source--target pairs. By linking retained patches to their diagnostic evidence and validation outcomes, KARMA supports inspectable, reversible knowledge adaptation for agents in Web marketplaces.

**一句话**：提出 KARMA 框架：不训练、不修改执行代码，只用 Trace Verifier、Critic 和工具约束的 Evolver 对六类带类型知识模块做原子化编辑，并由固定回归门控决定保留或回滚。

痛点：工业广告 agent 需要持续跟随商品、流量和政策变化更新决策知识，但重训成本高、不可逆。方法：把错误轨迹映射到具体知识模块，对比成功与失败轨迹引导受工具约束的编辑，只有增益超过阈值才保留原子补丁，否则回滚到上一知识状态。结果：在约 50 万条真实运营日志、6 个电商域、3 个 LLM 家族共 18 个设置上，hold-out 平均分从 30.19 升到 39.15（+29.7%），且进化出的知识可无额外适配地跨域迁移，平均增益 8.54 分。

> 🔗 **与课题**：主要支撑环节③（能力迁移）：它给出了“源域进化知识 → 目标域零样本适配”的实证与度量方式，可作为 transfer retention 的对照协议；其次支撑环节⑦（评测），其回归门控+留出增益+回滚机制是一种可审计、可逆的适配效果评估范式；再次与环节⑥相关但方向相反，它用知识层原子编辑替代权重更新（training-free），可作为 DAPT/SFT 路线的轻量对照方案；对环节②⑤（结构化/DAG 生成）与④（operator grounding）无直接贡献，学习对象是广告决策知识而非工作流结构或工具参数。

💡 **启发**：可借鉴“错误轨迹→定位到具体模块→只改该模块→门控验证”的闭环，把 QGIS 落地失败（幻觉算子、依赖断裂）映射回具体 workflow 步骤或 grounding 子技能，做原子化修正而非整体重训；同时应同时使用成功轨迹与失败轨迹来构建对比信号（消融显示二者缺一增益减半）。

⚠️ **局限**：知识模块由人工预定义类型、场景限于电商运营日志，迁移增益以任务 hold-out 分数衡量，无法直接说明工作流结构或图结构层面的迁移保持率。

### ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Locating and Closing Information Gaps in Multi-Agent Systems
**7/10** · Yaxin Gong、Chongming Gao、Gangyi Zhang、Chenxiao Fan、Leyang Shen、Jiakai Wang · arXiv · 2026-10-29 · [原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40186v1) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.40186v1)
*入选：工作流×工具推理*

> 📄 **摘要**：Multi-step Web tasks often require several LLM agents to gather and combine evidence. The communication graph of a fixed workflow restricts which agents can read each other's outputs: evidence retrieved by one agent may never reach the agent that needs it, leaving an information gap and an incomplete or incorrect answer. Adding a connection can deliver the missing evidence, but can also lead the receiver to adopt an incorrect upstream conclusion that displaces a correct answer; the final output alone does not separate the two. We propose Selective Probing and Link Insertion for Closing information gaps in multi-agent Execution (SPLICE), which locates information gaps per query and decides what to adopt after adding an edge. After the original workflow runs, SPLICE uses the query and node outputs to locate a possible gap, adds one directed edge from a source to a receiver to deliver the relevant evidence, and re-executes the receiver and affected downstream nodes, producing a candidate answer. The adoption decision reads both the final answers of the two runs and the process changes the edit caused inside the workflow: how far the receiver moved, where its new content came from, and whether the change reached the final answer. A selector trained on these process features chooses between adopting the candidate answer and retaining the original. On five benchmarks, SPLICE raises the task score on Web tool use by 8.0% and the mean score on four reasoning and planning benchmarks by 5.7% relative to the static skeleton, at 1.25 to 1.44$\times$ the skeleton's cost, and transfers zero-shot to four unseen backbones.

**一句话**：SPLICE 通过定位固定多智能体工作流通信图中的信息缺口，插入一条有向边并重跑下游节点，再用过程特征训练选择器判断是否采纳新答案。

痛点：多步 Web 任务中固定通信图限制了智能体间的证据可达性，造成信息缺口；而单纯加一条连接又可能让接收方采纳上游的错误结论。方法：先定位缺口，插入从源到接收方的有向边，重执行受影响的下游节点得到候选答案；再用两次运行的过程特征训练选择器决定是否采纳。结果：在 5 个基准上 Web 工具使用任务分数提升 8.0%，四个推理与规划基准平均分数也有提升。

> 🔗 **与课题**：主要支撑②（Workflow→结构化表示）与⑤（结构化图生成）两个环节，兼有⑦的评测思路。论文把工作流视为“节点+有向边”的通信图，核心操作是“定位缺失的边（依赖缺口）→插入边→重执行下游受影响节点”，这与我们关注的 QGIS processes+links 图中“依赖闭合、边缺失导致不可执行”的问题结构同构。其次，它用过程特征（接收方移动距离、新内容来源、改动是否传导到最终输出）而非仅最终答案来判定结构编辑是否有效，为“结构正确性/依赖闭合”的可测化提供了协议雏形。再次，它提供“两次运行对照+因果归因”的编辑验证范式，可用于衡量生成图的结构修复是否真的带来可执行性提升。局限在于它是修复既有图的一条边，而非从任务出发生成整张图，且不涉及工具/算子落地，因此只算支撑性通用方法，不构成①③的直接证据。

💡 **启发**：可直接借用“加一条边→只重跑受影响下游→用过程特征判定是否采纳”的因果验证协议：迁移到 QGIS 图生成上，就是先自动检测缺失 link、孤立 process 或依赖断链，再局部补边并重判该图是否变得依赖闭合、可执行。另外，把“改动是否传导到终点”作为结构编辑有效性的代理指标，可作为我们评测指标之一（对应传输到最终可执行图的改动传播率）。

⚠️ **局限**：只针对固定多智能体通信图做单边插入修复，既非从零生成结构，也未做跨平台/跨工具迁移验证；'信息缺口'是通信语义而非工具算子语义，与 QGIS operator grounding 之间的对应关系需自己论证。

### ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Online Dense Reinforcement Learning with Planning-Grounding Agent for UI Navigation on Live IOS Apps
**7/10** · Peitong Duan、Khashayar Gatmiry、Jeffrey Nichols、Samy Bengio、Emmanuel Abbe · arXiv · 2026-10-29 · [原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39264v1) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.39264v1)
*入选：RL×LLM方法*

> 📄 **摘要**：While frontier vision-language models demonstrate strong capabilities in user interface (UI) navigation, their high inference costs prohibit everyday use. Conversely, smaller models trained via supervised fine-tuning (SFT) or offline reinforcement learning often memorize static trajectories, leading to brittle execution and an inability to recover from errors. To address these limitations, we propose a novel online reinforcement learning framework that trains UI navigation agents directly on live iOS applications. Our framework decouples the agent into two specialized components: a Planning agent and a UI Grounding model. We rely on an LLM-as-a-Judge to provide comprehensive reward signals, combining dense, step-level feedback to evaluate intermediate progress with a terminal reward for overall task completion. To effectively leverage this dual reward structure, we optimize the agent using Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) with step-level advantage estimation. Furthermore, we introduce GiGPPO, a novel neighbor-conditioned value estimation method that improves critic stability by leveraging reward signals from visually similar states across concurrent trajectories. To support this online training paradigm at scale, we introduce an efficient infrastructure that orchestrates hundreds of concurrent virtual iOS devices. Evaluations on rigorous benchmarks, including UINavBench, iOSWorld, and AndroidWorld, demonstrate that our online RL trained agents substantially outperform both SFT and terminal-only RL baselines while accelerating training convergence. Notably, our 8B RL agents achieve nearly a sevenfold improvement over the SFT baseline in out-of-distribution generalization to Android environments, while outperforming Gemini-3.5-Flash and tying with Gemini-3.1-Pro on real iOS applications.

**一句话**：论文提出在线强化学习框架，把 UI 导航智能体拆成「Planning 智能体 + UI Grounding 模型」两个专门组件，在真实 iOS 应用上训练，并用 LLM-as-a-Judge 的密集步级奖励和 PPO 优化。

痛点：前沿视觉语言模型做 UI 导航推理成本过高，而小模型用 SFT 或离线 RL 训练只会记住静态轨迹，执行脆弱、出错后无法恢复。方法：把智能体解耦为负责规划的 Planning agent 和负责界面元素定位的 Grounding 模型，用 LLM-as-a-Judge 同时提供步级密集奖励和终局奖励，用 PPO 加步级优势估计优化，并引入 GiGPPO（邻居条件化的价值估计，利用并发轨迹中视觉相似状态的奖励信号稳定 critic），配套支持数百台并发虚拟 iOS 设备的基础设施。结果：在 UINavBench、iOSWorld、AndroidWorld 上，在线 RL 训练的智能体显著优于 SFT 与离线 RL 基线，并能从错误中恢复。

### ⭐⭐ Retrieving from Your Own Mistakes: On-Policy Training of Embedding Models for Multi-Hop Retrieval-Augmented Generation

> 📄 **摘要**：Complex questions in retrieval-augmented generation require evidence spread over several documents, and an answer is reliable only when the retriever finds all of it.
