### ⭐⭐⭐⭐ SLIC: Reinforcement Fine-Tuning Small LMs for Multi-Turn Analog Circuit Optimization
**7/10** · Yan Xu、Zhengqi Gao、Ching-Yun Ko、Zhang-Wei Hong、Wenjie Lu、Tao Yu · arXiv (Cornell University) · 2026-11-10 · [原文](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2611.13452)
*入选：能力迁移*

> 📄 **摘要**：Analog integrated circuit (IC) design is a multi-turn, simulator-grounded engineering task that demands iterative reasoning over coupled performance tradeoffs, yet proprietary process design kits (PDKs) make hosted large language model workflows difficult to deploy in practical design settings. We present SLIC (Small LM Iterating on Circuits), a reinforcement fine-tuning framework that trains a 4B-scale, locally deployable language model for closed-loop analog circuit optimization. SLIC pairs structured circuit representations with discretized relative-update actions, domain- and success-gated multiobjective rewards, and Reward-Guided Rollout Steering (RGRS), which turns per-step simulator feedback into signals for both policy learning and online rollout construction in this sparse, highly constrained domain. We further release an open, goal-conditioned benchmark of 41 amplifier topologies with standardized protocols for in-distribution validation, held-out topology evaluation, and cross-technology-node transfer. Trained solely with open source 65 nm CMOS predictive technology models (PTM), SLIC-Random achieves 87.1% in-distribution four-specification Pass@1 within eight turns, surpassing the 763B DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash Think by 48.5 percentage points. Beyond the training distribution, SLIC-Softmax reaches 72.6% Pass@1 on held-out topologies, and the trained policies consistently transfer across five unseen technology nodes without additional fine-tuning. This transfer extends to a commercial TSMC 65 nm CMOS PDK, where SLIC-Random achieves 49.9% held-out-topology Pass@1, demonstrating practical deployment through local inference without transmitting proprietary data to external LLM services. Performance further scales with test-time simulator interactions, reaching up to 83.2% held-out-topology Pass@1 at a 32-turn budget.

**一句话**：面向模拟电路多轮优化的强化微调框架：用4B小模型结合结构化电路表示与离散相对更新动作，在仿真器闭环中迭代优化。

痛点：模拟IC设计是多轮、依赖仿真器的迭代推理任务，而商业PDK使托管大模型难以落地，本地小模型又缺乏领域能力。方法：SLIC 用结构化电路表示、离散化相对更新动作、以领域与成功为条件的多目标奖励，以及把每步仿真反馈转成策略学习与在线 rollout 信号的 RGRS，对4B模型做强化微调。结果：在8轮内同分布四规格 Pass@1 达87.1%，超过763B模型48.5个百分点；在留出拓扑上达72.6%，并在五个未见工艺节点上零微调迁移。

> 🔗 **与课题**：支撑环节③⑦，部分支撑④⑥。③：论文给出"源域→目标域"能力迁移的实证，即只用65nm PTM训练、在五个未见工艺节点上零微调保持性能，并有专门的留出拓扑与跨节点迁移评测协议，可作为我们"迁移保持率（transfer retention）"指标的测量范式参照；⑦：发布41个放大器拓扑的目标条件基准，明确区分同分布验证、留出拓扑、跨域迁移三种评测设定，与我们"迁移保持率+落地可用率"的评测设计同构；④：把模型输出grounding到仿真器可执行的离散相对更新动作，是一种受限算子空间下的落地方式（token→可执行动作），与我们把workflow grounding到QGIS processes的思路类比；⑥：展示了在强约束、稀疏奖励、工具闭环场景下用小模型+RL后训练获得领域能力的可行路径。但研究对象是电路参数迭代优化，不涉及工作流分解、依赖图/DAG生成，因此不触及①②⑤。

💡 **启发**：可直接借用其"三档评测协议"（同分布、留出结构、跨技术节点零微调迁移）来设计我们的"GEE训练→QGIS迁移"保持率评测；以及"把每步执行器反馈转为在线rollout构造信号"的RGRS思路，可用于QGIS执行失败反馈驱动的工作流采样。

⚠️ **局限**：任务本质是连续参数的多轮数值调优而非离散工作流结构生成，迁移是参数/工艺分布层面的迁移，与跨工具、跨平台的工作流知识迁移不可直接类比。

### ⭐⭐ From Process Models to Composable Agentic Workflows: Combining BPMN, Agents and Enterprise Knowledge
**5/10** · Christian Fillies、Jan Fillies、Frauke Weichhardt、Gay Anne Wood-Albrecht · arXiv (Cornell University) · 2026-11-09 · [原文](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2611.13196)
*入选：工作流×工具推理*

> 📄 **摘要**：LLM-based agents can reason, use tools and collaborate. However, coordinating increasingly complex agentic applications becomes difficult to understand, monitor and improve. Decisions distributed across prompts, tool calls and application logic make workflows implicit. We introduce **Recursive Capability Composition**, enabling agents to execute business processes and expose them as reusable Agentic Tools. This paper presents a BPMN-based approach to Agentic Workflows, combining explicit orchestration with autonomous reasoning. SemTalk BPMN models are transformed into executable workflows using a LangGraph/Pregel runtime. Agentic Tasks introduce LLM reasoning at selected activities, while conventional BPMN controls process flows, deterministic decisions, system interactions and human approvals. Agentic Participants provide reusable interfaces for agent behavior or other executable processes. BPMN orchestration makes multi-agent systems more understandable and manageable. Explicit process models provide developers and operators with a shared view of workflow structures and execution progress, facilitating debugging, monitoring and optimization. Enterprise agents also need organization-specific context. Process Models and Knowledge Graphs describe activities, participants, information objects, decisions and relationships between processes. They provide structured knowledge of how an organization operates, supporting informed agent decisions and actions. Our contributions are threefold: 1. **Model-Driven Execution:** Transforming BPMN models into executable Agentic Workflows. 2. **Recursive Capability Composition:** Exposing agents and executable business processes as reusable tools for other agents. 3. **Enterprise Context:** Using Process Models and Ontologies to provide organization-specific knowledge for agent reasoning.

**一句话**：提出基于BPMN的Agentic Workflow方法，将业务流程模型经LangGraph/Pregel转换为可执行工作流，并把智能体能力封装为可复用工具。

痛点：LLM多智能体应用的决策分散在提示、工具调用和应用逻辑中，导致工作流隐式、难以理解、监控和改进。方法：用BPMN显式编排流程，在选定活动引入LLM推理，Agentic Participant提供可复用接口，并结合知识图谱提供企业上下文。结果：提升多智能体系统的可理解性、可监控性和复用性（摘要截断）。

> 🔗 **与课题**：主要支撑②Workflow→结构化表示和⑤结构化图生成：BPMN流程模型被转换为可执行工作流图；同时涉及④工具/算子落地（Agentic Tools封装）和①流程编排/任务分解。但该文依赖显式BPMN中间层，与Route-2“不依赖显式L3语义中间层”的核心设定形成对照，而非直接支撑；也缺少跨平台能力迁移（GEE→QGIS）的实证。

💡 **启发**：可把BPMN/DAG作为“显式中间层”对照基线，比较显式流程模型与模型内部工作流知识在生成QGIS processes+links图时的差异；Agentic Tool的可复用接口设计可借鉴为QGIS operator grounding接口。

⚠️ **局限**：企业业务流程场景，无LLM能力迁移或GIS operator grounding实证，且显式BPMN编排与课题的无显式中层假设相悖。

### ⭐⭐ DisasterAgent: An Evidence-Driven Agent for Verifiable Disaster Report Generation From Remote Sensing Imagery
**5/10** · Zhijian Li、Jiawei Song、Chao Ren、Yidan Zhang、Peijin Wang、Xian Sun · arXiv (Cornell University) · 2026-11-10 · [原文](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2611.13776)
*入选：领域×任务*

> 📄 **摘要**：Remote sensing disaster assessment is moving from simple damage classification toward comprehensive report-level analysis. In this setting, responders need not only damage descriptions but also verifiable evidence to guide response and recovery. Recent vision-language models (VLMs) and tool-augmented agents offer strong multimodal reasoning and perception, but they struggle to provide the evidence required for high-stakes verification. To address these limitations, we propose DisasterAgent, an evidence-driven agent that uses a Perception-Augmentation-Generation workflow to generate verifiable disaster reports from bitemporal optical imagery. DisasterAgent first invokes a specialized vision expert toolkit in the perception phase to build a Spatiotemporal Perception Graph (STPG), serving as a structured memory to store and organize disaster-induced changes across space and time. Evidence-based Augmentation (EA) then extracts evidence from the STPG, attaching inline citations that form a closed evidence loop from each statement to the supporting visual evidence. Extensive experiments on the DisasterM3 and RSCC benchmarks demonstrate that DisasterAgent consistently outperforms general-domain VLMs, remote sensing-specialized models, and existing remote sensing agents. Notably, compared with the original model, it improves the average score for disaster report generation on DisasterM3 by 13.5\% and ST5-SCS on RSCC by 15.34\%, providing a more accurate and verifiable solution for disaster emergency response. The code will be available at https://github.com/ZhijianLi2003/DisasterAgent.

**一句话**：提出 DisasterAgent 证据驱动智能体，用“感知—增强—生成”三阶段工作流调用遥感视觉专家工具构建时空感知图（STPG），为灾害报告生成可验证的内联证据引用。

痛点：高风险灾害评估场景中，VLM 与工具增强智能体虽具备多模态推理能力，却难以提供可供核验的证据链。方法：先用专用视觉专家工具集构建时空感知图作为结构化记忆，再从中抽取证据并附加内联引用，形成“陈述—视觉证据”的闭合回路。结果：在 DisasterM3 与 RSCC 上，它全面优于通用 VLM、遥感专用模型与现有遥感智能体，报告生成平均分提升 13.5% 和 15.34%。

> 🔗 **与课题**：部分支撑②“结构化表示”与⑦“评测”：STPG 是一种显式的结构化中间记忆图，证据闭合回路则可视为一种可核验性评测协议；工具集调用与④“算子落地”有弱关联，三阶段流水线对①“任务分解”也只是固定模板，并非可学习的工作流推理。更值得注意的是，它恰好站在本课题假设的对立面：它靠显式的结构化中间层实现工具落地与可验证性，因此可作为“显式 L3 语义中间层是否必需”这一核心论点的对照案例。但它本质上是遥感灾害报告生成的应用论文，GIS 与遥感是任务语境，而不是研究对象。

💡 **启发**：“证据闭合回路”这一做法（每条陈述强制内联引用到底层视觉证据）可直接迁移为本课题的幻觉算子率与 QGIS 落地可用率评测：要求每个生成的 process/link 节点必须回指到源任务或上游依赖证据。

⚠️ **局限**：工作流是人工固定的三阶段模板，且以遥感影像为唯一模态，无法说明工作流推理能力能否跨平台迁移到 QGIS 的算子 grounding。

### 🔥 ⭐⭐⭐⭐ ReTrace: Distilling Tool-Using Agents into Planner-Free Workflows
**8/10** · Kaiyuan Hou、Yang Ping Kuo、Indranil Bhattacharya、Rohith Nama、Arushi Gupta、Carlos D. Castillo · arXiv (Cornell University) · 2026-11-10 · [原文](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2611.14492)
*入选：工作流×工具推理*

> 📄 **摘要**：Business process automation runs the same Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) again and again on new inputs. Large Language Model (LLM) agents can follow an SOP written in natural language, but they call an LLM to choose every step of every run, which adds latency and cost at scale. We present ReTrace, which turns an SOP and a few logged agent runs into a reusable workflow in which code, not an LLM, selects the next step. The agent's reasoning, the explanation it writes with each tool call, often states why it took an action; the tool calls alone do not. ReTrace extracts the condition behind each step, merges these conditions across runs into a compact rule document, and generates an executable workflow from it. Only this last stage depends on the platform, so rule documents can be compiled for different orchestration platforms. On $79$ procedures from our production platform, ReTrace workflows reach $93.8\%$ completion, against $96.8\%$ for the agent, at $24\%$ lower cost per task. On $τ^2$-Bench, they reach $75.8\%$ task success (agent: $80.6\%$) and run in $1.3$s instead of $170$s; three rounds of repair on training failures lift $k{=}2$ workflows from $66.7\%$ to $81.2\%$. On eight SOP-Bench procedures, ReTrace without repair trails few-shot generation ($80.6\%$ vs. $83.6\%$), but repair from labeled training failures lifts it to $87.8\%$ at $0.40$s per task, compared to $85.5\%$ in $46.6$s for the agent.

**一句话**：ReTrace 将 LLM 工具使用智能体的推理轨迹（reasoning）和工具调用日志蒸馏成条件规则文档，再编译成由代码决定下一步、无需 LLM planner 的可执行工作流，并可跨平台复用。

痛点是 SOP 类业务流程每次执行都要 LLM 逐步决策，成本高、延迟大。方法是从智能体的 reasoning 中抽取每步背后的条件，跨多次运行合并成紧凑规则文档，再生成可执行工作流；只有最后一个环节依赖平台，因此同一规则文档可编译到不同编排平台。结果：生产 79 个流程上完成率 93.8%（agent 96.8%），单任务成本降低 24%；τ²-Bench 任务成功率 75.8%（agent 80.6%），耗时从 170s 降到 1.3s；三轮失败修复把 k=2 的工作流从 66.7% 提升到 81.2%。

> 🔗 **与课题**：支撑环节②③④⑥⑦，并直接触及本课题的“显式语义中间层是否必需”这一核心争点。① 它把一次性的任务执行整理成可复用的分步流程，属于 workflow 抽取而非从零规划，可作为“任务→工作流”的反向证据（工作流可从执行轨迹中被归纳出来）。② 它抽取每步条件并合并成规则文档、再生成可执行工作流，本质是 Workflow→结构化表示（隐式 DAG/依赖组织），且论证“步骤间的依赖条件”才是结构化的关键，与 processes+links 的依赖闭合直接对应。③ 它明确做跨平台迁移：规则文档与平台解耦，可编译到不同编排平台；这属于源域→目标域的工作流级迁移实证，但迁移发生在系统或规则层，而非模型内部参数层，所以是本课题“迁移保持率”的一个外部对照口径。④ 生成的工作流最终落到具体工具调用上执行（τ²-Bench、SOP-Bench），是 workflow→operator grounding 的落地实例，其“幻觉步骤”可通过规则文档中的条件约束来压低。⑥ 蒸馏：把 agent 的 reasoning 与工具调用日志蒸馏为静态流程，并把 reasoning 当作蒸馏源、在推理时丢弃（planner-free），这与本课题“不依赖显式 L3 语义层”的假设高度呼应——它说明只要语义层只在训练或蒸馏期存在，就能满足 Route-2 的目标。⑦ 评测：完成率、任务成功率、单任务成本、延迟，以及“用带标签训练失败做三轮 repair”的自提升协议，可直接迁移成 QGIS 落地修复回路。

💡 **启发**：最可借鉴的是：把 reasoning 当作蒸馏期的“条件抽取器”，在推理期彻底丢弃它，只保留由条件驱动的代码选择器；以及“用带标签的做法，对失败执行做多轮 repair，再回灌规则文档”的闭环，可直接搬到 QGIS process 落地失败（如幻觉算子、连接断开）的修复上。

⚠️ **局限**：它的“迁移”是规则文档跨编排平台的编译级迁移，不是模型内部工作流知识的迁移，因此不能直接回答 GEE→QGIS 的模型能力保持率问题；而且它依赖生产平台上大量带日志的同构 SOP 运行数据。

🚨 **为什么必须重视**：它与本课题的核心假设正面相关：论文用实证说明推理期可以完全去掉 LLM planner，只要蒸馏期保留 reasoning 抽取条件；这既是对“显式 L3 语义层非必需”的支持证据，也为 QGIS 落地提供了一种 planner-free 替代路线。它的条件规则文档、可执行工作流、失败 repair 三段式，与本课题的“Workflow 推理→结构化表示→落地”管线逐段对应，可直接作为对比基线与评测协议参照。若先把这套 distill→compile→repair 的做法实现到 QGIS 侧，就能在竞品之前拿到落地可用率与幻觉算子率的量化结果。

### ⭐⭐⭐ Standards and Requirements for LLM-Assisted, Human-Supervised Evidence-Centered Research: A Living Scoping Review
**6/10** · Brian P. Reagan · OSF Preprints (OSF Preprints) · 2026-11-09 · [原文](https://openalex.org/W7225841228)
*入选：领域×任务*

> 📄 **摘要**：This living scoping review maps what the recent and current literature (2020 to the present) requires, recommends, and reports as the methodological, technical, ethical, and evaluative standards for large-language-model-assisted, human-supervised evidence-centered research. Its primary output is an operating standard for the ERIS/GIS research-intelligence system: a genealogy and historical-research environment in which LLMs operate under recorded provenance, uncertainty labels, and active human review rather than as autonomous truth producers. The review uses a PCC (Population–Concept–Context) framing. Population: researchers, reviewers, genealogists, historians, evidence-synthesis teams, and research users. Concept: LLM, agentic, vision-language, and retrieval support for searching, screening, extraction, appraisal, coding, synthesis, drafting, provenance, and human review, together with the associated standards, evaluation, security, and ethics. Context: human-supervised research-intelligence systems across academic, historical, genealogical, digital-humanities, and research-information settings. Seven sub-questions address (1) methods and reporting standards; (2) where human review must gate and which interaction designs enforce active inspection; (3) evaluation methodology including golden sets, N-run protocols, calibration, and overconfidence; (4) provenance and verification standards (PROV, model cards, datasheets); (5) documented security and trust failure modes (hallucination, prompt injection, overconfidence, lack of scientific skepticism) and controls; (6) when vector RAG versus graph retrieval is appropriate for multi-hop evidence; and (7) AI-transparency reporting per RAISE and related guidance. Reporting follows PRISMA-ScR (scoping reviews), with a PRISMA-S search appendix and an AI-transparency appendix per RAISE v4 (2026), presented in a PRISMA-trAIce-format appendix labeled provisional, with a recorded decision rule for any official PRISMA AI extension published before synthesis. Eligibility uses a tiered date window: baseline 2020 to the search date; pre-2020 records eligible only as standards or foundational evidence; capability claims eligible from 2023; and the AI-transparency/methodology layer searched from 2024 with a re-search at synthesis. Information sources include PubMed/MEDLINE, ACM Digital Library, IEEE Xplore, ACL Anthology, Semantic Scholar, arXiv, Crossref, with the project's 1,139-document local research library as a named grey/reference layer rather than an automatic inclusion set. Screening follows the project's Candidate-Review Methodology (coverage ledger, fresh-disprover verifier, structured verdicts), with AI used only for prioritization or quality control, a defined subset of decisions human-checked, and discrepancies resolved by consensus. Extraction is checked against a golden set as a regression gate, with confidence reported as unmeasured/uncalibrated rather than as a fabricated quality signal. Appraisal uses the Mixed Methods Appraisal Tool and JBI checklists per design class; no single collapsed score is calculated, and no meta-analysis is performed on the heterogeneous corpus. The review is registered as living: searches are re-run every six months, each update logged with dates and version, findings are date-stamped as current through a stated date, a synthesis refresh is performed annually, and a stop rule transitions the review to a static dated snapshot after three consecutive updates with no substantive change. Protocol deviations will be recorded in a dated deviations log, not by rewriting the registration. Author of record: Brian Reagan (sole author). Substantial LLM assistance was used for drafting, screening support, and extraction under human direction and verification; no AI system is listed as an author; retrieved and machine-output content is treated as untrusted data and never as instructions. ### ⭐⭐⭐ AutoScaffold: AI-Guided Scaffolding for Reinforcement Learning of Language Models
**6/10** · Zijian Zhang、Rizhen Hu、Jiangweizhi Peng、Shiyang Li、Sujay Bhatt、Sihan Zeng · arXiv (Cornell University) · 2026-11-10 · [原文](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2611.14515)
*入选：RL×LLM方法*

> 📄 **摘要**：Reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR) produces rich training experience, yet outcome rewards reduce each attempt to a single number and leave most of this information unused. Training-time scaffolds, such as strategies or hints added to the prompt during training, offer a natural way to turn this information into guidance, but their design and adaptation are largely left to humans and tailored to specific domains. We introduce AutoScaffold, a unified AI-for-AI framework that turns a student's training experience into adaptive scaffolds, with minimal domain-specific information and no human intervention during training. A frozen teacher periodically examines this experience, diagnoses bottlenecks, and designs scaffolds in two forms: (i) strategies shared across a class of tasks and (ii) hints for individual hard tasks, adjusting the content and amount of support as the student learns. The scaffolds are removed at evaluation. We apply the same framework to ALFWorld, search-based QA, mathematical reasoning, and GPU kernel generation. When evaluated without scaffolds, students trained with AutoScaffold outperform those trained with the corresponding RL baselines in all four domains, with gains of 7.2 points in ALFWorld success and 4.0 points in average QA exact match. On GPU kernel generation, AutoScaffold increases the fraction of tasks with a correct kernel that runs faster than the PyTorch reference by up to 5.0 points. On math, our student surpasses QuestA's reported accuracy using approximately one-third as many student training steps. Analyses show that the teacher identifies a different failure in each task class and shifts its scaffolds from step-by-step workflows to targeted fixes as the student improves, and that its revised scaffolds help the student learn more than keeping the previous scaffold or using no scaffold.

**一句话**：提出 AutoScaffold 框架：用冻结教师模型周期性诊断学生 RLVR 训练经验中的瓶颈，自动生成任务类共享策略与难题级提示作为训练脚手架，评测时撤除脚手架。
