### 🔥 ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Google Earth AI Geospatial Reasoning Agent: Capabilities and a Systematic Evaluation
**9/10** · Noam Slonim、Aviv Slobodkin、Gil Fidel、Gal Moshitch、Mimi Sun、Lily Mihalkova · arXiv (Cornell University) · 2026-10-11 · [原文](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2610.15750)
*入选：领域×任务*

> 📄 **摘要**：Google Earth AI is a family of geospatial AI models, together with an agent that orchestrates them. We present this agent, Google Earth AI's Geospatial Reasoning agent (GRA), a Gemini-powered research system that combines geospatial data sources, foundation models, and tools to answer questions about our planet. GRA organizes these into modular domain skills and pairs them with upfront geospatial planning to decompose complex queries into multi-stage workflows. Evaluating such systems is hard: real-world problems admit multiple valid workflows, require data discovery across large catalogs, and rely on evolving ground truth. We introduce two benchmarks and release their development splits. GeoExpertQueries poses complex analytical problems grounded in practitioner use cases and refined by geospatial experts; each is paired with a consensus golden workflow from three independent experts and scored by a human-validated, tool-agnostic autorater. GeoKTools consists of templated queries with deterministic reference solvers that systematically vary the number of required tool types. We also use two external benchmarks, CBGB and UnivEARTH; the latter we find to be saturated. To isolate the contribution of GRA's tools and its agentic orchestration, we compare four configurations, each with Gemini 3.1 Pro and 3.8 Flash: a standalone LLM, the LLM with basic tools, the LLM with GRA's skills and tools but without its upfront planning and execution guidance, and GRA itself. With at most basic tools, an advanced model recovers much of the answer on templated queries but satisfies only a small fraction of the expert workflows on open-ended problems; GRA performs well on both. The largest step comes from GRA's skills and tools; on open-ended problems, its planning and execution guidance add a further gain with Gemini 3.8 Flash, letting Flash match or exceed Pro at lower cost.

**一句话**：Google 提出 Gemini 驱动的地理空间推理 agent（GRA），用上游地理空间规划把复杂查询分解为多阶段工作流并编排地理数据源/基础模型/工具，同时发布两个工作流级评测基准。

痛点在于真实地理空间问题存在多条合法工作流、需要跨大规模数据目录做发现、ground truth 不断演化，导致此类系统难以评测。方法上 GRA 把数据源、基础模型与工具组织为模块化领域技能，并配以前置（upfront）地理空间规划完成复杂查询的多阶段任务分解与执行；配套 GeoExpertQueries（专家打磨问题 + 三位专家共识 golden workflow + 人工验证的工具无关 autorater）与 GeoKTools（模板化查询 + 确定性参考求解器，可控改变所需工具类型数）两个基准。结果通过四种配置（纯 LLM、LLM+基础工具、LLM+GRA skills/tools 但无规划指导、完整 GRA）消融，隔离工具与 agentic orchestration 的贡献，并发现 UnivEARTH 已饱和。

> 🔗 **与课题**：① GIS Task→Workflow：论文的核心机制就是 upfront geospatial planning，把从业者式复杂分析问题分解为 multi-stage workflow，与 Route-2 的"任务分解、步骤规划、依赖组织"直接同构。② Workflow→结构化表示：其"modular domain skills + planning + execution guidance"实际上定义了工作流的阶段化结构表示，可作为结构化生成（DAG/依赖）的参照设计。④ Workflow→Operator 落地：GRA 将规划结果绑定到具体 geospatial data sources / foundation models / tools 并执行，正是 workflow→operator grounding 的地理空间实例。⑦ 评测：GeoExpertQueries 的"三位专家共识 golden workflow + 人工验证 tool-agnostic autorater"与 GeoKTools 的"确定性参考求解器 + 系统变化工具类型数"，为工作流推理质量与工具落地率提供了可迁移的指标与协议。⑥ 训练/后训练：四种配置消融（无工具 / 有工具无规划 / 完整 agent）提供了"规划能力 vs 工具能力"的归因范式，可直接映射到我们判断迁移保持率的实验设计。该文虽未涉及 QGIS 与跨平台迁移，但其问题设定、消融结构与评测口径均落在本研究主轴上，属高相关竞品与对标对象。

💡 **启发**：可直接偷两点：一是"多位专家共识 golden workflow + tool-agnostic autorater"作为工作流级评测协议（比执行成功率更细粒度地度量工作流正确性）；二是"确定性参考求解器 + 系统变化所需工具类型数"的模板化查询设计，用来做可控难度的能力/迁移梯度测试。其四种配置消融也是验证"工作流知识来自规划层还是工具层"的现成实验骨架。

⚠️ **局限**：系统与基准绑定 Google 内部工具链与特定 Gemini 版本、golden workflow 与 autorater 的可复现性受限，且只评测"规划正确性"而非生成的 processes+links 图结构能否真正落地执行（无 QGIS 类落地验证）。

🚨 **为什么必须重视**：与核心科学问题直接撞车：同样是 LLM/agent 在 GIS 场景下做 upfront planning、多阶段工作流分解与工具编排，是 Route-2 最直接的竞品与对标基线。其"有无 upfront planning"的消融设计，正是我们判断"工作流推理能力是否可迁移、是否由规划层承载"的现成实验范式，可直接复用于 GEE→QGIS 的迁移归因。两个基准（专家共识工作流 + 工具无关 autorater；可控工具类型数模板）可立刻借用为评测协议，故须立即精读并纳入对比基线。

### 🔥 ⭐⭐⭐⭐ SEAR: Self-Evolving Agentic Robotics
**9/10** · Xinyi Wang、Bangzheng Li、Hanyu Wang、Lin Zhang、Andrea Yaoyun Cui、Zhenggang Tang · arXiv · 2026-10-13 · [原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18792v1) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.18792v1)
*入选：能力迁移*

> 📄 **摘要**：Frontier language-model agents can now improve robots on their own: they write control code, test it, and keep what works as skills and memories for the next task. We introduce SEAR (Self-Evolving Agentic Robotics), a framework and benchmark built around two questions. Is the agent really learning, or just trying more often? And does what it learns still help on new tasks, in a new simulator, and on a real robot? The framework runs an agent in three modes: it writes code that becomes the policy (code-as-policy), acts live in the control loop (agent-as-policy), or works as a researcher that fine-tunes a vision-language-action model (auto-research). The benchmark adds controls that separate retained experience from repeated attempts, familiar tasks, privileged simulator state, larger budgets, and luck. Across seven frontier language models, 330 simulated and five physical tasks, and over four years of agent runtime, we highlight three findings. First, the learning curve of a robotic agent depends both on the overall time budget and the task difficulty relative to model capability. Harder tasks make longer sessions worthwhile; on easier tasks, re-rolling short attempts does just as well. Second, control code and shared routines transfer across tasks and simulators, while task-specific solutions do not. On a real robot, we observe a performance gap between agent-designed practice and novel human-designed scenes. Third, different models evolve more effectively in different ways. Some do best acting live, others writing code first. And a text-only model keeps pace with a comparable multimodal one: perception helps with error assessment, but agentic reasoning and tool use can matter more. We release the benchmark and codebase to support future research.

**一句话**：提出 SEAR 自演化智能体框架与基准，让语言模型智能体自主编写控制代码、实时充当策略或微调 VLA 模型，并在受控条件下检验'是否真在学、学到的东西能否迁移'。

痛点：前沿 LM 智能体宣称能自行改进机器人，但难以区分'真正学习'与'反复试错+运气'，也不清楚所学能否迁移到新任务、新仿真器和真机。方法：SEAR 提供三种运行模式（code-as-policy 写代码即策略、agent-as-policy 进入控制回路、auto-research 微调 VLA），并配套基准用控制变量分离保留经验、重复尝试、熟悉任务、特权仿真状态、更大预算与运气。结果：在 7 个前沿模型、330 个仿真任务与 5 个真机任务、累计四年智能体运行时长上发现——学习曲线取决于总时间预算与相对模型能力的任务难度；控制代码与共享例程可跨任务、跨仿真器迁移，而任务特定解法不迁移；真机上由智能体自行设计的练习场景与人类新场景之间存在性能落差。

> 🔗 **与课题**：本文是一篇在 LLM/基础模型上研究'可学习的工作流/程序性知识能否跨环境迁移'的实证工作，与 Route-2 的多个环节直接对应。①Workflow 推理：agent 需自主分解机器人任务、规划写码-测试-保留技能的循环，正是任务分解与流程规划。②结构化表示：code-as-policy 产出的控制代码是可执行的结构化程序，与 QGIS processes+links 图同属'生成可执行、依赖闭合的结构'。③能力迁移（核心命中）：论文明确区分'控制代码与共享例程跨任务、跨仿真器迁移'与'任务特定方案不迁移'，这正是我们从 GEE workflow 迁移到 QGIS operator grounding 所需验证的迁移保持率问题，且给出了迁移/不迁移的判别结论。④落地/grounding：智能体编写的代码要真正进入控制回路并在真机落地，与 workflow 落地为具体 QGIS operator 的 grounding 过程同构。⑦评测：基准用控制变量把'保留经验'从'重复尝试、熟悉任务、特权状态、大预算、运气'中剥离出来——这几乎就是为'迁移保持率'设计的一套协议，可直接迁移到我们的评测设计中。⑥训练/后训练：auto-research 模式用智能体自主微调 VLA，是 DAPT/SFT 式能力内化的一个可借鉴范式。

💡 **启发**：最可直接偷的是它的评测控制变量设计：用'保留经验 vs 重复尝试/更大预算/特权状态/运气'做消融，来证明迁移不是试错堆出来的；可照搬到 QGIS 落地评测中，用同预算/同尝试次数对照来量化 GEE→QGIS 的净迁移增益。另外'区分可迁移的共享例程与不可迁移的任务特定解法'这一分析口径，可直接用于诊断我们迁移失败是出在通用工作流层还是算子绑定层。

⚠️ **局限**：研究对象是机器人控制而非 GIS/桌面 GIS 算子，迁移结论建立在机器人动作与仿真器语境，跨到 QGIS 这种符号化算子图上时需重新验证'共享例程'的定义是否成立。

🚨 **为什么必须重视**：它直接命中我们的核心科学问题：'在源域学到的程序性/工作流知识能否不经显式中间语义层迁移到目标平台'，且给出了跨任务、跨仿真器迁移的正面与负面证据，是最接近的竞品式同行工作。更重要的是它提供了把'真迁移'与'试错运气'分离的评测协议，正是我们 Route-2 中 transfer retention 与执行成功率指标目前最缺的方法学支撑。若不尽快精读，可能在评测设计上被其抢先占位。

**▸ 建议：精读**

### 🔥 ⭐⭐⭐⭐ From Relevance to Validity: Zero-Shot Tool Selection via Functional and Contextual Validation
**8/10** · Xiaoxuan Dong、Jin Zhang、Zichen Yuan、Yue Zhou、Zihao Rao、Dexu Yu · arXiv · 2026-10-14 · [原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20388v1) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.20388v1)
*入选：能力迁移*

> 📄 **摘要**：Large language model (LLM) agents increasingly face tasks from unseen domains and execution contexts, making zero-shot tool selection essential for identifying tools that can satisfy task requirements. Existing dominant methods mainly rely on relevance ranking, but semantic matching alone cannot ensure that a tool is functionally compatible or remains applicable in a new context. We preliminarily identify a \emph{transfer-validity gap}, where semantically relevant tools may still fail to satisfy functional or contextual requirements after transfer. To address this gap, we propose \methodname{}, a progressive validation framework that complements semantic relevance with endpoint-grounded functional compatibility and task-conditioned contextual evidence. Across cross-domain and cross-dataset transfer, \methodname{} consistently improves capability ranking, with larger gains under functional or contextual ambiguity. Ablation results further confirm the complementary contributions of functional and contextual validation.

**一句话**：论文提出零样本工具选择框架，用端点级功能兼容性与任务条件上下文验证来弥补语义相关性在跨域/跨数据集迁移中的不足。

### 🔥 ⭐⭐⭐⭐ New Model, Old Habits: Behavior Transfer under Model Swapping in Multi-Session LLM Agents
**8/10** · Youngbin Choi、Sehyun Hwang、Suhyeon Jun、Seoyeon Lee、Sunghyun Choi、MoonJeong Park · arXiv · 2026-10-14 · [原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19712v1) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.19712v1)
*入选：能力迁移*

> 📄 **摘要**：Multi-session agents can switch between LLMs to balance performance and inference cost. Such switches require transferring a history for continuity, yet their effects on the recipient remain underexplored. Through controlled experiments with multi-session tasks, we uncover behavior transfer: recipients tend to adopt reasoning and action patterns from the models that generated their histories. This effect is most evident with raw-history transfer but is substantially reduced by summarization. Based on these findings, we propose Direction- and Authorship-aware Summarization of History (DASH) that preserves stronger-model behavior while minimizing weak-to-strong transfer via summarization. Experiments with repeated swaps show that DASH outperforms raw-history transfer and naive summarization baselines. These results suggest that effective multi-model orchestration requires considering not only which model to use, but also which behavioral patterns to carry forward.

**一句话**：该论文研究多会话 LLM agent 在切换模型时，历史传递会导致接收模型继承历史生成模型的行为模式，并提出 DASH 摘要方法来保留强模型行为、抑制弱到强迁移。

痛点：多会话 agent 为平衡性能与成本会切换 LLM，但历史传递对接收模型行为的影响未被充分研究。方法：通过受控多会话任务实验发现 behavior transfer，即接收模型会采纳历史中强/弱模型的推理与动作模式，原始历史传递最明显、摘要可显著减弱。结果：提出 Direction- and Authorship-aware Summarization of History (DASH)，在重复切换实验中优于原始历史传递和朴素摘要。

> 🔗 **与课题**：该文直接支撑环节③能力迁移：它证明 LLM agent 在跨模型切换时会发生行为迁移，即接收模型从历史中继承 donor 模型的 reasoning/action patterns，这为‘源域→目标域’的行为迁移提供了实证机制。对课题而言，GEE→QGIS 的工作流推理迁移可类比为源轨迹/源行为向目标平台的迁移，且提示迁移可能通过上下文/历史承载而非仅权重参数。环节⑦评测也有对应：behavior transfer 的测量与控制思路可转化为 transfer retention 评测，区分目标能力提升与源模型习惯残留。环节⑥训练/后训练可部分借用：DASH 的方向与作者感知摘要属于推理时适配/历史编排，可启发 SFT/DAPT 中避免弱教师或错误轨迹污染目标能力。环节①工作流推理有弱对应：论文的 action patterns 迁移与 workflow reasoning 模式迁移同构，但未涉及 GIS 任务分解；环节④grounding 仅间接相关，未评估算子落地正确性。

💡 **启发**：可借鉴 DASH 的‘方向与作者感知摘要’思想，在 GEE→QGIS 迁移实验中设计历史/轨迹摘要策略，保留源域 workflow 推理结构而过滤 GEE 平台特定噪声，从而提升 QGIS operator grounding 的迁移保持率。

⚠️ **局限**：论文聚焦多会话 agent 的跨模型历史传递，而非跨平台/跨工具的能力迁移，且未评估结构化图生成、依赖闭合或算子执行成功率。

🚨 **为什么必须重视**：该文把 transfer 问题从权重/参数层面扩展到历史与上下文层面，与课题关注的‘模型内部可迁移工作流知识’形成直接竞争性解释框架，必须立即跟踪。其 DASH 方法可低成本改造为迁移保持率评测中的历史摘要基线，用于判断 GEE→QGIS 迁移到底是目标能力习得还是源平台行为残留。若忽略这种 behavior transfer，后续评测可能把弱模型习惯或历史污染误算为工作流推理迁移成功。

### 🔥 ⭐⭐⭐⭐ XferAgent: Cross-Agent Experience Transfer for Self-Improving LLM Agents
**8/10** · Weihao Li、Jun Bai、Shijie Chen、Qincheng Lu、Xinyu Wang、Ziyang Song · arXiv · 2026-10-14 · [原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19581v1) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.19581v1)
*入选：能力迁移*

> 📄 **摘要**：Large language model (LLM) agents can reuse experience from prior task executions to improve performance on subsequent tasks. ### ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Explorer-Zero: Improving Self-Play for Long-Horizon Tasks with Decomposable Verifiable Rewards
**7/10** · Tianyu Liu、Davide Belli、Bence Major、Arash Behboodi、Tribhuvanesh Orekondy · arXiv · 2026-10-14 · [原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19868v1) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.19868v1)
*入选：RL×LLM方法*

> 📄 **摘要**：Training LLM agents on long-horizon interactive tasks with reinforcement learning typically relies on human-annotated tasks and reliable verification, both expensive to construct for stateful environments. Self-play offers an alternative, but must generate achievable, compositional tasks and provide feedback beyond binary outcomes. We propose Explorer-Zero, a self-play framework that jointly generates tasks and fine-grained, verifiable rewards through environment interaction. A learned proposer explores the environment, constructs tasks, and records intermediate and final states as verification targets. These checkpoints reward partial completion, while alternating proposer--solver training adapts the generated curriculum. We show theoretically that subtask rewards reduce collapse among unsuccessful rollouts when they complete different numbers of subtasks. Without human-annotated training tasks, Explorer-Zero outperforms the evaluated self-play baselines across ALFWorld, ScienceWorld, and AppWorld; on AppWorld, our 30B agent reaches 59.67% task success, approaching 62.65% for RL on human-annotated tasks. Further analyses support task-relevant composition and benefits on complex tasks, while self-play initialization improves subsequent human-data RL across all three benchmarks.

**一句话**：提出 Explorer-Zero 自博弈框架，让 LLM 在环境中自动生成任务并记录中间态作为可验证的子任务奖励，交替训练 proposer 与 solver。

痛点：长程交互式任务上用 RL 训练 LLM agent 依赖人工标注任务和可靠验证，代价高昂且反馈只有二值成败。方法：学习一个 proposer 探索环境、构造组合式任务并把中间态与终态记录为验证目标，用这些 checkpoint 给出细粒度部分完成奖励，并让 proposer–solver 交替训练自适应调整课程；理论证明子任务奖励可减少失败 rollout 的坍缩。结果：无需人工标注即在 ALFWorld、ScienceWorld、AppWorld 上超过自博弈基线，30B agent 在 AppWorld 达 59.67% 成功率，接近人工任务 RL 的 62.65%，且自博弈初始化还能提升后续人工数据 RL。

> 🔗 **与课题**：主要支撑环节⑥（训练/后训练）与①（任务分解/工作流推理）、⑦（评测）；把长程任务拆成带中间态验证点的子任务，等价于为多步流程构造可验证的依赖检查点，其'部分完成奖励'思路可用于评测工作流的结构正确性与依赖闭合程度，也可为 QGIS workflow 的中间步骤自动生成监督信号。属绑定了 tool/agent/环境交互场景的 RL，按校准算高相关储备；但它不涉及跨域/跨平台能力迁移（③）与 operator grounding（④/⑤），因此不到核心 8-10 档。

💡 **启发**：可借用'用环境交互自动产出中间态 checkpoints 作为可验证子奖励'的做法：把 QGIS workflow 的中间图层/要素状态当作子任务验证点，自动生成过程级奖励而非只看最终执行成败；proposer–solver 交替的课程自适应也可用于合成由易到难的 GIS 工作流任务。

⚠️ **局限**：评测环境为 ALFWorld/ScienceWorld/AppWorld 的文本交互任务，其'状态'远比 GIS 数据层/算子参数图结构简单，可验证性假设能否迁移到 QGIS 图生成尚待验证。
