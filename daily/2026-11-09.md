### ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Agent-R$^2$: Scaling Agents for Realistic Productivity Work via Real-Source Data Synthesis
**7/10** · Teng Pan、Zixuan Wang、Yuchen Yan、Qifei Wu、Yao Chen、Yingqi Qu · arXiv (Cornell University) · 2026-11-05 · [原文](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2611.08219)
*入选：工作流×工具推理*

> 📄 **摘要**：Large language models have made rapid progress in agentic reasoning and tool use, raising expectations for agents that can take over realistic productivity work from humans. However, current agents still struggle to finish such tasks, where success requires following long-horizon professional workflows, understanding heterogeneous file contexts, and producing usable deliverables under fine-grained requirements. A key bottleneck is the lack of scalable post-training data that reflects real work intent, authentic file environments, and deliverable-level evaluation. To close this gap, we introduce Agent-R$^2$, a $\textbf{r}$eal-source data synthesis framework that scales training data for $\textbf{r}$ealistic productivity work. Instead of relying on manually specified workflows or LLM-generated files, Agent-R$^2$ grounds synthesis throughout the pipeline in real occupational seeds, corpus-derived workflow evidence, and authentic web-sourced files. From 10k web-corpus candidates, Agent-R$^2$ distills 4k task instances that pair real multi-format attachments with fine-grained, deliverable-level rubrics. On this data, we train Agent-R$^2$-4B via supervised fine-tuning and rubric-based reinforcement learning, improving both end-to-end productivity performance and file-grounded reasoning. Trained on only 4k instances, Agent-R$^2$-4B surpasses Qwen3-235B-A22B-Thinking-2507 on both GDPval (51.0$\%$ vs. 48.0$\%$) and OfficeQA (14.8$\%$ vs. 13.8$\%$). Further analysis shows that Agent-R$^2$ mitigates shortcut behavior, where agents produce superficially plausible deliverables before sufficiently grounding, constructing, or verifying them, while promoting sustained exploration.

**一句话**：提出 Agent-R² 真实来源数据合成框架：从真实职业语料、工作流证据与网络真实文件中蒸馏出 4k 带交付级评分细则的任务实例，并用 SFT + rubric-based RL 训练 4B 智能体。

痛点在于现有智能体难以完成长时程真实生产力工作，关键瓶颈是缺少能反映真实工作意图、真实多格式文件环境与交付级评测的可扩展后训练数据。方法上以真实职业种子、语料反推的工作流证据和真实网页文件贯穿合成流程，从 1 万候选中蒸馏 4k 任务实例，配套细粒度 rubric，再做 SFT 与基于 rubric 的强化学习。结果 Agent-R²-4B 仅用 4k 数据即在 GDPval（51.0% vs 48.0%）和 OfficeQA（14.8% vs 13.8%）上超过 Qwen3-235B-A22B-Thinking-2507。

### ⭐⭐⭐⭐ ModalSearcher: Weaving Visual Evidence into Autonomous Cross-Modal Deep Search
**7/10** · Zhihan Yin、Ruyi Li、Chengyu Fang、Yifeng Yao、Nonghai Zhang、Pengfei Xiong · arXiv (Cornell University) · 2026-11-04 · [原文](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2611.06131)
*入选：RL×LLM方法*

> 📄 **摘要**：Static and incomplete parametric knowledge limits the performance of multimodal large language models on novel-knowledge and knowledge-intensive tasks, especially those requiring multi-hop evidence acquisition. Existing multimodal agentic search methods often fix visual evidence at the input or a predefined trajectory position, making it difficult for agents to select search modalities as their information needs evolve and to handle complex real-world queries. We define this capability as autonomous cross-modal search: an agent chooses among direct reasoning, text-to-text search, text-to-image search, and reverse-image search, actively introduces visual evidence when appropriate, and switches between and integrates modalities across multiple hops. We propose ModalSearch-Pipeline, a scalable procedure for constructing an interleaved multimodal knowledge graph. It samples paths with diverse depths and modality compositions and uses backward question construction to obscure entity names and image targets that would expose reasoning shortcuts while retaining necessary disambiguating clues, yielding text-only, image-initialized, and arbitrarily interleaved multi-hop problems. We train the ModalSearcher Agent with supervised fine-tuning and reinforcement learning in a live Web environment, and construct ModalSearch-Bench. Experiments show that ModalSearcher-8B improves average accuracy by 16.2 points over Qwen3-VL-8B under the same agentic workflow across five multimodal knowledge-intensive QA benchmarks, with consistent gains on independently constructed active visual-search benchmarks.

**一句话**：构建多模态知识图谱路径采样+反向问题构造的流水线，训练能在推理、文搜、图搜、反图搜之间自主切换的多跳跨模态搜索智能体（SFT+RL），并发布 ModalSearch-Bench。

痛点：多模态大模型的参数知识静态且不完整，现有 agentic search 把视觉证据固定在输入或预设轨迹位置，无法随信息需求演化自主选择检索模态。方法：提出 ModalSearch-Pipeline，从交错多模态知识图谱中采样不同深度与模态组合的路径，用反向问题构造遮蔽实体名/图像目标以避免推理捷径，生成纯文本、图像初始化、任意交错的多跳问题；再在真实 Web 环境用 SFT+RL 训练 ModalSearcher Agent 并建 benchmark。结果：ModalSearcher-8B 在相同 agentic workflow 下平均准确率比 Qwen3-VL-8B 高 16.2 分。

> 🔗 **与课题**：支撑环节①⑥⑦，部分触及③但不足 8 分档。①：多跳证据获取本质上要求智能体做步骤规划与信息需求分解（何时切模态、下一跳查什么），是可迁移的 workflow reasoning 实例，但其规划未显式输出结构化依赖图。⑥：SFT+RL 在 live Web 环境中联合训练工具选择策略，属于绑定 tool 场景的 RL（非纯算法改进），可作为训练手段参考。⑦：给出跨模态多跳搜索的评测协议与 benchmark 构造范式（深度/模态组合可控）。③：仅在同模型内部的跨模态工具切换上体现，未做源域→目标域的迁移保持率实证，不构成能力迁移研究。

💡 **启发**：“反向问题构造”把会暴露推理捷径的实体名与图像目标遮蔽、只留必要消歧线索——这套数据合成思路可直接搬来构造 GEE→QGIS 迁移训练集，强制模型走完整 workflow 而非靠平台线索猜答案；按深度/模态组合采样路径的做法也可类比为按依赖闭合度分级采样工作流图。

⚠️ **局限**：评测指标是下游 QA 准确率，不度量工作流结构正确性、依赖闭合与算子落地可用率，且无跨平台迁移保持的量化设计。
