### ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Your Vision Foundation Model Is Secretly an EEG Foundation Model
**7/10** · Guoan Wang、Feng Liu · arXiv (Cornell University) · 2026-11-12 · [原文](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2611.17031)
*入选：能力迁移*

> 📄 **摘要**：Electroencephalography (EEG) foundation models are pretrained on thousands of hours of recordings, yet their frozen features transfer poorly to downstream tasks, and this transfer does not consistently improve with larger models or more pretraining data. We show that a vision foundation model that has never seen EEG is a stronger frozen EEG encoder than these specialized models, once the signals are rendered as images that a vision transformer can read. Our method, SeeEEG, keeps the vision backbone frozen and learns only how EEG is displayed to it. Each channel occupies a dedicated row of patches and is drawn either as a waveform at a fixed gain or as a scalogram whose frequency range spans the patch height, so that task-relevant patterns fall within individual tokens. The montage, filtering, and contrast parameters of this display are initialized from a standard clinical montage and optimized with the task loss through the frozen backbone, which amounts to at most 1,113 trainable parameters per view. We compare SeeEEG with seven EEG foundation models on eight clinical, sleep, affective, and brain-computer interface datasets, with every encoder frozen and paired with identical readout heads. With a frozen DINOv3 ViT-B, SeeEEG outperforms every EEG foundation model in balanced accuracy on all eight datasets and reaches a mean rank of 1.3 in Cohen kappa or AUROC, compared with 2.9 for the strongest baseline, REVE. The learned displays transfer without further optimization to the video encoder V-JEPA 2.1, which outperforms every EEG foundation model on both metrics on six of the eight datasets. Replacing DINOv3 with a randomly initialized ViT-B lowers the score by 15.2 points on average, so the ability to read rendered EEG comes from visual pretraining.

**一句话**：该论文把 EEG 信号渲染成图像后输入冻结的视觉基础模型 DINOv3，只学习少量“显示参数”（每个视图不超过 1113 个可训练参数），让视觉基础模型成为比专用 EEG 基础模型更强的冻结编码器。

痛点：EEG 基础模型虽用数千小时数据预训练，但冻结特征在下游任务上迁移效果差，而且扩大模型或增加数据也不能稳定改善迁移。方法：SeeEEG 不改动视觉骨干，只优化 EEG 如何“呈现”给 ViT，具体包括通道占一行 patch、波形或尺度图渲染、montage、滤波和对比度参数；这些参数从临床标准 montage 初始化，并通过冻结骨干用任务损失优化。结果：在 8 个临床、睡眠、情感和 BCI 数据集上，冻结 DINOv3 ViT-B 的 SeeEEG 全部数据集 balanced accuracy 超过七个 EEG 基础模型，平均排名 1.3（最强基线 REVE 为 2.9）；学到的显示参数无需再优化即可迁移。

> 🔗 **与课题**：支撑环节③（能力迁移：源域→目标域）——该论文正面研究“预训练源域（自然图像）的冻结表征能否迁移到差异极大的目标域（EEG）”，并给出反直觉结论：迁移瓶颈不在模型规模或预训练数据量，而在输入接口（display）是否与骨干匹配；这对课题中“GEE 学到的工作流知识能否迁移到 QGIS grounding”的『迁移保持率』问题有直接类比价值。环节⑥（训练/适配手段）：只训练每个视图不超过 1113 个参数的输入侧适配，冻结骨干，可视为 PEFT 思想在输入层的极端形态，作为“轻量适配保迁移”的候选方案。环节⑦（评测）：所有编码器冻结加完全相同 readout head 的公平对比协议、以及“学到的适配不再优化直接跨数据集使用”的迁移保持测试，可直接借用为 transfer retention 的评测设计。注意：它迁移的是感知表征而非工作流推理/工具落地能力，全文无 workflow、tool、结构化生成内容，故不到 8 分。

💡 **启发**：“把迁移问题转化为接口/渲染问题，而不是改模型”这一思路：可考虑为 QGIS 侧设计一个轻量“算子呈现层”（把 operator schema/参数空间渲染成模型更易读的形式），只训练这一层而冻结主干，用来检验 grounding 增益是否来自接口而非重训。另外借鉴其公平协议：冻结编码器加相同解码头，并在新域零再优化测试，作为 transfer retention 的标准做法。

⚠️ **局限**：该论文属于跨模态表征迁移的实证，不涉及推理技能或工具调用能力的迁移，且每数据集都需针对任务优化 display 参数，其“零再优化迁移”仅在同一显示参数下成立，外推到符号化工作流空间需谨慎。

**5/10** · Lindong Xie、Yang Zhang、Beiyu Song、Zeren Xing、Flora D. Salim、Edward Chung · arXiv · 2026-11-15 · [原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19951v1) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.19951v1)

> 📄 **摘要**：Traffic signal control (TSC) is critical for mitigating urban congestion. ### ⭐⭐⭐ Where Should LLM Agents Adapt at Test Time? In Context, in Weights, or Both
**6/10** · Parsa Mirtaheri、Mohammadreza Armandpour、Keivan Alizadeh、Iman Mirzadeh、Parshin Shojaee、Aryo Lotfi · arXiv · 2026-11-15 · [原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19700v1) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.19700v1)
*入选：RL×LLM方法*

> 📄 **摘要**：LLM agents operating repeatedly in the same environment, such as a code repository, a database, or an application suite, encounter tasks that share latent structure. Experience can reveal this structure, allowing agents to reuse environment knowledge and learn from environmental feedback. Agents can adapt at test-time using this experience through two channels: retaining it in context and updating their weights. These channels are usually studied separately, leaving unclear what each contributes and when they complement each other. We compare in-context learning (ICL), reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards using GRPO, and self-distillation using SDPO, alone and in combination, using a shared online protocol and modular framework. Agents encounter matched task streams, are evaluated on each task before adapting to it, and learn only from their own attempts and environment feedback. We evaluate 14 adaptation mechanisms and a stateless baseline with Qwen3.5-9B on 109 tasks from four environments drawn from SWE-bench Verified, AppWorld, ScienceWorld, and BIRD-Critic-SQLite, using five task orders per environment. ICL provides the largest standalone gain: its best policy raises mean success across environments from 28.8% to 49.4%. Adding GRPO to this policy achieves the highest mean success, 51.6%. While adding GRPO improves the overall mean under every ICL policy, SDPO has mixed effects. Trace analyses show fewer lookups and shorter attempts under ICL, consistent with reusing knowledge of the shared structure. However, ICL also frequently skips inspection and verification for the current task. Adding GRPO to ICL helps restore these behaviors while preserving its reduced environment lookups.
