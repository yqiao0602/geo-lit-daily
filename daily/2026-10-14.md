### ⭐⭐⭐ Agent-assisted modeling for urban drainage systems
**6/10** · Meagan M Tobias、Jacob Marchionda、Branko Kerkez · Digital Water · 2026-10-11 · [原文](https://doi.org/10.1080/28375807.2026.2745819)
*入选：能力迁移*

> 📄 **摘要**：Recent advances in large language models (LLMs) offer new opportunities to make water management more intuitive through natural language interfaces. However, the generalised training of these models limits their effectiveness at domain-specific tasks such as stormwater modelling, particularly when nuanced answers require executing and modifying water models. The Model Context Protocol (MCP) addresses this challenge by providing a standardised interface for LLMs to interact with external tools while enforcing guardrails for quality control. Building on this protocol, we introduce SWMM-MCP, a cross-platform, model agnostic framework that enables conversational interaction with the United States Environmental Protection Agency’s Stormwater Management Model (EPA SWMM). SWMM-MCP leverages the swmm_api and PySWMM libraries through an MCP server built with FAST-MCP, allowing users to configure, modify, and evaluate SWMM models via natural language. We demonstrate SWMM-MCP using a benchmark task and prompt-response evaluations comparing performance using eight different base LLMs. For the benchmark SWMM task, all base LLMs completed the tasks, but at varying levels of computational efficiency and detailed responses. For the prompt-response evaluations, the LLMs exhibited distinct personalities. Results highlight that even within a structured MCP framework, LLM selection significantly affects task efficiency and domain specific prompting remains important for optimal performance.

**一句话**：提出 SWMM-MCP，一个基于 Model Context Protocol 的模型无关框架，让 LLM 通过自然语言配置、修改并评估 EPA SWMM 城市排水模型。

痛点：LLM 的通用训练使其难以胜任需实际执行与修改模型的领域任务（如雨洪建模），且缺少质量控制。方法：用 FAST-MCP 搭建 MCP server，封装 swmm_api 与 PySWMM，把 SWMM 建模能力暴露为标准化工具接口并加护栏。结果：8 个基座 LLM 在基准 SWMM 任务上均能完成，但计算效率与回答详细度差异明显，模型选择与领域提示对性能影响显著。

> 🔗 **与课题**：主要支撑环节④ grounding：把外部仿真模型（SWMM）的算子/操作通过 MCP 标准化暴露给 LLM，使自然语言指令能落地为可执行、受护栏约束的工具调用，与课题中「Workflow→QGIS Operator 落地」在机制上同构。其次支撑环节⑦评测：对 8 个基座 LLM 做基准任务与 prompt-response 对比，提供了「多模型 × 任务效率」的评估协议模板。环节②结构化表示有很弱的间接关联（对模型配置结构做修改），但不涉及工作流推理①、跨域/跨平台能力迁移③与 processes+links 图生成⑤。

💡 **启发**：可把 QGIS processing algorithms 注册为 MCP tools，在协议层做参数 schema 校验与幻觉算子拦截（guardrails）；多基座 LLM 的任务效率对比范式可直接借为 QGIS 落地可用率/执行成功率的评测骨架。

⚠️ **局限**：仅限 SWMM 单一水建模域，没有工作流分解、DAG/图结构生成或迁移保持率实验，评测指标偏定性（效率、回答粒度、模型个性），难以直接迁移结论到 QGIS 算子空间。

### ⭐⭐⭐ LLM-Based IFC Mapping and Automatic IDS Generation for Supporting Automated Code Compliance
**6/10** · Sherief Ali、Markus König · Proceedings of Digital Frontiers in Buildings and Infrastructure International Conference Series · 2026-10-12 · [原文](https://doi.org/10.66408/dfbi.2026.26308)
*入选：领域×任务*

> 📄 **摘要**：Building regulations are typically expressed in natural language, which makes their interpretation heavily dependent on the manual work of domain experts. Practitioners must read regulatory clauses, identify the relevant requirements, and convert them into rule sets for BIM-based compliance checking. This process is time-consuming, error-prone, and difficult to scale across different regulatory domains. Although recent research has begun to automate parts of this workflow, the subsequent steps of integrating regulatory information into BIM processes and generating machine-readable rules still rely largely on manual effort. This paper introduces a two-stage approach that automates these remaining tasks using large language models (LLMs) through the Gemini API. The method operates on regulatory clauses that have already been semantically tagged with objects, properties, qualities, and values, providing a structured representation of the text from which the two stages proceed. In the first stage, the LLM performs schema-aware reasoning over a knowledge base of Industry Foundation Classes (IFC) 4.3 definitions to map each tagged element to its corresponding IFC class, property set, or attribute. In the second stage, these mappings are used to automatically generate Information Delivery Specifications (IDS) that encode the relevant attribute-based regulatory requirements. The workflow is validated using clauses from the International Building Code (IBC), with the generated IDS executed in Solibri to assess correctness and practical applicability. The results demonstrate that LLM-driven semantic mapping, combined with automated IDS generation, significantly enhances the consistency and scalability of digital compliance processes, providing a practical approach to integrating regulatory knowledge into openBIM environments.

**一句话**：该论文用 LLM 在 IFC 4.3 知识库上做 schema 感知映射，并据此前向自动生成可执行的 IDS 合规规则集。

建筑法规以自然语言表述，把它人工转成 BIM 可校验的规则集耗时、易错、难跨规范扩展，且既有自动化工作只覆盖流程前段。作者提出两阶段方法：先让 LLM 对已语义标注（对象/属性/质量/取值）的条款做 schema-aware 推理，映射到 IFC 类、属性集或属性；再基于映射自动生成编码属性要求的 IDS。用国际建筑规范（IBC）条款验证，生成的 IDS 可直接用于自动化合规检查。

> 🔗 **与课题**：主要支撑④（工具/算子落地，此处表现为把语义单元接地到受控 schema 的 IFC 类/属性集）与⑤（生成机器可读的结构化产物 IDS），并部分支撑②（从语义表示到结构化规格的转换）；但它没有任务分解/步骤规划（①），不涉及跨平台/跨工具能力迁移（③），生成的 IDS 也不是带依赖闭合的 processes+links 图（⑤只是弱形式），因此属于方法层面的旁证而非核心问题的直接研究。可类比：受控 schema 知识库 + LLM 映射 + 下游可执行产物，正是我们 Route-2 去掉 workflow 层后的骨架。

💡 **启发**：可借鉴其“先注入受控 schema 知识库，再做 schema-aware 映射”的两段式接地范式，以及用下游真正可执行产物（IDS/校验规则）作为端到端有效性证据的评测思路——正对应我们要的 QGIS 落地可用率与执行成功率指标。

⚠️ **局限**：输入条款已由人工完成语义标注，接地难度被人为降低；且仅在单一规范（IBC）与单一 schema（IFC 4.3）上验证，规模与跨域泛化性未证。

### ⭐⭐ MoEKD: Advancing Robustness, Performance, and Behavioral Fidelity in Compressed Code Models via Mixture-of-Experts Knowledge Distillation
**5/10** · Md. Abdul Awal · Figshare · 2026-10-12 · [原文](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.34338606)
*入选：能力迁移*

> 📄 **摘要**：Large language models for code have achieved strong performance across diverse software analytics tasks, yet their real-world adoption remains limited by high computational demands, slow inference speeds, significant energy consumption, and environmental impact. Knowledge distillation (KD) offers a practical solution by transferring knowledge from a large model (teacher) to a smaller and more efficient model (student). Despite its effectiveness, recent studies show that models distilled from a single teacher often exhibit degraded adversarial robustness, even when robustness-aware distillation techniques are employed. These observations suggest a fundamental limitation of single-teacher distillation in simultaneously transferring high-quality and robust knowledge. To overcome this limitation, we propose Mixture of Experts Knowledge Distillation (MoEKD), a KD framework that leverages a Mixture of Experts (MoE) architecture to enable more effective and robust knowledge transfer from multiple specialized experts into a compact model. MoEKD decomposes the distillation process into expert and router training, aggregation of expert knowledge through a learned routing mechanism, and distillation from the aggregated knowledge. We evaluate MoEKD on the vulnerability detection task using CodeBERT and GraphCodeBERT models. Experimental results show that MoEKD not only improves adversarial robustness by up to 35.8%, but also enhances predictive performance by up to 13%, compared to state-of-the-art KD baselines, including Compressor and AVATAR. Moreover, MoEKD-based students exhibit greater behavioral fidelity to their teachers, indicating better preservation of the teacher's predictive behavior after distillation. Deployment efficiency and environmental sustainability evaluations further show that MoEKD-based students maintain deployment costs comparable to their single-teacher counterparts, with slightly lower inference latency, energy consumption, and carbon emissions observed in most CPU and GPU settings. Our ablation analyses show that MoEKD retains its performance and robustness advantages under more aggressive student compression, while lightweight heterogeneous experts substantially reduce offline training costs and largely preserve its performance gains, but exhibit reduced adversarial robustness. The router analysis further shows that top-2 expert selection mitigates top-1 routing errors, while routing quality influences both the aggregated knowledge and the resulting student performance. Overall, these findings highlight the effectiveness of multi-expert knowledge aggregation in addressing key limitations of existing single-teacher KD approaches.

**一句话**：提出 MoEKD——用混合专家(MoE)架构做多专家知识蒸馏，把多个专门化教师的知识经路由聚合后蒸馏进一个紧凑代码模型，在漏洞检测任务上同时提升对抗鲁棒性与预测性能。

痛点：单教师知识蒸馏虽能压缩代码大模型，却常导致学生模型对抗鲁棒性下降，难以同时兼顾质量与鲁棒知识迁移。方法：MoEKD 把蒸馏拆成专家训练、路由训练、路由聚合专家知识、再从聚合知识蒸馏三步，用 MoE 结构替代单一教师。结果：在 CodeBERT/GraphCodeBERT 的漏洞检测任务上，对抗鲁棒性最高提升 35.8%、预测性能最高提升 13%。

> 🔗 **与课题**：主要对应环节⑥（训练/后训练）——它是蒸馏这一模型压缩/知识迁移手段的方法创新，可作为"把大模型能力压进小模型"的候选实现框架；同时与环节③有弱关联，因为它讨论的是教师→学生的知识迁移保真度（behavioral fidelity）与鲁棒性保持，可类比迁移保持率(transfer retention)的度量思路。但它迁移的是同任务内的代码语义知识，而非跨域/跨平台/跨工具（GEE→QGIS、tool→tool、NL→API）的能力迁移，也不涉及工作流推理、算子落地或结构化图生成，因此不触及①②④⑤⑦核心环节。

💡 **启发**：可借鉴其"多专家+学习式路由聚合后再蒸馏"的结构，设计多源（多平台/多工具）工作流知识蒸馏：让 GEE 侧、QGIS 侧、通用 code 侧的教师各自专精，再用路由聚合迁移到统一学生模型；其"鲁棒性—性能"双指标同时评估的做法，也可映射为迁移保持率与落地可用率的联合评测。

⚠️ **局限**：仅在代码漏洞检测这一判别式任务上验证，未涉及生成式/结构化输出，且未证明该方法在跨域迁移场景下同样成立，引用时不宜直接外推到 GIS workflow 场景。

**▸ 建议：略读**

### ⭐⭐⭐ When Faster Decisions Do Not Make Better Agents: An Empirical Study of Jev in Agent Workflows
**6/10** · Yuchen Ling、Shengcheng Yu、Zhenyu Chen、Chunrong Fang · arXiv (Cornell University) · 2026-10-09 · [原文](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2610.13092)
*入选：能力迁移*

> 📄 **摘要**：AI agents depend on repeated decisions to accomplish tasks, with large language models (LLMs) commonly serving as their core decision makers. The recent emergence of Jev offers an opportunity to make these decisions faster and at lower cost. However, it remains unclear whether such local savings can be translated into efficient and reliable task completion. In this paper, we investigate this efficiency transfer across five agents in two representative application settings, embodied tasks in ALFWorld and mobile app use in AndroidWorld. Across 4,500 task executions, we jointly evaluate task effectiveness, decision-level and task-level resource use, and execution behavior. Experimental results show that Jev integration substantially reduces decision cost and latency across all five agents, with task-level gains varying by workflow. Task success remains close to baseline levels on ALFWorld and declines more substantially in two mobile agents. Auxiliary reasoning and additional interactions absorb decision savings, while failed recovery and premature completion limit successful delivery. Mean cost per successful outcome increases in one agent even as spending per attempt falls. These findings highlight the importance of preserving progress, supporting recovery, and verifying completion when integrating efficient decision models into agent workflows. Their practical value should be assessed through successful task delivery, recovery behavior, and resources per delivered outcome.

**一句话**：在 ALFWorld 与 AndroidWorld 上用 4500 次任务执行，实证考察把高效决策模型（Jev）接入五类 Agent 工作流后，决策层的成本/延迟节省能否转化为任务层的成功率与效率。

痛点：Agent 依赖 LLM 反复做决策，用更快更便宜的决策模型能否真正带来端到端任务收益并不清楚。方法：在两类场景（具身 ALFWorld、移动应用 AndroidWorld）的五个 Agent 中系统替换决策模型，联合评测任务有效性、决策级/任务级资源开销与执行行为。结果：决策成本与延迟普遍下降，但任务成功率在 ALFWorld 基本持平、在两个移动 Agent 明显下降；辅助推理与额外交互吞掉节省，失败恢复与过早结束阻碍交付，甚至出现'单次尝试更便宜但单次成功成本更高'的现象。

> 🔗 **与课题**：主要支撑环节⑦（评测）：论文给出了任务级成功交付、恢复行为、单次成功成本（resources per delivered outcome）等指标体系与错误归因方式，可直接迁移为 QGIS 工作流生成中'执行成功率、依赖闭合率、失败恢复率'的评测协议设计参考。次要支撑环节①：把 Agent 的'重复决策—辅助推理—恢复—完成验证'拆成可观测的工作流阶段，与分析 workflow reasoning 中步骤规划的失败模式（提前终止/恢复失败）同构。与③能力迁移只有弱关联——它讨论的是'效率增益跨 Agent/工作流的迁移保持'，而非跨域/跨平台的能力知识迁移，可借用其'迁移后保留率'的量化思路但不能作为迁移实证依据。

💡 **启发**：可借用其'决策级指标 vs 任务级指标'的双层评测范式：在我们评测 GEE→QGIS 迁移时，不能只看单步算子生成的准确率/成本，必须报告'每个成功交付的完整 QGIS 流程的成本与步数'，并把失败恢复失败与过早终止作为独立错误类别统计。

⚠️ **局限**：仅在两个非 GIS 的 Agent 基准（ALFWorld/AndroidWorld）上做黑盒替换实验，未触及模型内部表征，结论的可迁移性依赖 Agent 框架与任务分布相似性。

### ⭐⭐ Mind the Spatial Gap: A Benchmark for Geospatial Reasoning and Constrained Spatial Editing
**5/10** · Zheng Ren、Yanning Wang、Ziheng Huang、Fei Huang、Shiqi Wang、Ziqian Ye · arXiv (Cornell University) · 2026-10-09 · [原文](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2610.12881)
*入选：领域×任务*

> 📄 **摘要**：Understanding and manipulating spatial relations is fundamental to AI systems that reason about physical environments and construct grounded world models. However, existing evaluations of multimodal large language models (MLLMs) for spatial reasoning rely predominantly on static question-answering (QA) over small-scale or synthetic scenes, rarely probing whether models can modify spatial configurations while satisfying explicit geometric constraints. We introduce GeoSpatialBench, a benchmark designed to evaluate an escalating progression of spatial cognition, ranging from spatial relation understanding and geometric reasoning to constrained spatial editing. Grounded in real-world urban data across six geographically diverse cities, GeoSpatialBench evaluates a comprehensive taxonomy of 28 topological, directional, metric, and ordering relations across structured GeoJSON and map image modalities, with ground-truth references and constraints verified via GIS operations. Our empirical evaluation across frontier MLLMs uncovers a stark spatial gap: structured GeoJSON consistently outperforms map images, with multimodal combinations offering no reliable gain over GeoJSON alone, and while models show reasonable competence on qualitative topological and ordering, performance drops on quantitative metric relations. Crucially, editing performance on ordering relations declines relative to QA, and most failed edits violate at least one specified spatial constraint. These results highlight a gap between passively recognizing spatial relations and actively manipulating geometric configurations. GeoSpatialBench provides an open, rigorous diagnostic testbed to benchmark these spatial limitations, laying essential empirical groundwork for physically grounded world models and embodied spatial AI.

**一句话**：提出 GeoSpatialBench 基准，评测多模态大模型从空间关系理解、几何推理到受约束空间编辑的能力。

现有 MLLM 空间推理评测多局限于小规模/合成场景的静态问答，很少考察模型能否在满足显式几何约束的前提下修改空间配置。作者基于六个地理多样化城市的真实城市数据，构建覆盖 28 种拓扑/方向/度量/顺序关系、横跨结构化 GeoJSON 与地图图像两种模态的基准，真值与约束均由 GIS 操作验证。结果显示：结构化 GeoJSON 一致优于地图图像，多模态组合无稳定增益；定性拓扑/顺序关系尚可，度量关系下滑；编辑任务（尤其顺序关系）比问答更差，且多数失败编辑至少违反一项空间约束。

> 🔗 **与课题**：主要支撑⑦评测：它给出了一个可借鉴的评测协议——用真实 GIS 操作验证生成结果是否满足显式约束，并统计约束违反率，这与课题关心的结构正确性与幻觉算子率指标同构。同时对②结构化表示有间接启示：结构化 GeoJSON 优于图像、多模态无增益，提示 workflow/图结构表示应走符号化而非纯视觉路径。但它不涉及①GIS 任务→工作流分解、③跨平台能力迁移（GEE→QGIS）、④operator grounding、⑤processes+links 图生成，研究对象是空间关系认知而非工作流推理，因此属于通用评测方法层面的弱相关，而非核心问题论文。

💡 **启发**：可直接偷两点：一是把生成结果交给真实 GIS 引擎执行、以“约束满足/违反”做自动评测；二是“结构化符号格式 vs 图像格式”的对照实验设计，可用于验证 workflow 表示形式对下游落地的影响。

⚠️ **局限**：聚焦空间关系理解与约束编辑的静态基准，未触及工作流分解、工具调用或跨平台迁移，结论也受所选 MLLM 与数据规模限制。

## 🎲 RL/偏好（储备池）（1 篇）

### ⭐⭐⭐ SOAR: Source-Aware Reinforcement Learning with Precise Credit Assignment for Search Agents
**6/10** · Shengze Huang、Zihao Tang、Jinluan Yang、Yu Xin、Zhenghao Lin、Xiao Liu · arXiv (Cornell University) · 2026-10-09 · [原文](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2610.13157)
*入选：RL×LLM方法*

> 📄 **摘要**：Reinforcement learning (RL) for search agents often suffers from evidence fabrication, a failure mode where agents bypass external information ($E_{ext}$) and fabricate an incorrect answer. We argue that this fabrication arises from an over-reliance on unreliable internal knowledge ($E_{int}$) as shortcuts due to the inherent opacity of reasoning trajectories. To address this, we propose SOAR (SOurce-Aware Reinforcement learning), a paradigm that transforms monolithic reasoning into source-explicit trajectories. SOAR (i) disentangles $E_{ext}$ and $E_{int}$ into parallel, observable streams via a Source-Aware Workflow, and (ii) incorporates Source-Consistent Reward to guide the policy evolution. By making information-source attribution explicit, SOAR enables precise credit assignment between different knowledge streams. Evaluations show that SOAR outperforms baselines on 3B and 7B backbones by 7.3% and 5.4%, while reducing evidence fabrication by 74.8%. These confirm that SOAR significantly improves performance on knowledge-intensive benchmarks while effectively mitigating evidence fabrication, fostering more faithful and grounded reasoning with enhanced robustness.
