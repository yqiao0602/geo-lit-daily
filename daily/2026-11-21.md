### 🔥 ⭐⭐⭐⭐ SiliconGym: A Versatile Reinforcement Learning Environment for Agentic EDA
**8/10** · Hejia Zhang、Sheng Lu、Yitong Ma、Zhongming Yu、Zhijing Wu、Wentao Ni · arXiv · 2026-11-19 · [原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25658v1) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.25658v1)
*入选：能力迁移*

> 📄 **摘要**：Reinforcement learning (RL) with electronic design automation (EDA) feedback is advancing large language models for hardware design and verification. Extending these gains to hardware-engineering agents requires environments that span tasks, preserve state across tool calls, and support large hierarchies alongside parallel rollouts. We present SiliconGym, the first versatile, cross-task and cross-toolchain RL environment for hardware design and verification. By decoupling versioned workspaces, budgeted execution, and acceptance-gated scoring, SiliconGym scales from module-level RTL, testbench, and PPA tasks to multi-round repository repair and full-hierarchy SoC flows. Streaming dispatch overlaps tool checks with generation, finishing a 256-answer RL batch 21.9% faster than native ChipSeek. Using interchangeable open-source and commercial backends, we show that open-source-trained functional correctness transfers cleanly to commercial simulation, while paired netlist evaluation reveals when open-source PPA gains survive commercial synthesis.

**一句话**：提出 SiliconGym——首个面向硬件设计与验证智能体的跨任务、跨工具链强化学习环境，支持带状态工具调用与多层级流程。

现有硬件智能体缺乏跨任务、跨工具链且能在工具调用间保持状态的 RL 环境。SiliconGym 通过解耦版本化工作区、预算化执行和验收门控评分，并配合流式调度，覆盖从模块级 RTL 到 SoC 流程的多层级任务。实验显示 RL 批次更快完成，且开源工具链训练的功能正确性可迁移到商用仿真，成对 netlist 评估可判断 PPA 增益是否在商用综合后保留。
