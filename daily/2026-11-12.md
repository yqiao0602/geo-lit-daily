### ⭐⭐⭐ Are Causal Neuron Circuits Unique and Transferable?
**6/10** · Ömer Tekin Yavuz · arXiv · 2026-11-10 · [原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13885v1) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.13885v1)
*入选：能力迁移*

> 📄 **摘要**：Interpretability demonstrations often show that intervening on a group of neurons changes a model's answer. A recent example removes an 18-neuron group in Qwen3-32B and the model answers "Simba" instead of "Scar" to a two-hop Lion King riddle. Such a demonstration establishes that the group matters, but not how much of it is necessary, whether other groups could do the same, or whether the effect carries over to other inputs. We ask: when intervening on a group of neurons changes a model's answer, how much of that group is actually necessary? For every selected group we measure all 4,096 subsets of 12 MLP neurons. On a two-alternative capital task (Qwen3-0.6B), zero-ablating 12 selected neurons lowers the answer margin by 27% while random neurons barely change it, yet replacing the same neurons with their values from other questions also barely changes it: their activations are nearly constant across questions, and we find no evidence that they carry question-specific content. On two-hop relation chains, patching 12 neurons with activations from the first-hop question shifts the answer to the bridge entity (from Scar to Simba): the greedy answer starts with the bridge in 9/11 chains for Qwen3-1.7B, 9/11 for Llama-3.2-1B and 8/10 for Gemma-3-1B; random neurons never do. The full group is rarely necessary: a few neurons often suffice, and in many chains disjoint subsets each flip the answer on their own, in an exploratory analysis most often when the model is less confident. Group effects transfer to rephrasings of the same chain but are much weaker on other tested chains. We study neuron-level causal interventions and alternative subsets; we do not recover the full connectivity of circuits.

**一句话**：该论文系统测量MLP神经元组对模型答案的必要性、唯一性与跨输入迁移性，通过枚举12个神经元的全部4096个子集，并结合零消融与激活补丁做因果分析。

痛点：以往的可解释性演示只证明“干预某组神经元会改变答案”，却无法说明该组中多少是必要的、是否存在等效替代组、效应能否迁移到其他输入。方法：对每个选中的组枚举12个MLP神经元的全部4096个子集，在首都问答（Qwen3-0.6B）与两跳关系链（Qwen3-1.7B/Llama-3.2-1B/Gemma-3-1B）上做零消融与跨问题激活补丁。结果：随机神经元几乎无影响；把首跳问题的激活补丁到12个神经元上，可把答案从Scar推向桥接实体Simba（9/11、9/11、8/10条链），而随机神经元从不如此；完整组很少必要，少数神经元往往足够，且常存在多个互不相交、各自即可翻转答案的充分子集，这一现象在模型置信度较低时更常见。

> 🔗 **与课题**：支撑环节②与⑦，并间接关联管线中“模型内部可迁移的工作流知识”这一中间环节。②：两跳关系链（首跳→桥接实体→答案）是工作流组合式多步依赖的最小类比，论文刻画了这种组合依赖在模型内部的载体形态（少数神经元即可承载、存在冗余不相交子集）。⑦：论文提出的“必要性/充分性/唯一性”测量范式（子集穷举＋零消融＋激活补丁＋不相交子集冗余度）可作为一种机制层面的评测协议，用来检验“某类工作流知识是否真实、必要且非冗余地存在于模型内部”。注意：其“transferable”仅指同一电路效应能否跨输入复现，属于模型内跨输入，而非课题所需的跨平台/跨工具（GEE→QGIS）能力迁移，因此不进入③的高相关档；也未涉及结构化图生成、算子grounding或训练方法。

💡 **启发**：可借鉴其“必要性—充分性—冗余度”测量设计：若要声称模型内部存在可迁移的工作流知识，不仅要做行为层迁移保持率，还应通过子集消融/激活补丁验证该知识的承载单元是否必要、是否存在多个不相交的等价单元（冗余性会直接影响迁移的鲁棒性与评测解读）。

⚠️ **局限**：实验任务为首都问答与《狮子王》两跳谜题等玩具任务、模型规模在1B级，结论能否外推到大型模型上的多步真实工作流（GIS process 图生成）尚无证据。

**▸ 建议：略读**

### 🔥 ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Beyond Code Translation: Agentic Android-to-HarmonyOS Migration
**8/10** · Yinghang Ma、Wuyang Dai、Song Wang、Filipe Cogo、Zhen Ming、Jiang · arXiv (Cornell University) · 2026-11-08 · [原文](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2611.11192)
*入选：能力迁移*

> 📄 **摘要**：Migrating Android applications to HarmonyOS is an important yet challenging software engineering task. Existing large language model (LLM)-based migration approaches primarily translate code at the method or file level, overlooking project-wide dependencies and workflow coordination. Moreover, ArkTS, the primary language for HarmonyOS, is relatively low-resource, and migrated applications often lack sufficient tests for reliable verification, making end-to-end migration error-prone. We present A2HTrans, an agentic framework for end-to-end Android-to-HarmonyOS application migration. Rather than performing isolated code translation, A2HTrans decomposes applications into functionality-aware migration tasks, prioritizes them through dependency analysis, and orchestrates translation, validation, and iterative repair within a unified execution workflow. To improve migration reliability, A2HTrans employs a tool-augmented verification strategy that combines compiler-based syntax checking with LLM-based semantic validation and automated repair. We evaluate A2HTrans on 14 Android applications spanning diverse sizes and domains using Claude-Sonnet-4.6 as the underlying migration model. Compared with Claude Code, A2HTrans preserves 8.3% more functional completeness, improves behavioral correctness by 8.0%, and reduces migration time by 28.8%. These results demonstrate that structured agentic execution substantially improves the reliability and efficiency of project-level cross-platform software migration.

**一句话**：提出 A2HTrans 智能体框架，把 Android 应用端到端迁移到 HarmonyOS：按功能把应用分解成迁移任务并做依赖分析排序，统一编排翻译、验证与迭代修复，再用编译器加 LLM 的双重验证闭环提升可靠性。

痛点是现有 LLM 跨平台迁移只在方法级或文件级做代码翻译，忽视项目级依赖与工作流协调，ArkTS 低资源且缺少测试，使端到端迁移易错。方法上，该工作把应用切分为功能感知的迁移任务，按依赖关系排序，在统一执行工作流中编排翻译、验证与迭代修复，并结合编译器语法检查、LLM 语义校验与自动修复做工具增强验证。在 14 个 Android 应用上，以 Claude-Sonnet-4.6 为底座，相比 Claude Code，功能完整度提升 8.3%、行为正确率提升 8.0%、迁移时间降低 28.8%，说明结构化智能体执行能显著提高项目级跨平台迁移的可靠性与效率。

> 🔗 **与课题**：与课题高度相关，主要支撑环节③能力迁移（跨平台、跨语言迁移的实证研究：Android→HarmonyOS，源域→目标域，与 GEE→QGIS 的平台迁移结构相同），同时覆盖①（按功能分解任务，并按依赖关系做优先级规划）、②（依赖分析产出带先后约束的任务图与调度顺序）、④⑤（用编译器报错与语义校验把中间产物落到真实目标语言与工具链上，形成验证-修复闭环）、⑦（功能完整度、行为正确率、迁移耗时等迁移保持率类指标）。其中效率最高的一处是其"依赖感知任务排序 + 编译反馈迭代修复"的编排范式，可直接类比 QGIS processes+links 的依赖闭合生成与执行校验。它没有 GIS 词汇但正是我们需要的 LLM 跨平台迁移能力实证研究。

💡 **启发**：可借鉴它把"迁移保持率"拆成功能完整度、行为正确率、迁移代价三类指标，并引入编译器反馈做自动修复闭环——对应 QGIS 侧可用 QGIS 执行/算子存在性校验替代编译器做 hallucinated operator 纠正。

⚠️ **局限**：仅单一底座模型（Claude-Sonnet-4.6）、14 个应用，且依赖编译器可判定的语法错误；语义正确性仍依赖 LLM 自评，验证信号强度与 GIS 场景（无强编译器）不完全可比。

🚨 **为什么必须重视**：这是 LLM 跨平台项目级迁移 + 依赖感知任务分解 + 工具增强验证闭环的直接竞品级工作，其问题设定（源域→目标域的端到端落地可靠性）与 Route-2 的 GEE→QGIS 迁移几乎同构，若不立即精读，可能在评测协议与迁移保持率指标上被抢跑。

**一句话**：该论文与对应的 Zenodo 复现包提出并发布一套测量框架，检验携带任务求解信息的隐状态能否通过训练出的“桥”从冻结源模型迁移到冻结目标模型，并给出五条件对照与预注册置换判据。

⚠️ **局限**：任务为合成任务与 RACE 变体，源与目标均为通用冻结 LM，迁移轴是模型间而非工具或领域间；且该条目本身是论文的复现资产包，方法细节需以正式发表稿件为准。

### 🔥 ⭐⭐⭐⭐ ReTrace: Distilling Tool-Using Agents into Planner-Free Workflows
**8/10** · Kaiyuan Hou、Yang Ping Kuo、Indranil Bhattacharya、Rohith Nama、Arushi Gupta、Carlos D. Castillo · arXiv · 2026-11-10 · [原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14492v1) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.14492v1)
*入选：工作流×工具推理*

> 📄 **摘要**：Business process automation runs the same Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) again and again on new inputs. Large Language Model (LLM) agents can follow an SOP written in natural language, but they call an LLM to choose every step of every run, which adds latency and cost at scale. We present ReTrace, which turns an SOP and a few logged agent runs into a reusable workflow in which code, not an LLM, selects the next step. The agent's reasoning, the explanation it writes with each tool call, often states why it took an action; the tool calls alone do not. ReTrace extracts the condition behind each step, merges these conditions across runs into a compact rule document, and generates an executable workflow from it. Only this last stage depends on the platform, so rule documents can be compiled for different orchestration platforms. On $79$ procedures from our production platform, ReTrace workflows reach $93.8\%$ completion, against $96.8\%$ for the agent, at $24\%$ lower cost per task. On $τ^2$-Bench, they reach $75.8\%$ task success (agent: $80.6\%$) and run in $1.3$s instead of $170$s; three rounds of repair on training failures lift $k{=}2$ workflows from $66.7\%$ to $81.2\%$. On eight SOP-Bench procedures, ReTrace without repair trails few-shot generation ($80.6\%$ vs. $83.6\%$), but repair from labeled training failures lifts it to $87.8\%$ at $0.40$s per task, compared to $85.5\%$ in $46.6$s for the agent.

**一句话**：ReTrace 把 SOP 加上少量 agent 运行日志，蒸馏成由代码而非 LLM 决定下一步的可复用可执行工作流，并能编译到不同编排平台。

痛点：LLM agent 按 SOP 执行业务流程时每一步都要调用 LLM 选动作，规模化后延迟与成本高。方法：从 agent 每次工具调用附带的 reasoning 文本中抽取每一步背后的分支条件，跨多次运行合并成紧凑的 rule document，再由此生成可执行工作流，且只有最后一步编译与平台绑定。结果：生产平台 79 个流程上完成率 93.8%（agent 96.8%），单任务成本降 24%；τ²-Bench 上成功率 75.8% 但耗时从 170s 降到 1.3s；对训练失败样本做三轮修复后 k=2 工作流从 66.7% 提升到 81.2%。

> 🔗 **与课题**：主要支撑 ②Workflow→结构化表示、⑤结构化图生成、④落地，其次是 ③跨平台与 ⑥蒸馏。②：它把散落在多次 agent 运行中的隐式步骤与分支条件显式化为紧凑 rule document，正是'工作流→依赖与条件结构化表示'的一种实现。⑤/④：从 rule document 生成可执行工作流，由代码选择下一步，等价于把流程编译成带依赖与分支的算子图，并在真实编排平台上执行落地。③：仅最后一阶段与平台绑定，rule document 可编译到不同编排平台，属于'平台无关中间表示＋目标平台编译'的移植范式，与 GEE→QGIS 的落地形态同构（但迁移的是工件而非模型能力）。⑥：把 agent 行为蒸馏到 planner-free 执行体，并用训练失败样本做多轮 repair 迭代，是可借鉴的'失败驱动蒸馏'回路。⑦：报告了完成率、任务成功率、单位任务成本与耗时，构成成本-成功率联合评测口径。

💡 **启发**：可直接借鉴两点：一是把'平台无关 rule document + 平台相关编译器'作为中间表示，用于设计 GEE workflow → QGIS processes+links 的两段式管线，从而把迁移难题局部化到编译器；二是从 agent 的 reasoning 文本中反推分支条件（而非仅看 tool call），这正是把模型内部工作流知识外化为可执行图结构的关键提取手段，另加'训练失败样本多轮 repair'作为无标注增广。

⚠️ **局限**：其目标是去 LLM 化的代码化工作流，不考察模型内部能力是否保持与迁移；且依赖高质量运行日志与带标注的失败样本，实验限于企业 SOP 与 τ²-Bench，没有任何算子级 grounding 的验证。

🚨 **为什么必须重视**：它是'从 agent 轨迹中抽取并外化工作流结构、再编译到目标执行平台'的完整竞品级范式，与本课题 Workflow Reasoning→QGIS 图生成的两段式设计正面撞车，但其 rule document 中间层恰好可作为对照基线。其 failure-driven repair 与成本-成功率评测协议可直接搬到 QGIS 落地可用率/幻觉算子率的评测设计中，必须尽快精读以确定差异化定位。

**▸ 建议：精读**

## 🤖 GIS Agent 与工具使用（1 篇）

### ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Toward Autonomous RTL and Synthesis Optimization: Benchmarking and Self-Improving Agents
**7/10** · Wenji Fang、Yao Lu、Jinghua Wang、Zhiyao Xie · arXiv · 2026-11-10 · [原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13713v1) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.13713v1)
*入选：领域×任务*

> 📄 **摘要**：Recent advances in large language models (LLMs) and autonomous agents are creating new opportunities for hardware design optimization. This paper examines three connected directions toward autonomous RTL and synthesis optimization: (1) Realistic benchmarking. We discuss reference-based and open-ended RTL optimization benchmarks that evaluate optimization opportunities under realistic EDA workflows. (2) Tool-grounded RTL optimization. We present Dr. RTL, a multi-agent framework that automates critical-path analysis, RTL rewriting, and implementation-level evaluation, while continuously improving through reusable optimization skills. Evaluated on 20 real-world RTL designs, Dr.~RTL achieves average WNS/TNS improvements of 21\%/17\% with a 6\% area reduction over commercial synthesis. (3) Design-specific synthesis optimization. > 📄 **摘要**：Language-model systems use self-judgment to decide which answers to release, review, or recompute. ### ⭐⭐⭐⭐ SLIC: Reinforcement Fine-Tuning Small LMs for Multi-Turn Analog Circuit Optimization
**7/10** · Yan Xu、Zhengqi Gao、Ching-Yun Ko、Zhang-Wei Hong、Wenjie Lu、Tao Yu · arXiv · 2026-11-10 · [原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13452v1) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.13452v1)
*入选：能力迁移*

> 📄 **摘要**：Analog integrated circuit (IC) design is a multi-turn, simulator-grounded engineering task that demands iterative reasoning over coupled performance tradeoffs, yet proprietary process design kits (PDKs) make hosted large language model workflows difficult to deploy in practical design settings. SLIC pairs structured circuit representations with discretized relative-update actions, domain- and success-gated multiobjective rewards, and Reward-Guided Rollout Steering (RGRS), which turns per-step simulator feedback into signals for both policy learning and online rollout construction in this sparse, highly constrained domain. Trained solely with open source 65 nm CMOS predictive technology models (PTM), SLIC-Random achieves 87.1% in-distribution four-specification Pass@1 within eight turns, surpassing the 763B DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash Think by 48.5 percentage points. Beyond the training distribution, SLIC-Softmax reaches 72.6% Pass@1 on held-out topologies, and the trained policies consistently transfer across five unseen technology nodes without additional fine-tuning. This transfer extends to a commercial TSMC 65 nm CMOS PDK, where SLIC-Random achieves 49.9% held-out-topology Pass@1, demonstrating practical deployment through local inference without transmitting proprietary data to external LLM services. Performance further scales with test-time simulator interactions, reaching up to 83.2% held-out-topology Pass@1 at a 32-turn budget.

**一句话**：提出 SLIC，用强化微调训练 4B 小模型在模拟电路多轮仿真器闭环优化中迭代调整电路参数，并发布含分布内/留出拓扑/跨工艺节点迁移的基准。

> 🔗 **与课题**：主要支撑③能力迁移与⑦评测，兼及⑥训练与④落地：(1) ③：它给出了“源域训练→目标域零样本迁移保持率”的实证范式（65nm 训练、五个未见节点评估），正是我们 GEE→QGIS 迁移保持率（transfer retention）测量的同构实验设计；(2) ④：把模型的每一步动作约束为离散化的相对更新动作（discretized relative-update actions）并直接作用于仿真器，等价于 operator-level grounding，与我们 QGIS operator grounding 的落地形态可比；(3) ②：用结构化电路表示作为中间载体，但没有显式语义中间层，思路与我们“不依赖 L3 语义中间层”的假设一致；(4) ⑥：RFT + 奖励引导 rollout，属于绑定 tool/simulator 场景的强化学习，可作为训练手段候选而非主轴；(5) ⑦：发布带标准化协议（分布内/留出拓扑/跨节点）的基准，是可借的评测协议模板。但迁移仅在同类模拟电路的不同工艺节点内，未覆盖 tool→tool、跨平台，故不到 8 分。

### ⭐⭐ AutoScaffold: AI-Guided Scaffolding for Reinforcement Learning of Language Models
**5/10** · Zijian Zhang、Rizhen Hu、Jiangweizhi Peng、Shiyang Li、Sujay Bhatt、Sihan Zeng · arXiv · 2026-11-10 · [原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14515v1) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.14515v1)
*入选：RL×LLM方法*

> 📄 **摘要**：Reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR) produces rich training experience, yet outcome rewards reduce each attempt to a single number and leave most of this information unused. Training-time scaffolds, such as strategies or hints added to the prompt during training, offer a natural way to turn this information into guidance, but their design and adaptation are largely left to humans and tailored to specific domains. We introduce AutoScaffold, a unified AI-for-AI framework that turns a student's training experience into adaptive scaffolds, with minimal domain-specific information and no human intervention during training. A frozen teacher periodically examines this experience, diagnoses bottlenecks, and designs scaffolds in two forms: (i) strategies shared across a class of tasks and (ii) hints for individual hard tasks, adjusting the content and amount of support as the student learns. On math, our student surpasses QuestA's reported accuracy using approximately one-third as many student training steps. Analyses show that the teacher identifies a different failure in each task class and shifts its scaffolds from step-by-step workflows to targeted fixes as the student improves, and that its revised scaffolds help the student learn more than keeping the previous scaffold or using no scaffold.

> 🔗 **与课题**：主要支撑环节⑥（训练/后训练）：提供一种基于可验证奖励的 RL 训练期引导机制，可视为 workflow reasoning 训练时注入策略提示、再撤除的候选方案。其次与代码生成/agent 场景（GPU kernel、ALFWorld）绑定，属 tool/code 场景下的训练方法，因此高于纯 RL 算法改进档。但不涉及①任务分解、②结构化/DAG 生成、③跨域跨平台迁移、④算子 grounding、⑤图生成，也不提供迁移保持率类指标。

⚠️ **局限**：依赖可验证奖励的可获得性，脚手架仍由教师模型生成、缺少对结构化输出正确性的约束，迁移到无自动验证的 GIS 工作流任务需额外设计奖励与验证器。

### ⭐⭐ Cognitive Mapping of Learning by Thinking Mechanisms in Large Language Models
**5/10** · Dong Han、Jin Zhang、Kunyue Yang · arXiv · 2026-11-10 · [原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14382v1) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.14382v1)
*入选：领域×任务*

> 📄 **摘要**：Large language models (LLMs) increasingly exhibit capabilities that resemble human Learning by Thinking (LbT), the acquisition of new insight through internal processes such as explanation and reasoning, without new external input. This paper proposes a Cognitive LbT Framework that maps four mechanisms (Reflective Reasoning, Synthetic Memory Formation, Internal Simulation, and Conceptual Self-Organization) onto computational processes in LLMs. Drawing on representative methods, including chain-of-thought prompting, self-consistency, self-reflection, Tree-of-Thoughts search, Self-Instruct, and self-taught reasoning, the analysis argues that selected methods can functionally approximate aspects of these mechanisms and can improve performance on some tasks without additional labeled examples, although this does not always constitute durable learning in model parameters. The framework yields testable propositions about when LbT in LLMs should succeed or fail and offers a structured basis for designing more reliable autonomous learning systems.

**一句话**：该论文提出一个“Cognitive LbT Framework”，把反思推理、合成记忆、内部模拟、概念自组织四种“Thinking 式学习”机制映射到 LLM 的计算过程，并分析 CoT、self-consistency、self-reflection、ToT、Self-Instruct、STaR 等方法以及它们的失效模式。
