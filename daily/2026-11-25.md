### 🔥 ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Learning What vs. Learning How: Decomposing Task and Interface Competence in Agentic Training
**9/10** · Chaeeun Lee、Hossein A. Rahmani、Aryo Pradipta Gema、Kexin Xu、Emine Yilmaz、Pasquale Minervini · arXiv · 2026-11-23 · [原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31427v1) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.31427v1)
*入选：RL×LLM方法*

> 📄 **摘要**：LLM agents operate through harnesses that define system prompts, action formats, feedback loops, and resource constraints. A model may possess the knowledge needed to solve a task yet fail to express that knowledge through a particular harness. This raises a basic question: when a policy fails under a given harness, is it missing task knowledge or the competence to operate the interface? We distinguish two components of agentic competence: task competence, the knowledge and reasoning needed to solve a task, and interface competence, the ability to act on that knowledge through a given harness. Across four reasoning and agentic benchmarks, we show that harness-conditioned training yields vastly different outcomes depending on how these interact. Multi-harness distillation aggregates task knowledge from trajectories collected through different harnesses into a shared policy, but this knowledge is realised only when training preserves or improves competence on the inference-time interface. Otherwise, single-harness training can induce interference in interface competence, sometimes catastrophically collapsing performance on a held-out harness despite improvements in underlying task knowledge. To disentangle task and interface supervision, we introduce harness rehearsal: distillation on traces that carry minimal task knowledge while exercising the target interface. Cross-harness task supervision or rehearsal alone yields little gain, but their combination yields gains far larger than either: on BFCL V3 multi-turn with Qwen3-4B, they achieve 8.5% and 32.5% respectively, versus 58.8% together. Finally, on-policy reinforcement learning (RL) is more robust than distillation to single-harness training: it does not substantially degrade performance on other harnesses, and interface competence acquired through multi-harness distillation persists through subsequent RL.

**一句话**：把智能体能力拆成"任务知识"与"接口(harness)操作能力"两部分，用多harness蒸馏加harness rehearsal证明两者必须分别监督才能跨接口迁移。

痛点：模型具备解题知识却因不熟悉某个harness（系统提示、动作格式、反馈回路、资源约束）而失败，单harness训练还会对接口能力造成干扰甚至灾难性崩溃。方法：作者区分task competence与interface competence，提出在多harness轨迹上做蒸馏聚合任务知识，并设计harness rehearsal——用几乎不含任务知识的轨迹专门演练目标接口。结果：单独的任务监督或接口排练收益都很小，二者结合收益远超任一单独使用，且能避免held-out harness上的性能坍塌。
