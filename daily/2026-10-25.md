### ⭐⭐⭐ SLoSa: Small, Low-Power, and Safe Foundation Models for Robotic Task Planning
**6/10** · Weizhe Xu、Jialiang Fan、Essien Taylor、Zijun Wang、Russell Joseph、Fanxin Kong · arXiv (Cornell University) · 2026-10-20 · [原文](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2610.28955)
*入选：RL×LLM方法*

> 📄 **摘要**：Safe robotic task planning requires planners that can reliably achieve task goals while satisfying safety constraints. Classical planners search over explicit symbolic task representations and can provide formal guarantees with respect to encoded safety constraints, but require rigid input formats. Their computational cost also increases rapidly with the search space. In contrast, large language models (LLMs) offer flexible task interfaces and strong generalization capabilities, but they are large, power-hungry, and pre-trained with little robot-specific safety data and knowledge. Thus, they can be difficult to deploy locally on robotic systems with limited resources. To address these limitations, we present SLoSa, a neuro-symbolic compression and fine-tuning framework for training foundation-model-based robotic task planners with Small size, Low power demand, and Safety awareness. The framework first constructs a safety-aware dataset using classical planners, an advanced teacher LLM, and formal verifiers, followed by two training stages: supervised fine-tuning and reinforcement learning (RL) fine-tuning guided by a verification-based reward machine to enforce safety alignment. We evaluate SLoSa across four Planning Domain Definition Language (PDDL) domains, two household simulation environments, local edge platforms, and a desktop robotic arm. Across the PDDL tasks, verifier-guided post-training raises the success rate of compact 4B-14B planners to 70.5-76.0%, while INT4 deployment on a Jetson Orin requires 17.85-26.41 W of average power. Results in household simulators and on the physical robot further demonstrate the applicability of SLoSa across diverse robotic task settings.

**一句话**：SLoSa 提出神经-符号压缩与微调框架，用经典规划器、教师 LLM 和形式验证器合成安全数据集，再经 SFT 与验证奖励机引导的 RL 训练 4B-14B 小型机器人任务规划器。

痛点：LLM 规划器灵活但体积大、耗能高、缺乏机器人安全知识，经典规划器可保证安全但输入格式死板、搜索代价高。方法：用经典规划器+教师 LLM+形式验证器构造安全感知数据集，做 SFT 后接验证奖励机驱动的 RL 微调，实现小型化与安全对齐。结果：在四个 PDDL 域、两个家庭仿真环境和边缘设备上，验证器引导的后训练把 4B-14B 紧凑规划器成功率提升到 70.5-76.0%，INT4 量化后可本地部署。

> 🔗 **与课题**：支撑环节①与②、⑥、⑦的弱相关版本：①方面，它把 PDDL 任务描述转成动作序列，属于任务分解/规划，但用经典符号规划范式而非 LLM 自由工作流推理；②方面，PDDL 计划本身就是带前提-效果依赖的结构化表示，可作为"依赖闭合"的一种形式化参照；⑥方面，其 SFT + 验证奖励机 RL 的两阶段后训练是可直接借用的训练配方；⑦方面，验证器判定成功率 + 形式验证指标是可迁移到 QGIS 落地可用率/幻觉算子率的评测思路。但它不做 tool/operator grounding，也不做跨域跨平台能力迁移（压缩与微调均在同一机器人任务域内），因此不触及核心的 GEE→QGIS 迁移问题。

💡 **启发**：把"形式验证器"当作奖励信号和评测裁判：可以用 QGIS 侧的依赖图合法性/算子存在性检查器替代 PDDL 验证器，构造 verifier-based reward machine 来做后训练与执行成功率评估；另外"经典规划器产数据 + 教师 LLM 扩增"的数据合成路径可用于构造 workflow→图结构 的监督数据。

### ⭐⭐⭐ Steering Black-Box Large Language Model Agents with Pitfall Anticipation World Model
**6/10** · Jiongxiao Wang、Han Ding、Youzhi Luo、Yawei Wang、Yun Zhou、Linbo Liu · arXiv (Cornell University) · 2026-10-21 · [原文](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2610.29139)
*入选：RL×LLM方法*

> 📄 **摘要**：Frontier black-box Large Language Model (LLM) agents are proficient at long-horizon reasoning and precise action generation. However, when it comes to a specific agentic task, they have no internalized knowledge of how that particular environment unfolds. A world model offers a natural remedy, providing the agent with environment specific knowledge by foreseeing how the environment will respond to the action it is about to take. Prior world models for LLM agents mainly predict next observations at each step, which is costly and often uninformative because individual observations provide limited task-relevant guidance and not every step requires call for foresight. This raises a key question: what a world model should predict and when should it be invoked for agentic tasks? Our pilot study suggests that world model guidance is most useful when it anticipates pitfalls within a bounded multi-step future. Motivated by this, we introduce Pitfall Anticipation World Model (PAWM), which selectively intervenes when a potential pitfall arises and predicts the impending failure to steer the agent away from it. PAWM is trained in two stages: Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) on synthetic data labeled with agent pitfalls, followed by GRPO co-training with a black-box LLM to further optimize it in the target environment. PAWM improves Claude Sonnet 4.5 performance by 24.3% on ScienceWorld and 7.7% on WebArena-Lite, outperforming a range of baselines. These results demonstrate that PAWM provides an effective form of world model guidance for black-box LLM agents.

**一句话**：提出 Pitfall Anticipation World Model（PAWM），让世界模型只在多步未来的有限范围内预判即将发生的失败（pitfall）并选择性介入，以此引导黑盒 LLM agent。

痛点：黑盒前沿 LLM agent 擅长长程推理与动作生成，但对具体 agentic 环境缺乏内化知识，而逐步预测下一观察的世界模型代价高且信息量低。方法：先做试点研究，发现世界模型的引导只在一段有界多步未来的 pitfall 预判上最有效；于是 PAWM 采用两阶段训练——先用标注 agent pitfall 的合成数据做 SFT，再与黑盒 LLM 在目标任务环境中用 GRPO 协同训练，并只在潜在 pitfall 出现时选择性介入。结果：Claude Sonnet 4.5 在 ScienceWorld 提升 24.3%、WebArena-Lite 提升 7.7%，优于多个基线。

> 🔗 **与课题**：支撑环节⑦（轨迹级错误预判与失败分析的评测思路）与①（多步 agentic 任务的长程规划与中途纠错），并提供环节⑥中 SFT + GRPO 协同训练的配方——且这里的 GRPO 绑定在 agent/工具环境（ScienceWorld、WebArena）而非纯算法改进，因此按校准规则算作高相关边缘。但它不涉及 Workflow→结构化表示（②）、GEE→QGIS 的跨平台能力迁移（③）、算子 grounding（④）或 processes+links 图生成（⑤），也不涉及模型内部工作流知识的可迁移性，因此只到支撑层而非核心层。对课题的用处在于：若要把「工作流推理质量/依赖闭合」做成可评测对象，pitfall 预判提供了一个「只在高风险步骤介入并判错」的评测与干预范式。

💡 **启发**：可借鉴「有界多步未来内的失败预判 + 选择性介入」作为 Workflow Reasoning 的轻量评判器：在模型生成 QGIS 图的中途，只对即将出现的依赖断裂/幻觉算子做预警，而非每步都验证；两阶段（合成 pitfall 数据 SFT + 与黑盒模型 GRPO 协同）也可迁移到工作流纠错模块的训练上。

⚠️ **局限**：实验只在 ScienceWorld/WebArena 两个文本 agent 环境验证，pitfall 标签依赖合成数据与人工定义，未见对结构化产出（如 DAG/图结构）正确性的直接评测。

### ⭐⭐ Reasoning-Search-Augmented LLMs: An Operational Taxonomy, Benchmark Analysis and Controlled Evidence Synthesis
**5/10** · Biswas Poudel、Nilson Chapagain、Amit Kumar、Xianshun Jiang · arXiv (Cornell University) · 2026-10-20 · [原文](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23524292)
*入选：RL×LLM方法*

> 📄 **摘要**：Large language models (LLMs) demonstrate impressive emergent reasoning capabilities, yet they remain constrained by fixed model parameters and finite context windows. Even when augmented with static retrieval, they can produce statements that are unsupported by, or even contradictory to, retrieved evidence. This leads to hallucinations, difficulty incorporating up-to-date information, and failures to correctly interpret or reconcile sources. Recent work addresses these limitations by tightly coupling step-by-step reasoning with interactive search, treating retrieval as an action within an iterative agentic loop rather than a one-shot preprocessing step. This study reviews frameworks that implement such reasoning-search loops by interleaving planning, querying, retrieval, integration, verification, and stopping. We analyze these systems through four architectural axes: retrieval coupling, controller design, knowledge source, and grounding strictness. We separately treat the learning signal as a cross-cutting dimension because prompting, supervision, preference optimization, and reinforcement learning can instantiate the same architecture while producing materially different search, stopping, and grounding behavior. We further distinguish benchmark difficulty from RSA-loop demand and synthesize controlled within-study ablations and quality-cost deltas, avoiding invalid pooling across heterogeneous model backbones, retrievers, corpora, and evaluation protocols. Our goal is to clarify the landscape of reasoning-search-augmented LLMs and outline a unified perspective on the design choices shaping future research.

**一句话**：系统综述将逐步推理与交互式搜索紧耦合的“推理-搜索增强”LLM框架，提出四轴架构分类学（检索耦合方式、控制器设计、知识源、接地严格度）并做基准分析与对照证据综合。

痛点：LLM受固定参数与有限上下文的约束，即使接入静态检索也会产生无据甚至与证据矛盾的陈述，导致幻觉与信息时效性问题。方法：把检索视为agentic循环中的一个动作，梳理“规划—查询—检索—整合—验证—停止”的迭代循环架构，沿四个架构轴并叠加学习信号（提示、监督、偏好优化、RL）这一横切维度进行分类，同时区分基准难度与循环需求，只综合同一研究内部的消融与质量-成本增量，避免跨异构模型、检索器与语料的无效合并。结果：给出可操作的分类学与评测证据综合框架。

> 📄 **摘要**：Large Language Models (LLMs) are increasingly integrated into cybersecurity workflows, creating both defensive opportunities and new forms of offensive enablement. This review synthesizes recent evidence and develops a methodological framework for evaluating offensive utility as a multidimensional construct encompassing technical capability, correctness, contextual grounding, reliability, operational relevance, temporal efficiency, and human-expertise compression.
