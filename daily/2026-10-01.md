### ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Asking for What Was Never Requested: Horizontal and Vertical Proactivity in Agents
**7/10** · Ido Levy、Asaf Yehudai、Segev Shlomov、Asaf Adi、Leshem Choshen · arXiv · 2026-09-29 · [原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37236v1) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.37236v1)
*入选：RL×LLM方法*

> 📄 **摘要**：An agent that uses tools typically responds to what the user explicitly asks, yet completing the task may require information the user never requested. Work on proactive agents mainly studies whether and when an agent should act on its own, not what information it should pursue. We study a distinct axis of proactivity: its content. Horizontal proactivity pursues unstated information that the current context already identifies, and vertical proactivity pursues needs that only earlier evidence reveals. A need graph, recovered from a benchmark's own decomposition, records which needs depend on which, so both forms, and whether the agent stops at the right time, can be scored from a transcript without a model judge. To learn this behavior, we propose Q&D (questioner and drafter), which trains a questioner to prefer the question whose continuation retrieves more of the required evidence, with no reward model or judge. On held-out splits of three multi-hop question-answering benchmarks, at equal retrieval spend, the trained questioner improves both forms of proactivity over the same model, prompted, and outperforms a prompted model $15\times$ larger in the same role on two of the three, and the gain persists after controlling for question volume and length. Without further training, we place the questioner in an interactive customer-service agent with a simulated customer, where it completes more tasks while asking fewer questions, and in retail it outperforms the $15\times$ larger model with fewer follow-up turns from the customer. These results show that proactivity depends not only on whether an agent acts without being asked, but also on what it chooses to pursue and when it stops.

**一句话**：论文提出用从基准分解恢复的“需求图”区分横向/纵向主动性，并训练 Q&D（提问者+起草者）在不依赖奖励模型或评委的情况下提升多跳问答中的主动信息获取。

痛点：现有主动性智能体研究只关心“何时该自主行动”，不关心“应当主动追问/获取什么信息”。方法：把主动性内容分为横向（当前上下文已隐含的需求）与纵向（须由先前证据揭示的需求），用需求图记录需求间依赖，训练提问者偏好“其后续能检索到更多必要证据”的问题，全程无需奖励模型或模型评委。结果：在三个多跳QA基准的留出集上，同等检索开销下两种主动性均优于同模型提示版、且在两项基准上超过15倍大的提示模型，控制提问量与长度后增益仍在；该提问者无需再训练即可嵌入模拟客服（完成更多任务、提问更少）与零售场景。

> 🔗 **与课题**：主要支撑①：将任务分解为相互依赖的信息需求并规划获取顺序，属于“任务分解/依赖组织”的工作流推理能力（多跳QA即多步依赖推理）。同时支撑②：need graph 把需求间的依赖关系显式结构化，可用于判断依赖闭合与何时停止，与 workflow→结构化依赖表示同构。还支撑⑥：Q&D 用“偏好能带来更多证据的中间步骤”做训练，无需 reward model/judge，是偏好式后训练的一种轻量实现，可直接借鉴到工作流推理的中间步骤偏好训练。支撑⑦：基于需求图的无评委评测协议可统计覆盖度与“停止时机”错误，对应我们评测中的依赖闭合与遗漏/多余步骤指标。对③仅弱对应（训练所得提问能力零样本迁移到客服/零售属源域→目标域迁移示例）；完全不涉及④算子 grounding 与⑤可执行 processes+links 图生成。

💡 **启发**：可直接借用其“需求图 + 无模型评委”的评测思路：为 QGIS workflow 构造 ground-truth 依赖图，用转录/输出中的需求覆盖度来自动打分依赖闭合与提前停止，避免遗漏或多余步骤；以及“同等检索/交互预算下比较”的公平对照协议与“偏好带来更多证据的中间步骤而无奖励模型”的偏好构造方式。

⚠️ **局限**：研究停留在信息检索式多跳问答与对话场景，不涉及可执行算子/代码/结构化图生成，对其向 QGIS 类工具落地与图结构生成的可迁移性目前只能类推。

### 🔥 ⭐⭐⭐⭐ SYNCR: Diagnosing and Learning Cross-Video Reasoning from Simulation
**8/10** · Sara Ghazanfari、Siddharth Garg、Prashanth Krishnamurthy、Farshad Khorrami · arXiv · 2026-09-29 · [原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37918v1) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.37918v1)
*入选：能力迁移*

> 📄 **摘要**：Reasoning across videos requires aligning events, matching identities, comparing motion, and integrating partial observations. Evaluating these capabilities and testing how to improve them requires both reliable labels and targeted supervision. We introduce SYNCR, a simulator-grounded framework that connects these two needs through shared task generators. Built on Habitat, Kubric, and CLEVRER, SYNCR derives answers from environment state and provides 4,000 evaluation questions and 15,960 training questions over disjoint videos, spanning eight cross-video reasoning tasks. Visual ablations and human evaluation assess dependence on the supplied evidence and answer recoverability. Evaluation of 22 multimodal large language models reveals persistent difficulties in physical comparison and scene integration that increasing model size does not consistently resolve. Supervised fine-tuning raises Qwen3-VL-8B's average SYNCR accuracy from 32.6% to 61.6%, with gains extending to task configurations and video sources absent from training for those tasks. Transfer to real footage is most consistent for temporal ordering: accuracy improves by 9.0-20.5 percentage points on constructed Assembly101 and Panoptic ordering sets across three checkpoints spanning two model families and two model sizes, with additional gains on existing temporal reasoning benchmarks. These results establish SYNCR as a controlled setting for diagnosing cross-video reasoning failures, testing their learnability, and identifying where synthetic supervision transfers.

**一句话**：提出 SYNCR——一个基于 Habitat/Kubric/CLEVRER 仿真器的跨视频推理诊断与训练框架，用共享任务生成器自动造出 8 类跨视频推理任务的评测集与训练集，并验证 MLLM 的可学习性与向真实视频的迁移。

痛点：跨视频推理（事件对齐、身份匹配、运动比较、整合部分观测）既缺可靠标注也缺针对性监督。方法：以仿真环境状态直接导出答案，构建 4,000 评测题与 15,960 训练题（视频互不重叠），并用视觉消融与人工评估检验答案对证据的依赖性与可恢复性。结果：22 个 MLLM 在物理比较与场景整合上持续失败且不随规模稳定改善；SFT 把 Qwen3-VL-8B 平均准确率从 32.6% 提到 61.6%，并泛化到未见任务配置与视频源；在 Assembly101/Panoptic 构造的时间排序集上迁移提升 9.0-20.5 个百分点。

> 🔗 **与课题**：主要支撑 ③（能力迁移），兼及 ⑥（训练/后训练）与 ⑦（评测）。③对应关系：论文做的正是“源域→目标域”能力迁移的实证研究——在仿真视频上做目标监督，再量化同一能力迁移到真实视频（Assembly101/Panoptic）的保持率，并进一步测其在“训练中未出现的任务配置与视频源”上的泛化，这正是我们 GEE→QGIS 迁移保持率（transfer retention）想复刻的实验骨架。⑥对应：用成对的仿真自动标注数据做 SFT，并在多个 checkpoint、两个模型族、两种参数规模上验证增益是否稳定，等价于我们“DAPT/SFT 后工作流知识是否残留并可迁移”的训练实验设计。⑦对应：仿真状态导出标签 + 视觉消融 + 人工评估 + 跨模型/跨规模迁移曲线，是一套可直接映射为“工作流推理质量、迁移保持率、落地可用率”指标的可复用评测协议。需要注意的是，它迁移的是跨视频时序/物理推理能力，而非工具/算子 grounding 能力，因此其机制解释对我们只能作为“迁移可测且可提升”的方法学证据链，而非直接的能力等价。

💡 **启发**：最可直接偷的是“共享任务生成器”范式：用同一套生成器同源产出评测集与训练集，并以环境状态充当自动标签——可类比为在 GEE/仿真端程序化生成 workflow/processes+links 图作为 (任务, 图) 标注对，再用跨 checkpoint、跨模型族的迁移曲线量化 QGIS 落地保持率。另外，它“训练源与测试源故意不重叠 + 报告未见任务配置泛化”的设计，可作为我们防泄漏评测切分的模板。

⚠️ **局限**：其迁移对象是视频感知-时序推理技能而非工具/工作流落地能力，迁移增益也主要集中在时间排序一类任务，引用时不能直接声称其结论适用于 operator grounding 的迁移。

🚨 **为什么必须重视**：它和我们的核心科学问题在“源域训练→目标域迁移保持率”这一评测骨架上高度撞车，且已经给出可运行的协议（多 checkpoint、跨模型族与规模、未见任务配置泛化、消融+人工双重校验），属于必须立即对标的方法竞品。若我们的 GEE→QGIS 迁移复现实验不采用同类量化口径，将无法与已有工作比较，也难以证明迁移是真实发生而非任务泄漏。

### 🔥 ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Zero-shot Dependency Parsing with Unsupervised Cross-Lingual Bootstrapping
**8/10** · Lalita Lowphansirikul、Attapol Rutherford、Jian Gang Ngui、Sarana Nutanong、Peerat Limkonchotiwat · arXiv · 2026-09-29 · [原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37883v1) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.37883v1)
*入选：能力迁移*

> 📄 **摘要**：Pre-trained language models (PLMs) with encoder-based architectures have shown impressive capabilities in zero-shot cross-lingual transfer for various language understanding tasks. However, applying this technique to dependency parsing remains a significant challenge due to its syntactic nature. To boost model generalizability across linguistic typologies, we propose a cross-lingual unsupervised bootstrapping method to improve syntactic knowledge within the PLM. We show that our method achieves a significant improvement in zero-shot parsing performance in low-resource languages. Analysis of these bootstrapped models uncovers increased robustness in recognizing syntactic structures, evidenced by higher scores in parameter-free tree probing tests.

**一句话**：提出跨语言无监督自举方法，在预训练语言模型内部注入句法知识，从而显著提升低资源语言的零样本依存句法分析性能。

痛点：编码器式PLM虽在零样本跨语言迁移上表现不俗，但依存句法分析高度依赖句法结构，跨语言类型差异导致迁移困难。方法：用跨语言无监督自举在PLM内部强化句法知识，不需目标语言标注。结果：低资源语言零样本解析性能显著提升，且无参数树探测（parameter-free tree probing）得分更高，说明模型内部句法结构表征更稳健、更可迁移。

> 🔗 **与课题**：支撑环节③（能力迁移）为主：这是LLM/PLM上标准的源域→目标域零样本能力迁移实证研究，与GEE→QGIS迁移在问题结构上同构——都在追问'源域习得的能力在没有目标域监督时能否保持'，且给出了迁移增益的量化证据。支撑②（结构化表示）：依存树是显式依赖结构，是DAG/图结构的特例，其自举目标就是让模型内部稳定产出结构正确的依赖树，与Workflow→结构化表示、processes+links图生成同属'把隐式理解转成依赖闭合的结构'。支撑⑦（评测）：parameter-free tree probing 提供了一种不依赖下游任务精度的机理层探测协议，可直接迁移用于验证'模型内部是否真的学到了可迁移的工作流知识'，作为transfer retention的隐层证据，而非只报告最终成功率。支撑⑥（训练/后训练）：无监督自举本质是不依赖标注的自训练式知识注入流程，可作为DAPT/持续学习式后训练的参考范式。不涉及④Workflow→Operator grounding与⑤QGIS图生成，也无任何tool/tool calling内容。

💡 **启发**：最可直接偷的是'parameter-free probing'思路：在隐状态上构造无参数探测任务，检验模型内部是否存在可迁移的workflow/DAG结构知识，把它作为transfer retention的机理指标（比只看执行成功率更有解释力）；其次是'无监督自举注入源域能力'的流程，可用于GEE→QGIS无目标平台标注条件下的能力内化训练。

⚠️ **局限**：迁移发生在语言/句法域，与工具算子落地之间存在明显语义鸿沟，其自举增益能否外推到tool/operator grounding并无证据。

🚨 **为什么必须重视**：它把'源域→目标域能力迁移是否保持'做成了可量化、可探测的实证，且提供了隐层探测协议，正好补上本课题'可迁移工作流知识'如何被验证这一空白环节，是目前最接近③环节方法论的现成模板。若不自建这套探测评测，后续GEE→QGIS的transfer retention结论将只能停留在下游成功率层面，说服力弱于本文范式。

### 🔥 ⭐⭐⭐⭐ XRepoSkill: Learning Transferable Skills for Software Engineering Agents
**8/10** · Yaoqi Guo、Haoyang Zhou、Jiayi Zhang、Yiran Zhang、Yang Liu、Qiuyuan Chen · arXiv · 2026-09-29 · [原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36807v1) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.36807v1)
*入选：能力迁移*

> 📄 **摘要**：Software engineering agents increasingly use reusable skills distilled from prior experience to resolve repository-level issues, yet such skills often fail to transfer across repositories. A central challenge is that a behavior appearing in a successful trajectory is not necessarily responsible for the successful outcome: it may be genuinely useful, merely incidental, or simply a recurring habit of the model. We introduce XRepoSkill, a trajectory-based approach for learning transferable skills. We represent a skill as a collection of rules, each specifying what action to take and when to take it during issue resolution. XRepoSkill first contrasts successful and failed trajectories of the same agent on the same issue and derives candidate rules from where their execution paths diverge. Each rule is paired with an executable predicate that enables its prescribed behavior to be evaluated systematically on other trajectories. A rule is verified based on its association with successful issue resolution and retained only when its prescribed behavior recurs across multiple repositories; repository-specific variants of the same behavior are then consolidated into transferable rules. For a new issue, XRepoSkill selects relevant rules to guide the agent. We learn skills from publicly released trajectories on the official SWE-bench Verified leaderboard and evaluate them on SWE-bench Pro and DeepSWE using three backbone LLMs from different vendors; none of the evaluation repositories appears in the skill-learning trajectory pool. Against three recent skill learning methods, XRepoSkill achieves the highest issue resolution rate in all six benchmark--LLM combinations. In particular, on the challenging long-horizon DeepSWE benchmark, XRepoSkill improves issue resolution by 10.3 percentage points over the same agent without learned skills and by 5.0 points over the strongest skill-learning baseline.

**一句话**：XRepoSkill 通过对比同一 issue 上同一 agent 的成功/失败轨迹分叉点提取候选规则(rules)，再用可执行谓词与跨仓库复现验证筛选出真正可迁移的技能，用于引导新仓库的软件工程 agent。

痛点：agent 从历史经验蒸馏的技能难以跨仓库迁移，且成功轨迹中出现的行为未必是成功的原因（可能只是 incidental 或模型习惯）。方法：用同一 agent 在同一 issue 上的成功/失败轨迹做差分，从执行路径分叉处导出候选规则，为每条规则配可执行谓词以便在其它轨迹上系统验证，仅保留与成功结果关联且在多个仓库复现的行为，并把仓库特定变体合并为可迁移规则；新 issue 时检索相关规则引导 agent。结果：在 SWE-bench 官方 Verified 榜单的公开轨迹上学习技能，在 SWE-bench Pro 与 DeepSWE 上使用三家不同厂商的三种 backbone LLM 评测，评测仓库均未出现在训练轨迹中。

> 🔗 **与课题**：支撑环节③（跨域/跨平台能力迁移）为主，并覆及①⑦。①GIS Task→Workflow：论文把技能表为 rule = "做什么动作 + 何时做"，本质是带触发条件的步骤级流程知识，与 workflow reasoning 的任务分解/步骤规划同构。③能力迁移：这是本文主轴——源域（学习轨迹所属仓库）→目标域（未见仓库）的技能迁移实证，其"成功/失败轨迹分叉点"因果筛选与"跨仓库复现"判据，正是我们 GEE→QGIS transfer retention 需要的判据设计；它明确处理了"成功轨迹中的行为未必是成功原因"这一与我们"幻觉算子/偶发正确步骤"高度同构的问题。⑦评测：用三个不同厂商 backbone、在完全 held-out 仓库上评测，构成一种迁移保持率的评测协议，可改写为跨平台（GEE→QGIS）保留率指标。⑥训练/后训练：属于不更新参数的经验蒸馏/持续学习式技能库构建，可归入 distill 档，为我们的 skill-library 路线提供对照基线。②⑤较弱：规则为扁平 if-then 集合，未涉及 DAG/依赖闭合的图结构生成。

💡 **启发**：可直接偷用其"成功 vs 失败轨迹分叉点 → 候选规则 → 可执行谓词验证 → 跨域复现筛选 → 变体合并"五步管线，把 GEE 上留下的成功/失败工作流轨迹同样做差分，用谓词在 QGIS 任务上验证复现率，以剔除 incidental 步骤；另可借鉴其三厂商 backbone × 双未见基准的迁移评测矩阵形式。

⚠️ **局限**：技能被压缩为扁平 if-then 规则+谓词，无法表达带依赖的 DAG/图结构，且验证域仍限于代码仓库，跨平台（工具/API 语义不同）迁移是否成立未验证。

🚨 **为什么必须重视**：它直接以"学习可迁移技能"为标题与核心问题，与我们 Route-2 的"模型内部可迁移的工作流知识"正面撞车，其"行为出现≠行为致胜"的因果甄别正是我们 transfer retention 评测的隐含假设。其跨仓库复现验证协议可低成本改写为跨平台（GEE→QGIS）复现协议，是我们尚未建立的迁移判据的直接来源。若被同赛道先行用于跨工具迁移，会直接占据我们③环节的方法位置，需尽快精读并做对比基线。

### 🔥 ⭐⭐⭐⭐ EASE: Behavior-Adaptive Skill Curation for Self-Evolving Agents
**8/10** · Zhen Xiong、Qiaoyu Tan · arXiv · 2026-09-29 · [原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36746v1) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.36746v1)
*入选：RL×LLM方法*

> 📄 **摘要**：Agent skills provide a lightweight mechanism for self-evolving agents to accumulate reusable procedural knowledge without updating model parameters. However, existing learned skill curators typically optimize curation without explicitly modeling downstream executor behavior. **一句话**：提出 Vestrum 框架，把 agent 执行轨迹中的失败转化为 harness 层面的有界修改（验证、检索、分解、记忆、知识综合），无需训练任务模型。

> 🔗 **与课题**：支撑环节③（跨域/跨平台能力迁移）与①（工作流推理/任务分解），兼及⑦（评测/错误分析）。其核心 overhypothesis『同类任务的失败修复可在类内迁移』正是源域→目标域的迁移主张，可用来说明工作流知识/修复策略的可迁移性边界。Vestrum 在提案空间中显式包含 decomposition（任务分解）改动，对应①的任务分解/流程规划环节。它把失败表示为可识别类别并先筛查作用范围（scope screening）再整体评估，这一失配→修复→筛选流程可直接映射到我们 workflow reasoning→grounding 的错误归因与迁移保持率评估。但其迁移是 harness 层面的策略迁移，而非工具/算子语义的 grounding 迁移，也未生成结构化 DAG，故未触及②④⑤。

⚠️ **局限**：面向通用 agent harness 自适应，不涉及工具/算子落地、显式 L3 中间层或 DAG 结构化生成；结论依赖已有强模型与较长环境交互，迁移收益以 harness 为单位而非模型内部知识。

### ⭐⭐⭐⭐ PR-OPD: Privileged Representation On-policy Self-Distillation for Agentic Reinforcement Learning

> 📄 **摘要**：Language-model agents are usually trained by reinforcement learning from one reward per episode, and privileged self-distillation enriches it by letting the same policy, given a skill, teach its skill-free self through token probabilities. 💡 **启发**：可直接偷两条：(1) 用线性探针/隐状态对齐度做 "transfer retention" 指标——训练前后探测各层隐状态中是否仍可线性解码出 GEE 工作流结构信息，而非只看 QGIS 生成正确率；(2) 把 hindsight skill 自蒸馏当作低成本后训练配方：让模型自写 QGIS 工作流技能说明并以此重读自身响应做层间对齐，无需外部技能库与独立教师。

⚠️ **局限**：仅在 ALFWorld/WebShop 两个文本智能体环境验证，技能由模型自写缺乏外部监督，隐状态对齐依赖同源策略，跨模型/跨平台的表示可迁移性未验证。

### ⭐⭐⭐ AdviSD: Learning to Advise Frontier LLMs via Targeted Multi-Turn Self-Distillation
**6/10** · Rishabh Agrawal、Hejie Cui、Shasha Li、Shanchan Wu、Sercan Ö. Arık · arXiv · 2026-09-29 · [原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38142v1) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.38142v1)
*入选：能力迁移*

> 📄 **摘要**：A small trainable advisor can steer a frozen language-model executor using natural-language advice. Keeping them less often than the rest improves the model's eventual performance compared to learning from every correction. **一句话**：提出 AdviSD，用小模型 advisor 通过自然语言建议引导冻结的大模型 executor，并用选择性自蒸馏+结果导向 RL 训练 advisor 何时该给建议。

> 🔗 **与课题**：主要支撑⑥训练/后训练与⑦评测，弱支撑③能力迁移。⑥：论文的核心贡献是选择性自蒸馏+RL 的 advisor 训练范式，属于可迁移到我们 DAPT/SFT/蒸馏路线的通用后训练方法，其'按决策影响幅度选择监督样本'的机制可直接用于筛选 workflow reasoning 训练数据。⑦：在 BFCL-v3（工具调用）与 EnvScaler 上做评测，说明如何衡量多轮工具交互失败率，与我们关注的执行成功率/幻觉算子率指标相近。③：论文证明了 advisor 可跨 executor 版本与模型族迁移，但迁移的是'建议策略'而非工作流知识本身，只能作为跨模型迁移的实验范式参考，不构成 GEE→QGIS 式能力迁移的实证。

💡 **启发**：可按'有无该步骤/建议时下游 executor 输出差异幅度'来为 workflow 步骤做信用分配，用差异大小筛选高质量 workflow 样本用于 SFT/自蒸馏；另外可借鉴其跨 executor 版本/模型族迁移的评测协议来设计 GEE→QGIS 的迁移保持率实验。

⚠️ **局限**：方法绑定在'advisor 指导 executor'的双模型设定上，讨论的是建议策略的蒸馏选择而非工作流结构生成，GIS 与结构化图生成场景无涉。

### ⭐⭐⭐⭐ RLTL;DR: Self-improvement by Internalizing Self-generated Feedback
**7/10** · Michael Kirchhof、Eleonora Gualdoni、Andrew Szot、Khashayar Gatmiry、Aryo Lotfi、Abbas Kazerouni · arXiv · 2026-09-29 · [原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37633v1) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.37633v1)
*入选：RL×LLM方法*

> 📄 **摘要**：The common paradigm of reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR) is to let agents make multiple attempts at a task, and optimize towards the successful ones. This becomes problematic in the realms of self-improvement, where tasks are so difficult that the agent has a low or even no chance of success, and where there are no teacher models or example solutions to distill from. In this paper, we introduce RLTL;DR. After each failed attempt, we show the policy the verifier outputs and let it write its own feedback, in the form of a single TL;DR insight. The next rollout is conditioned on all previous insights, and we sequentially sample rollouts until a solution is found. Moreover, we enable backpropagation on the in-context insights to internalize a direct task to insight mapping. On challenging tool-calling and coding datasets (filtered to Pass@128=0), standard GRPO training of a Qwen 3.5 9B Thinking policy stays flat at a Pass@1 of 0% to 1%. RLTL;DR breaks through this learning barrier, achieving a Pass@1 of 14-31% with insights in context during training and, crucially, 12-13% when no insight is in context at eval time. We identify that the key is the task to insight internalization. To study this further, we reduce our approach to SFTL;DR, training only on (task, insight) tuples, without showing or backpropagating on any rollouts. Training on only 4k of these tuples recovers almost the full performance of RLTL;DR and classical SFT on full rollouts. This demonstrates a promising compacted training paradigm of the form "on this sort of task, keep this sort of thing in mind", which we hope to inspire future research on.

**一句话**：提出 RLTL;DR：让策略在每次失败后依据验证器输出自写一条 TL;DR 式洞见并为后续 rollout 提供上下文，同时对该 in-context 洞见反向传播以把"任务→洞见"映射内化到权重中，并可退化为只用 4k (task, insight) 元组的 SFTL;DR。
