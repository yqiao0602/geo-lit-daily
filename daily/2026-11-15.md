### ⭐⭐⭐ Same Task, Different Statement: When and How Reformulation Helps LLM Problem Solving
**6/10** · Wentao Zhang、Xiaoyu Zhang、Chengwei Liu、Yutao Hu、Tao Zhang、Junsheng Wu · arXiv (Cornell University) · 2026-11-11 · [原文](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2611.14997)
*入选：能力迁移*

> 📄 **摘要**：When people face a hard problem, they often restate it in a clearer way before solving it. We study whether the same habit helps large language models by rewriting the statement of a problem. A legal rewrite must keep the meaning, the constraints, and the correct answer unchanged. We test three kinds of rewrites. Decomposition makes hidden subgoals explicit. Typed Reformulation casts the task into a more suitable solving frame. Concept Reduction removes surface detail and exposes the task structure. We evaluate them on eight math, logic, and code benchmarks with eight models, all under the same chain-of-thought solving prompt. The effect is large but depends on the problem type. An oracle that picks the best rewrite after the fact raises accuracy by +27.6% on average. Yet no single rewrite helps every problem, and a mismatched rewrite can hurt. Still, the effect follows clear patterns. Each kind of rewrite fits certain problem types. A simple fixed rule learned from one set of problems transfers part of the gain to new problems. It gains +11.0% on the problems it chooses to rewrite, while blind rewriting loses 5.6% on a similar share of problems. Rewriting is therefore not a universal way to solve problems. The question is when and how to rewrite.

**一句话**：系统研究对 LLM 的问题陈述进行三类改写（分解、类型化重构、概念约简）何时有益、何时有害，并检验从一类问题学到的改写选择规则能否迁移到新问题。

痛点：LLM 面对难题时，换一种表述是否有帮助、在什么条件下有帮助，尚不清楚；盲目的改写甚至会带来损失。方法：在 8 个数学、逻辑与代码基准和 8 个模型上、在统一的 CoT 提示下比较分解式（显式子目标）、类型化重构（换求解框架）与概念约简（剥离表层细节）三类改写，并比较事后 oracle 选择与从一组问题学到的固定规则。结果：oracle 平均提升 +27.6%，但不存在对所有问题都适用的改写；可迁移的固定规则在它选择改写的问题上带来 +11.0%，而盲目改写损失 5.6%。

> 🔗 **与课题**：主要支撑①与③，并对⑦有方法借鉴。①：论文的核心操作之一就是 decomposition，即把隐含的子目标显式化；这正是 GIS Task→Workflow 中任务分解与步骤规划的同构操作，为“任务表述如何影响工作流推理质量”提供实证。③：固定改写选择规则从一个问题集合迁移到新问题集合（+11.0% vs 盲改 -5.6%），是一个可迁移的“策略层规则”而非模型内部权重的迁移，可作为源域→目标域迁移的弱对照，但与“模型内部工作流知识跨平台迁移”不同层级，引用时需区分。②：类型化重构（换求解框架）与概念约简（暴露任务结构）在概念上接近“把任务投影到结构化表示”，但论文未做任何图或 DAG 结构化产出。⑦：oracle 上界 vs 可执行规则、以及“在选中子集上的增益 vs 盲应用的损失”这一双指标设计，可直接类比迁移保持率（transfer retention）的度量方式。

💡 **启发**：可借鉴其“oracle 上界—可迁移规则—盲应用”三段式评测协议，用来量化 QGIS grounding 中“模型自选分解策略”相对“强制统一分解模板”的增益与损失；另外“改写类型与问题类型匹配”的思路可对应“工作流模式与 GIS 任务类型匹配”的选择器设计。

⚠️ **局限**：全部实验限于数学、逻辑、代码的单轮解题，改写只发生在文本陈述层，不涉及工具调用、算子落地或可执行图结构，迁移证据也只是浅层规则迁移而非模型能力迁移。

### ⭐⭐ An interactive spatio-temporal task computing framework with incremental graph reconstruction for remote sensing cloud platforms

*入选：强命中*

> 📄 **摘要**：In cloud-based environments, remote sensing computing platforms typically rely on executable scripts to orchestrate the invocation of spatio-temporal operators, thereby supporting the solution of geoscientific problems. However, existing task orchestration paradigms are primarily oriented toward monolithic end-to-end modeling, making it difficult to meet the requirements of scientific Workflow Management Systems (WfMS) for staged workflow construction, modular management, and iterative optimization. Moreover, in interactive analysis scenarios, repeatedly executed workflows with similar structures often lack effective reuse mechanisms, resulting in substantial redundancy in cloud computing resource consumption. To address these challenges, this study proposes an interactive spatio-temporal task computing framework tailored with an incremental computation approach based on graph reconstruction. We formally represent and dynamically construct spatiotemporal tasks using graph structures, then track the evolution of workflows during the spatiotemporal task modeling, and analyze the topological differences and dependencies between successive task versions. This enables us to systematically identify change operations and potential reuse opportunities during interactive orchestration, ultimately supporting fine-grained incremental computation. The proposed framework and methodology have been implemented on the Open Geospatial Engine (OGE) platform. Quantitative evaluations demonstrate that the proposed approach consistently reduces computational redundancy and execution time across different remote sensing tasks and data scales compared with conventional cloud-based geoprocessing workflows.

### ⭐⭐ Agentic Quantitative Trading: A Survey of Workflows, Systems, and Evaluation
**5/10** · Fengrui Hua、Hengyi Yang、Xinlei Hao、Haohan Zhang、Bokai Cao、Yiyan Qi · ACM International Conference on AI in Finance (ICAIF) · 2026-11-14 · [原文](https://doi.org/10.1145/3858063.3858844)
*入选：工作流×工具推理*

> 📄 **摘要**：Quantitative trading is moving from isolated predictive models toward agentic workflows that combine reasoning, tool use, memory, and feedback. This survey reviews agentic quantitative trading across five stages: factor mining, signal discovery, portfolio construction, order execution, and risk management. We further examine agentic quant trading systems through architecture, coordination, and adaptation, while comparing benchmarks across strategy construction, offline trading, live market evaluation, and reliability assessment. Our review finds that current systems remain concentrated on signal discovery, while complete integration with portfolio construction, execution, and risk control is still uncommon. Multi-agent systems also rely heavily on aggregation despite increasingly diverse workflow structures. Benchmark evidence further shows that strong model or forecasting capability does not reliably translate into trading performance under live market conditions and reliability controls. We conclude with future directions for more complete trading workflows, stronger coordination, and evaluation matched to the capability being assessed.
