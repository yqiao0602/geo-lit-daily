### 🔥 ⭐⭐⭐⭐ The Persona Hierarchy Model: Understanding Contextual Generalization in Fine-Tuning LLMs
**8/10** · Jiachen Zhao、Zhengxuan Wu、David Bau、Weiyan Shi · arXiv · 2026-10-07 · [原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09384v1) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.09384v1)
*入选：能力迁移*

> 📄 **摘要**：Language models are routinely fine-tuned under a fixed context, such as a generic system prompt, persona or domain-specific instruction, yet the learned behavior sometimes stays confined to that context and sometimes broadly generalizes to unseen contexts. We propose the Persona Hierarchy Model to explain this: a shared default persona influences behavior across contexts. Under this model, fine-tuning that modifies the shared persona promotes broader transfer, whereas changes to local personas remain more context-specific. Across 120 fine-tuned models spanning four behaviors and 15 training contexts, generalization narrowness positively correlates with the similarity between the training context's persona and the default persona (Pearson's r = 0.72 for Qwen3-4B). Prior fine-tuning under the default context can broaden generalization in subsequent training under other contexts. Aligning contextual responses with default-persona responses produces stronger effects. Finally, we propose persona-preserving regularization (PPR) to confine undesired contextual generalization. In RL, PPR cuts reward hacking from 42-55% to at most 0.2% under every evaluated prompt while retaining accuracy gains. These results support the Persona Hierarchy Model as an explanation for contextual generalization and can motivate future controls on unintended generalization for better alignment of LLMs.

**一句话**：该论文提出 Persona Hierarchy Model，用共享默认人格与局部人格的层级结构解释微调后的行为何时泛化到新情境、何时被锁死在训练情境中，并提出 persona-preserving regularization (PPR) 约束不期望的泛化。

痛点：LLM 在固定上下文（系统提示、persona、领域指令）下微调后，学到的行为有时能广泛迁移到未见上下文，有时却被严格限制在训练情境内，其机制不明。方法：提出 Persona Hierarchy Model——存在一个跨情境共享的默认 persona，微调若修改共享 persona 则泛化更广，若只改局部 persona 则泛化更窄；在 4 种行为 × 15 个训练上下文共 120 个微调模型上验证，泛化狭窄度与训练上下文 persona 和默认 persona 的相似度正相关（Qwen3-4B 上 r=0.72），并发现先在默认上下文微调可拓宽后续其他上下文的泛化，以及 PPR 正则。结果：在 RL 中 PPR 把 reward hacking 从 42-55% 降到最多 0.2%，同时保留准确率增益。

> 🔗 **与课题**：主要支撑环节③能力迁移（源域→目标域）：论文用 120 个微调模型的实证回答“微调行为何时能迁到未见上下文、何时被情境锁死”，这正是本课题“GEE 学到的 workflow reasoning 能否迁到 QGIS”的同构问题——把“训练上下文 persona”类比为 GEE 场景、“默认 persona”类比为跨平台共享的 workflow 推理知识，其“与默认 persona 相似度越高→泛化越窄”的结论可直接改写为本课题的可检验假设（GEE 训练上下文离默认 workflow 先验越近，越难迁移到 QGIS）。环节⑥训练/后训练：论文给出 SFT 的多上下文训练设计、先在默认上下文微调再迁移的分阶段训练策略、以及 PPR 正则与 RL 中抑制 reward hacking 的手段，可直接映射为“先在通用 GIS workflow 语料上 DAPT/SFT，再在 GEE 上 SFT”的课程式训练方案。环节⑦评测：其“泛化宽度/狭窄度”度量与跨情境保持率，为本课题的 transfer retention 与迁移保持率指标提供了可操作的测量范式和统计相关性分析方法。对①GIS Task→Workflow 与④grounding 仅间接触及——默认 persona 可以理解为共享的任务分解先验，但论文未涉及工作流结构或算子落地。对②⑤结构化/DAG 生成无贡献。

💡 **启发**：可直接偷的两条：(1) 把“训练上下文 persona 与默认 persona 的相似度”改造成“源平台情境与共享 workflow 先验的相似度”这一自变量，用 Pearson 相关量化它对迁移保持率的影响，作为本课题的核心分析维度；(2) 借鉴“先在默认上下文微调可拓宽后续泛化”的课程式训练顺序，以及 PPR 这类正则项来抑制模型把 QGIS 输出退化成 GEE API 的“情境泄漏”，并把 reward hacking 抑制指标移植为幻觉算子率抑制。

⚠️ **局限**：结论建立在合成/受控的 persona 行为实验（4 种行为、15 个上下文）上，是否可外推到复杂、长程、结构化的 GIS 工作流推理与跨平台算子映射仍未验证，且“默认 persona”在本课题中需要重新操作化为“共享 workflow 知识”，存在概念移植风险。

🚨 **为什么必须重视**：它与本课题核心科学问题正面撞车：都在问“在源情境学到的能力为何能/不能迁到目标情境”，只是它用 persona 层级作解释变量，而本课题用 GEE→QGIS 作实证场景。其 r=0.72 的定量规律、默认上下文预训练可拓宽后续迁移的发现，以及 PPR 抑制不希望泛化的手段，都可直接作为本课题的假设来源、训练配方与评测协议，属于必须立即精读并纳入对比基线的竞品动向。

### 🔥 ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Training Language Models To Be Coherent Decision-Makers
**8/10** · Khurram Yamin、Xavier Fernandes、Paul Koch、Bryan Wilder、Eric Horvitz · arXiv · 2026-10-06 · [原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09164v1) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.09164v1)
*入选：能力迁移*

> 📄 **摘要**：Reliable decision-making requires more than accurate prediction: a model must preserve its beliefs, apply the relevant utilities, and recognize when the information needed to justify an action is missing. We study whether language models can learn this decision procedure from supervised fine-tuning and generalize it across domains and differing natural-language expressions of the decision challenge. Across 20 datasets, we explore challenges of belief instability and decision-making errors by first eliciting probabilities of outcomes and then varying only the utilities and the framing of the decision problems, while holding the evidence fixed. We train models to preserve elicited beliefs while selecting the action that maximizes expected utility, and evaluate transfer to unseen application domains, held-out framings, and different classes of payoff structures. We further introduce incomplete-information settings in which required utilities are withheld and replaced with irrelevant text, testing whether models can distinguish missing decision-relevant information from merely additional context. We find that targeted fine-tuning substantially improves coherent decision-making and that in many situations, learning transfers across domains and framings to situations unobserved during training. Further, models trained for decidability learn to identify when action cannot be justified based on missing information. Finally, we show the value of a routed system that considers separately the recognition of decision completeness and utility-sensitive decision execution.

**一句话**：通过监督微调训练语言模型在决策中保持已诱导的信念、正确应用效用函数，并在信息缺失时判定不可决策，且能迁移到未见领域与未见表述框架。

痛点是可靠决策不止需要准确预测，还要保持信念一致、用对效用、并识别决策所需信息缺失。方法是在20个数据集上先诱导结果概率，再仅改变效用与问题表述框架、固定证据，用SFT训练模型保持信念并选期望效用最大的动作，同时构造以无关文本替换缺失效用的不完整信息设定。结果表明定向微调显著提升决策一致性，且在未见领域、held-out框架与不同收益结构上均有迁移，模型还学会识别信息缺失导致的不可决策，并展示了路由式系统的价值。

> 🔗 **与课题**：逐环节对照：③能力迁移最直接——论文核心实验即训练后跨领域、跨表述框架、跨收益结构迁移，并量化未见域与held-out framings上的保持情况，与课题GEE→QGIS的迁移保持率测量范式同构；①Workflow推理——把决策过程显式拆成“诱导信念→应用效用→选择动作→判定信息缺失”的可学习步骤序列，属于程序化推理能力的习得；⑥训练——定向SFT让模型内化该决策程序，与DAPT/SFT路径一致；⑦评测——设计了固定证据、只扰动效用与表述的控制变量评测，以及用无关文本替换缺失效用的分布外测试，可直接借鉴为迁移保持率与幻觉算子率/不可决策率指标；⑤结构化生成——其routed system按情况分派给不同专用组件的思路，对多模块、依赖闭合的processes+links图生成架构有参考价值。

💡 **启发**：借鉴其“固定证据、只扰动框架/效用/上下文”的受控迁移测评设计，构造GEE→QGIS迁移保持率与“依赖信息缺失时是否乱编算子”的检测协议；routed system的多专家路由可用于按任务类型分派到不同的QGIS grounding生成器。

⚠️ **局限**：任务形态以效用/信念的形式化决策问题为主，与工具调用、workflow依赖图生成仍有距离，其迁移结论未必可直接外推到QGIS processes+links的依赖闭合场景。

🚨 **为什么必须重视**：该文与课题③能力迁移环节正面撞车：同在LLM上用SFT学习程序化推理并做跨域/跨表述的迁移实证，其评测设计与迁移保持度量可直接作为我们GEE→QGIS迁移实验的对照协议乃至竞品基线；其“固定证据、只扰动框架与效用”的控制变量法，正是我们测量迁移保持率最干净的实验模板；其缺失信息下的不可决策检测思路也可直接改造成“幻觉算子率”评测构造，建议立即精读。

### 🔥 ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Selective Transfer of RL Updates for Visual Reasoning
**8/10** · Suxin Ji、Hungtao Wan、Mingjun Liu、An Zhang · arXiv · 2026-10-06 · [原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08659v1) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.08659v1)
*入选：能力迁移*

> 📄 **摘要**：Model merging provides a training-free way to transfer reasoning capabilities from language models to vision-language models (VLMs), but endpoint-based transfer can conflate pre-existing model differences with changes acquired during reasoning post-training. We instead formulate capability transfer around the training-stage update, isolating the parameter changes induced by reinforcement learning (RL). Yet transferring this update in full remains suboptimal: we find that its components differ substantially in cross-model transferability, with dominant directions transferring more effectively than the complete update. Based on this finding, we introduce Selective-RL, which isolates the RL-stage update, retains its dominant matrix-wise directions with magnitude preservation, and transfers them to the language modules of a VLM. Across three model families and five visual-reasoning benchmarks, Selective-RL improves full-update interpolation in 12 of 15 comparisons, including an 8.55 percentage-point MathVision gain on the Qwen recipient. Matched controls show that update magnitude or arbitrary low rank alone does not reproduce these gains. These results highlight a distinction between what is acquired during post-training and what remains transferable across models, providing a training-stage perspective on cross-model capability transfer. Code is available at https://anonymous.4open.science/r/selective-rl.

**一句话**：论文提出 Selective-RL，隔离强化学习阶段的参数更新并只保留其主导矩阵方向做保幅迁移，从而把推理能力从语言模型免训练地迁移到视觉语言模型。

痛点：基于端点（endpoint）的模型合并会把模型固有差异与后训练期间获得的变化混在一起，且整体迁移 RL 更新并非最优，因为更新内部各成分的跨模型可迁移性差异很大。方法：把能力迁移重新定义为围绕“训练阶段更新”的问题，隔离 RL 阶段产生的参数增量，保留其主导的矩阵级方向并保持幅度，迁移到 VLM 的语言模块。结果：在三个模型家族、五个视觉推理基准上，15 组比较中 12 组优于全量更新插值，Qwen 接收方在 MathVision 上提升 8.55 个百分点，匹配控制实验表明单纯幅度或任意低秩都无法复现增益。

> 🔗 **与课题**：逐环节对照：③ 能力迁移（最核心）——论文研究的正是源模型（LM）→目标模型（VLM）的跨模型能力迁移，且明确提出“后训练中获得的”与“可跨模型迁移的”之间存在区分，这与本课题“GEE workflow 学到的工作流知识能否迁移到 QGIS grounding”是同构命题，可直接借用其“选择性迁移 + transfer retention”思路。⑥ 训练/后训练——以 RL 阶段更新为迁移单元（隔离 stage-specific update、保幅保留主导方向），属于后训练产物如何被提取与复用，而非纯 RL 算法改进，符合“绑定推理/能力迁移场景”的高相关标准。⑦ 评测——跨三个模型家族、五个视觉推理基准的 12/15 胜出比较，并设置 magnitude-only 与 arbitrary low-rank 的匹配对照，是“迁移是否真的发生”的可复用评测协议与消融设计。①②④⑤ 未直接涉及（无任务分解、结构化/DAG 生成、工具算子 grounding）。总体而言它全文无 GIS 词汇，但属于基础模型上源域→目标域的迁移实证，按校准规则应判 8-10。

💡 **启发**：可把“只迁移 stage-specific update 的主导方向（保幅、低秩筛选）”直接搬到本课题：先在 GEE 工作流推理上做 SFT/RL 得到能力增量 Δ，再只迁移其主导方向到 QGIS grounding 模块，用 transfer retention 衡量增益，而非整体微调或端点合并；其 magnitude/low-rank 匹配控制设计可直接借用为迁移增益的归因实验。

⚠️ **局限**：迁移方向限于 LM→VLM 的视觉推理，模型合并式迁移是否适用于工具/算子 grounding 与结构化图生成任务、以及“主导方向”选取对模型家族与任务分布的敏感性，均需额外验证。

🚨 **为什么必须重视**：它正撞上本课题的核心命题——把“能力迁移”从端点式整体迁移改写为训练阶段更新的选择性迁移，并给出“习得 vs 可迁移”的区分，与本课题的 transfer retention（迁移保持率）指标同源，可立即对标。其免训练、保幅主导方向的迁移协议和 12/15 匹配对照评测框架，可直接用于设计 GEE→QGIS 的能力迁移实验与基线。若不做跟踪，相关贡献框架可能被他人先用于 tool/agent 场景的能力迁移叙事。

### ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Training Advisors for LLM Agents from Task Outcomes
**7/10** · Sergei Polezhaev、Barys Liskavets、Ori Press、Alexander Golubev · arXiv · 2026-10-07 · [原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09858v1) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.09858v1)
*入选：能力迁移*

> 📄 **摘要**：Large language model agents tackle multi-step tasks by interleaving reasoning and tool calls with observations from the environment. Prior work has shown that natural-language feedback can help these agents revise their decisions during task execution. We introduce Caddie, a method for training critics to provide natural-language analysis and advice as agents work through a task. Unlike approaches that rely on step-level labels or reference critiques, Caddie learns from whether the agent ultimately succeeds after receiving the critic's feedback. We optimize the critic through reinforcement learning while keeping the base model frozen. Trained on multi-hop question answering with a single base model, our Qwen3-4B critic improves success rates across four base models of different scales and architectures, including three not used during critic training. On the MuSiQue benchmark, the trained critic improves Qwen3-4B's success rate by more than 25 percentage points, surpassing the performance of Kimi K3 without a critic. The same critic also yields gains on out-of-domain interactive benchmarks, including $τ^3$ and DeepDive, with no additional training. Our results show that agents can decide when to seek help from a critic at inference time and that outcome-based critic training can produce guidance that transfers across base models and task domains.

**一句话**：提出 Caddie：以任务最终成败为奖励、通过强化学习在一个冻结基座上训练"评论家/顾问"模型，让它在多步工具调用任务中向 agent 提供自然语言分析与建议。

痛点：多步 agent 需要过程性反馈来修正决策，但步级标注或参考评论代价高昂且难获取。方法：把 agent 收到 critic 建议后最终是否成功作为唯一奖励信号，用 RL 训练 critic（基座冻结），推理时由 agent 自行决定何时求助 critic。结果：在 MuSiQue 上使 Qwen3-4B 成功率提升 25+ 个百分点、超过无 critic 的 Kimi K3，且同一 critic 零额外训练迁移到 4 个不同规模/架构的基座模型及 τ³、DeepDive 等域外交互基准。

> 🔗 **与课题**：主要支撑③与⑦，兼及⑥。③能力迁移：critic 仅在单一基座（Qwen3-4B）+ 单一任务（多跳 QA）上训练，却能迁移到 3 个未参与训练的基座模型和多个域外交互基准，是一次典型的"源域→目标域"迁移保持率实证，可作为 GEE→QGIS 跨平台迁移评测设计的类比范式（训练域单一、目标域多样、按成功率增量度量保持率）。⑦评测：以成功率绝对增量、跨模型/跨域零样本迁移增益作为指标，并做了跨 3 个异构基座的对照，属于可复用的迁移评测协议。⑥训练/后训练：用任务终局结果（outcome-based）而非步级标签做 reward 的 RL 训练，且基座冻结，只训练辅助模块——这一"结果奖励 + 冻结主干"的做法可移植到"以 QGIS 图执行成功为 reward"训练工作流生成顾问。①/②仅弱相关：critic 对多步工具调用流程给出过程性纠偏建议，但全文不涉及任务分解为 DAG、依赖组织或结构化 processes+links 生成，也未触及算子 grounding。

💡 **启发**：可直接偷两点：一是用"下游任务最终成败"作为唯一 reward 训练顾问模块，绕过昂贵的步级/过程标注；二是把"跨基座模型 + 跨任务域零样本迁移增益"做成标准评测维度，用于量化 QGIS 工作流知识的迁移保持率。

⚠️ **局限**：迁移的对象是外挂 critic 的自然语言建议而非模型内部工作流知识，且多跳 QA 的动作空间与依赖结构同 GIS 工作流差异较大，其迁移结论外推到 QGIS 算子落地需额外验证。

### ⭐⭐⭐⭐ A Systematic Study of Small Language Models on Abstract Reasoning Tasks
**7/10** · Nur A Zarin Nishat、Jens Lehmann、Andrei Aioanei、Sahar Vahdati · arXiv · 2026-10-06 · [原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08680v1) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.08680v1)
*入选：能力迁移*

> 📄 **摘要**：Endpoint accuracy on abstract-reasoning benchmarks does not reveal whether a language model has acquired a transferable rule or fit distribution-specific regularities. We study this distinction in small language models on the ARC-TGI benchmark, which organizes abstract grid transformations into controllable task families and supports resampling, spatial shifts, and cross-benchmark transfer. Across more than 1,000 runs, we profile decoder-only, encoder--decoder, and mixture-of-experts model families under supervised fine-tuning. We examine the efficiency and stability of skill acquisition, robustness beyond the training distribution, interactions with model family and task formulation, and layer-wise attention signatures that accompany behavioral differences. ### ⭐⭐⭐ Self-Evolve With a Reference:Anchored Training of Tool-Integrated Agents
**6/10** · Wenjie Liao、Liangjie Zhao、Zehong Cao · arXiv · 2026-10-07 · [原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09856v1) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.09856v1)
*入选：RL×LLM方法*

> 📄 **摘要**：Self-evolving tool-integrated agents learn from tasks and feedback generated within their own training loop. A Curriculum Agent generates tasks, while an Executor Agent learns from self-consistency signals through reinforcement learning. However, relying solely on the current Executor for feedback has two limitations: group-relative advantages vanish under full consensus, while uncertainty-based curriculum rewards favor disagreement without showing whether the generated tasks support further learning. These limitations motivate an additional reference beyond the current Executor. We propose \textit{AnchorLoop}, which introduces a frozen copy of the previous iteration's Executor as a historical reference and reuses it on both sides of the training loop. For the Executor, the anchor provides a cross-reference advantage that evaluates current outputs against both current and historical majority answers. For the Curriculum, it provides an agreement-based reference based on differences in sampled majority agreement. Since the Executor and anchor have identical parameters during Curriculum training, this comparison serves as a proxy for task selection rather than evidence of inter-version improvement or correctness. Across 13 reasoning benchmarks, AnchorLoop improves over Agent0 by 2.5\% on mathematical reasoning and 2.8\% on general reasoning tasks. It also maintains higher effective-advantage variance and continues improving in later iterations as the unanchored baseline shows diminishing gains. These results demonstrate the benefit of introducing a lightweight historical reference into self-evolving tool-integrated agents without external task or answer supervision.

**一句话**：提出 AnchorLoop：在自进化工具集成智能体训练中引入上一轮 Executor 的冻结副本作为历史锚点，同时改进 Executor 的交叉参照优势与 Curriculum 的任务选择信号。

痛点：纯自进化（如 Agent0）仅靠当前 Executor 提供反馈，群体相对优势在全票一致时消失，而基于不确定性的课程奖励只偏好分歧、不判断任务是否有益。方法：冻结上一轮迭代的 Executor 作为锚点，在训练回路两侧复用——为 Executor 提供对标当前与历史多数答案的交叉参照优势，为 Curriculum 提供基于多数一致度差异的参照信号。结果：在 13 个推理基准上，数学推理比 Agent0 提升 2.5%、通用推理提升 2.8%，且有效优势方差更高、后期迭代仍持续改进。
