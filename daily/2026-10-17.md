### ⭐⭐⭐ DocPilot: Interactive Agent Orchestration and Training for Long Visual Document Understanding
**6/10** · Zhuowen Liang、Xiaotian Lin、Yuyu Luo、Haixun Wang、Nan Tang · arXiv (Cornell University) · 2026-10-15 · [原文](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2610.21327)
*入选：能力迁移*

> 📄 **摘要**：Multimodal long-document question answering requires models to seek sparse evidence across pages and modalities, understand heterogeneous document content, and reason over the collected evidence. Even advanced vision-language models (VLMs) can overlook sparse evidence and struggle with cross-modal reasoning in a single pass. Existing document agents further introduce multi-step interaction, but some still rely on predefined workflows or coarse-grained actions, limiting the flexibility and granularity of document reasoning. We introduce DocPilot, an agentic document framework that supports expressive orchestration and makes complex document solving learnable by small language models (SLMs). DocPilot orchestrates evidence seeking through page-level navigation and fine-grained evidence localization, and integrates local visual inspection, code-based numerical reasoning, and cross-modal reasoning for multimodal understanding. To transfer these capabilities to small models, we propose curriculum-based agentic training that progresses from single-ability learning to adaptive orchestration through supervision from data-grounded reasoning traces and executable interaction trajectories. Across MMLongBench-Doc, DocBench, FinRAGBench-V, and MADQA, DocPilot achieves strong gains with frontier VLM backbones, while DocPilot-4B delivers competitive performance against substantially larger models, demonstrating strong effectiveness and generalization across diverse long-document reasoning settings. The code is available at https://github.com/HKUSTDial/DocPilot.

**一句话**：DocPilot 是一个面向长篇多模态文档理解的智能体框架；它通过页级导航和细粒度证据定位，编排视觉检查、代码数值推理与跨模态推理，并用课程式智能体训练把这种编排能力迁移到小模型。

痛点：长文档多模态问答证据稀疏，VLM 单次推理容易漏证据、跨模态推理弱；已有文档智能体依赖预定义流程或粗粒度动作，灵活性与粒度不足。方法：DocPilot 用页面级导航、细粒度证据定位、局部视觉检查与代码化数值推理组成可学习的智能体编排，并通过从单能力到自适应编排的课程式训练，利用数据接地的推理轨迹和可执行交互轨迹监督小模型。结果：在 MMLongBench-Doc、DocBench、FinRAGBench-V、MADQA 上，前沿 VLM 主干带来显著提升，DocPilot-4B 可与更大模型竞争，展示泛化性。

> 🔗 **与课题**：支撑⑥训练/后训练：论文核心是课程式智能体训练，从单能力学习逐步过渡到自适应编排，用数据接地的推理轨迹和可执行交互轨迹做监督，这与课题关心的“工作流能力如何被训练进模型”直接对应，可作为 SFT/轨迹监督式训练设计的参考。也涉及①工作流推理/任务分解：其自适应编排需要模型自行决定导航、取证、代码计算与跨模态推理的步骤顺序，而不是走预定义 workflow，这为“任务分解+步骤规划”提供了非 GIS 域的实证样例。还涉及③能力迁移：把大模型或专家轨迹上的编排能力迁移到 4B 小模型，属于“强模型能力→弱模型”或“源域→目标域”的迁移设定，可用作迁移保持率讨论的类比。④工具/算子落地只到“代码化数值推理”和交互动作层面，不是 GIS operator grounding，也没有结构化图生成（⑤）或显式依赖/DAG 建模（②），因此不触及课题的 QGIS processes+links 核心。评测维度上，它提供了长文档多步推理的任务级评测，可借鉴其轨迹可执行性与端到端成功率的度量思路，但缺少幻觉算子率、结构正确性等指标。

💡 **启发**：可借鉴“课程式智能体训练”：先分能力监督，例如导航、取证、代码计算，再做自适应编排；并用“可执行交互轨迹”而非纯自然语言推理链作为监督信号。另一个可借鉴点是将代码执行作为工具落地的一种可验证动作，用于构造执行成功/失败的训练与评测信号。

⚠️ **局限**：面向长文档多模态问答，其编排动作空间与工具集合是文档领域专用，且未涉及结构化工作流图或跨平台算子映射，迁移到 GIS/QGIS 场景需要重新定义动作空间与 grounding 监督。

### ⭐⭐ Neos: Learning to Interleave Search and Visual Reasoning
**5/10** · Ekko He、Jack Hong、Shilin Yan、Xintong Zhang、Yujie Zhang、Wenjie Li · arXiv (Cornell University) · 2026-10-12 · [原文](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2610.17043)
*入选：RL×LLM方法*

> 📄 **摘要**：Recent multimodal deep search agents have advanced tool use and visual content retrieval, yet they still struggle to convert intermediate visual evidence into actionable guidance for subsequent search, limiting their effectiveness in long-horizon settings. To address this gap, we present Neos, a multimodal deep search framework that learns both how to acquire evidence and how to use it to guide subsequent decisions. To make this coupling learnable, Neos constructs training tasks with intermediate visual dependencies while filtering out textual shortcuts, and introduces a lightweight, trainable Multimodal Evidence Interface (MEI) that transforms retrieved content into goal-conditioned observations. At its core, an agent-environment co-evolution procedure treats MEI as a co-adaptive component of this loop. Specifically, reference-guided credit assignment uses verified successful trajectories to localize critical policy errors while preserving credit for useful decisions in failed searches, whereas rollout feedback selects informative local evidence-processing interactions for MEI supervised fine-tuning (SFT). By alternating policy reinforcement learning (RL) with MEI SFT, Neos allows the policy's evolving evidence requests to shape MEI training and the adapted MEI to reshape subsequent observations, closing the loop between evidence acquisition and evidence-conditioned action. Across ten benchmarks, Neos-9B and Neos-35B demonstrate leading performance among specialized multimodal deep-search agents.

**一句话**：提出 Neos 多模态深度搜索框架，通过可训练的 Multimodal Evidence Interface（MEI）与策略强化学习交替共演化，学习交错检索与视觉推理。

痛点是多模态搜索智能体难以把中间视觉证据转化为对后续检索的可执行指导，长程任务效果受限。方法上构造带中间视觉依赖、过滤文本捷径的训练任务，引入轻量可训练 MEI 把检索内容转成目标条件观测，并用参考引导的信用分配加 rollout 反馈，交替进行策略 RL 与 MEI 的 SFT。结果在十个基准上 Neos-9B/35B 在专用多模态模型中领先。

> 🔗 **与课题**：主要支撑环节⑥（训练/后训练：策略 RL 与证据接口 SFT 交替的共演化训练范式）与⑦（十个基准上的评测协议与错误定位式信用分配）；对①有弱支撑——它研究的是长程交错“检索—推理”步骤规划与证据条件化决策，但粒度是搜索动作而非可分解的工作流/流程规划。与②④⑤基本无关：全文没有结构化流程/DAG 生成，也没有把自然语言步骤落地到具体算子（operator grounding）的图结构生成。也不涉及③跨平台/跨工具能力迁移的实证。

💡 **启发**：“参考引导的信用分配 + rollout 反馈筛选高信息量交互”可借用为工作流推理的细粒度训练信号：只用成功轨迹定位关键决策点，同时在失败轨迹中保留对有用步骤的信用，正好对应我们评测中的依赖闭合与算子幻觉定位。MEI 式“可训练中间接口”也提示可把 QGIS 算子 grounding 层做成与策略共同演化的适配组件，而不是冻结的显式 L3 语义层。

⚠️ **局限**：领域是多模态视觉搜索而非 GIS/GEE→QGIS 工作流，实验不涉及算子级落地与图结构可执行性，迁移到本课题需自行改造其证据接口与信用分配粒度。

### ⭐⭐ The belief-action gap in language model agents
**5/10** · Ayse Sila Okcu、Weisheng Wang、Tongmeng Xie、Niall Dalton、Angelos Nalmpantis、Raghu Arghal · arXiv (Cornell University) · 2026-10-12 · [原文](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2610.17444)
*入选：RL×LLM方法*

> 📄 **摘要**：Language model (LM) agents can fail for different reasons, requiring different explanations and interventions. We study how state beliefs, action values, and action preferences interact as LM agents reason and commit to decisions in text-rendered grid worlds. We estimate task-relevant beliefs using report-based and representation-based probes, and action values using inverse reinforcement learning. We find that task-relevant state and value information is often represented more reliably than it is reflected in behaviour. Tracking action preferences throughout reasoning further shows that decisions sharpen around a commitment point and reported belief uncertainty decreases near commitment, although individual belief changes do not consistently explain shifts in action preference. Finally, forced actions follow reasoning prefixes from other decisions when transplanted, whereas the tested reasoning-window activation edits provide limited control over the action. Together, these results suggest that some failures arise not from missing task-relevant information, but from how available information is translated into action. Our measurements distinguish information availability from belief-action consistency without by themselves identifying the cause of each failure. Our code is available at: https://anonymous.4open.science/r/belief-action-gap-F745/

**一句话**：在文本网格世界中用报告式/表征式探针与逆强化学习测量LM智能体的状态信念与行动价值，揭示「知行差距」：任务相关信息在内部表征中比在行为中更可靠。

痛点：LM智能体失败原因各异，需要区分「信息缺失」与「信息到行动的转化失败」两类失败。方法：在文本化网格世界中用report-based与representation-based探针估计任务相关信念、用逆强化学习估计行动价值，并全程追踪行动偏好、承诺点与强制动作移植、推理窗口激活编辑。结果：状态与价值信息常被更可靠地表征而非体现在行为中，决策在承诺点附近收敛、信念不确定性下降，但强制动作可被其他决策的推理前缀带动，而激活编辑对行动控制有限。

> 🔗 **与课题**：弱支撑环节⑦评测：提供「信息可用性 vs 知行一致性」的失败归因测量协议（探针+IRL+承诺点追踪），可用于区分工作流推理失败是知识缺失还是落地断层；亦与①有间接关联（推理过程中决策收敛/commitment的现象学）。但不涉及工作流分解、结构化/DAG生成、工具/算子接地与跨域迁移。

💡 **启发**：可借用其「表征探针 vs 行为表现」的对照范式，设计实验检验「模型内部已具备可迁移工作流知识，但在QGIS算子接地上未体现」这一假设，即把transfer retention与grounding failure做操作性区分。

⚠️ **局限**：实验局限于玩具级文本网格世界与决策承诺现象，不含真实工具/API调用与结构化输出，外部效度有限，作者也自认未识别失败的具体因果。

**▸ 建议：略读**

### ⭐⭐ X-Ragent: Tool-Augmented Reinforcement Learning for Evidence-Grounded Chest X-Ray Reasoning
**5/10** · Yi Wang、Zhenlong Yuan、Yuchan Guo、Maolei Huang、Zijian Song、Dapeng Zhang · arXiv (Cornell University) · 2026-10-12 · [原文](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2610.16811)
*入选：RL×LLM方法*

> 📄 **摘要**：Chest X-ray (CXR) interpretation often requires integrating visual findings with task-specific evidence rather than relying on direct visual recognition alone. While tool-augmented medical agents can supply such evidence via external modules, they often lack explicit mechanisms for learning when auxiliary evidence is needed and for preventing inapplicable quantitative evidence from entering diagnostic reasoning. To address this, we propose X-Ragent, a post-training framework for tool-augmented reinforcement learning that decouples learned auxiliary-evidence seeking from validity-gated quantitative measurement acquisition for evidence-grounded CXR reasoning. We construct Rad-CoT, a 7.7K-sample corpus combining causally ordered tool-augmented trajectories with report-style diagnostic reasoning, and use it to initialize Qwen3-VL-8B via agentic supervised fine-tuning (SFT). We then optimize the policy with Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) using an accuracy-gated reward that applies process-level terms for measurement consistency and auxiliary-tool cost only to correct trajectories, preventing plausible process behavior from compensating for an incorrect diagnosis. Across three CXR benchmarks, X-Ragent achieves the strongest results, including an 8.6-point improvement on the fully held-out ChestAgentBench. By seeking evidence selectively and admitting only valid measurements, X-Ragent supports more faithful and evidence-grounded CXR reasoning.

**一句话**：提出 X-Ragent 后训练框架：用 agentic SFT + GRPO（带准确性门控的过程奖励）训练胸部X光诊断agent，学会在需要时主动调用工具取证据、并屏蔽无效的量化测量。

痛点：工具增强的医学agent缺少“何时需要外部证据”的学习机制，也缺少阻止无效定量证据进入推理的门控。方法：构建7.7K条因果有序的工具增强轨迹 Rad-CoT，先做 agentic SFT，再用 GRPO 优化，奖励只对诊断正确的轨迹施加过程级一致性/工具成本项。结果：三个CXR基准最优，完全留出的 ChestAgentBench 提升8.6点。

> 🔗 **与课题**：与课题相关但非核心：偏医学影像应用，不涉及 GIS workflow reasoning，也未做跨域/跨平台能力迁移。可支撑的点：(a) ⑥训练——给出“过程级奖励只施加在结果正确的轨迹上”的可复用后训练配方，直接对应工作流推理训练中如何避免过程看似合理却掩盖错误终态；(b) ⑦评测——用准确性门控把结果正确性与过程合规性解耦，可作为 workflow reasoning 质量与执行成功率的联合指标设计参考；(c) 弱关联 ④grounding——对辅助工具证据做有效性门控（拒绝不可用的量化结果），思路可类比“幻觉算子率/无效算子注入”的抑制机制。但工具是医学测量模块，而非 QGIS operator grounding，也不研究内部工作流知识的迁移保持。

💡 **启发**：可直接借用“accuracy-gated process reward”：只在终态正确（QGIS 图可执行且结构依赖闭合）的样本上叠加过程项（步骤顺序、依赖闭合、工具成本），防止模型刷过程分；另可借“有效性门控”思想做 QGIS 算子参数合法性校验，作为幻觉算子率指标的执行侧过滤器。

⚠️ **局限**：任务域为胸部X光诊断、工具集为医学专用模块，其“证据寻求”与工作流分解/依赖组织不是同一能力，迁移到 QGIS 图生成需重新论证；且未做任何跨平台迁移实验。

### ⭐⭐⭐ LLM4GIS: Methods, Applications, and Prospects
**6/10** · Huayi Wu、Lutong Xie、Shuyang Hou、Zhangxiao Shen、Haoyue Jiao、Shaowen Wu · ISPRS International Journal of Geo-Information · 2026-10-16 · [原文](https://doi.org/10.3390/ijgi15100471)
*入选：作者跟踪: 侯舒扬 Hou Shuyang*

> 📄 **摘要**：The rapid advancement of large language models (LLMs) offers new avenues for overcoming the limitations of current data-driven GIS analysis methods, giving rise to the emerging field of large language model-driven GIS analysis (LLM4GIS). This study presents a systematic review of 673 relevant research articles published between 2022 and July 2026. By tracing the five-stage evolutionary trajectory of GIS analysis methods and elaborating the framework for LLM4GIS, we conduct a quantitative bibliometric analysis that reveals the field’s rapid growth from sporadic exploration to explosive expansion, along with the shifting landscape of its dominant technical trajectories. Building on these analyses, we develop a taxonomy spanning four dimensions—technical approaches, datasets, applications, and evaluation—and systematically analyze the advances and evaluation methodologies across five core application areas: geospatial knowledge question answering, knowledge extraction, spatiotemporal reasoning, geospatial code generation, and analysis modeling. Key challenges and future research directions are further discussed along five dimensions: multimodal data integration, high-quality data development, domain-specific model development, integration of world models with real-world geospatial environments, and interpretability and trustworthiness. This review provides a systematic reference for advancing the bidirectional synergy between LLM4GIS and GIS4LLM and for fostering a new generation of spatiotemporal intelligence ecosystems.

**一句话**：对2022—2026年673篇LLM4GIS文献做系统综述，提出技术路线、数据集、应用、评测四维分类体系，并覆盖地理空间代码生成与时空推理等五大应用方向。

痛点：LLM驱动的GIS分析（LLM4GIS）快速扩张但缺乏系统梳理，技术路线与评测方法分散。方法：基于673篇文献的计量分析与五阶段演化轨迹梳理，构建技术/数据/应用/评测四维taxonomy，重点分析地理空间知识问答、知识抽取、时空推理、地理空间代码生成、分析建模五类应用。结果：揭示领域从零星探索到爆发式增长的态势，总结挑战（多模态集成、高质量数据、领域模型、世界模型、可解释可信）与未来方向。
