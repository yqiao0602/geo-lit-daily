### ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Reasoning Patterns for Efficient AI Coding: An Empirical Study
**7/10** · Zhe Yin、Yuling Shi、Xiaodong Gu、Beijun Shen · arXiv · 2026-10-26 · [原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34943v1) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.34943v1)
*入选：领域×任务*

> 📄 **摘要**：Large Reasoning Models (LRMs) generate long reasoning traces that expose intermediate inference behavior, offering opportunities to study and control reasoning in AI coding. However, the underlying patterns in these traces and their potential for analyzing and guiding coding models remain poorly understood, limiting systematic characterization and inference-time control of LRM reasoning. We conduct an empirical study of reasoning patterns across four representative coding tasks. We transform reasoning traces into structured action sequences and mine recurring fragments using within-task support, cross-task contrasts, and valid--invalid support contrasts. We characterize four general reasoning patterns by coding agents: design-to-implementation for code generation, state tracing for execution reasoning, evidence--fix--recheck for debugging, and semantic mapping for translation. We further identify positive and negative patterns associated with valid and invalid reasoning traces, respectively. Positive patterns reflect verification-driven convergence, whereas negative patterns reflect repeated design revision, reflection, and knowledge recall without closure. To demonstrate their practical utility, we evaluate three pattern-based applications: task identification, pattern-guided prompting, and pattern-aware early stopping. Features capturing general reasoning patterns enable accurate task identification from reasoning traces without accessing task descriptions or final answers. Prompting guided by positive and negative patterns improves the overall accuracy of downstream tasks, while negative pattern-aware early stopping substantially reduces API-reported token usage with only a modest decrease in pass@1. All three applications also generalize to previously unseen models, showing that reasoning patterns can support both the analysis and control of LRM behavior in AI coding.

**一句话**：该实证研究把大型推理模型在编码任务中的推理轨迹转成结构化动作序列，挖掘出四类通用推理模式与正负模式，并用于任务识别、模式引导提示和提前停止。

痛点：LRM 的编码推理轨迹冗长且缺乏系统刻画，难以在推理期进行控制或质量分析。方法：覆盖四类代表性编码任务，把 trace 转成结构化动作序列，用任务内支持度、跨任务对比与有效—无效对比挖掘反复出现的片段，归纳出 design-to-implementation、state tracing、evidence-fix-recheck、semantic mapping 四种模式。结果：正模式表现为验证驱动的收敛，负模式表现为反复设计修改、反思、知识召回而无收敛；基于模式特征可实现任务识别、提示引导和提前停止。

> 🔗 **与课题**：主要支撑环节①与⑦，并部分触及②。①：它给出的四类编码推理模式（design-to-implementation、state tracing、debug 的 evidence-fix-recheck）本质上就是任务分解与步骤规划的微观形态，可作为衡量 workflow reasoning 结构的分析维度。②：把自由文本 trace 规约为结构化动作序列，并用支持度和对比度挖掘可复用片段，这套表示与挖掘协议可直接迁移到 workflow 结构（步骤、依赖）的表征与模式抽取。⑦：有效—无效 trace 对比以及“负模式=反复修改/反思/召回而不收敛”的刻画，为工作流推理质量与幻觉/不闭合的自动识别提供了可操作的错误分析指标，也可作为早停信号。训练与迁移环节（③⑥）不涉及。

💡 **启发**：把“反复反思、反复改设计但不收敛”作为负模式特征，可以做成 QGIS 落地阶段的判定信号：如果生成的 process 链在推理中出现设计反复却没有验证闭环，就高度提示依赖不闭合或幻觉算子。同时可借用其 trace→动作序列→片段支持度统计的流水线，把模型在 GEE workflow 上的推理轨迹整理成可比较的结构化片段库。

⚠️ **局限**：研究聚焦编码任务的推理轨迹模式，没有涉及工具/算子落地、跨平台迁移，也没有 GIS 或结构化图生成实验，模式分类与结论的跨任务泛化性尚待验证。

### 🔥 ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Pretraining Knows What, Simulation Teaches How: Compositional Generalization in a Sim-Trained Robot Agent
**8/10** · Benjamin Pikus、Daniel Saunders、Mircea Mironenco、Salman Mohammadi、Mathew Mathew、Sirou Zhu · arXiv · 2026-10-26 · [原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35817v1) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.35817v1)
*入选：能力迁移*

> 📄 **摘要**：A robot policy needs to understand what a task is asking for in a scene, and how to move the robot to accomplish that task. To achieve these two skills, we introduce Zero-real Agent Policy (ZAP), a recipe that teaches a small off-the-shelf vision-language model to control a robot via a simple tool harness. We use a pretrained 27B parameter model to bootstrap world understanding and reasoning, followed by supervised fine-tuning and GRPO on simple simulated tasks to teach it how to move the robot. Without any real-world robot data during training, ZAP reaches 96.7% success on real-world tasks, matching GPT-6 Astra in our harness and outperforming the MolmoAct2 VLA (33.3%), while running 3x faster and 17x cheaper than GPT-6 Astra. We demonstrate that ZAP also generalizes to completely new objects, goals, strategies, and language by seating a test tube in a rack (and choosing a different hole in the rack when prompted), handing a test tube between arms mid-air, drawing an X on a tic-tac-toe board, and playing chess. These goals and types of manipulation don't appear in our training data. Furthermore, ZAP also transfers to an unseen embodiment. On a single-arm Franka, ZAP reaches 55.9% on RoboLab-120, 4.9 percentage points ahead of the leading VLA/WAM baseline.

**一句话**：提出 ZAP：用预训练 27B VLM 承担“理解任务是什么”，再用仿真任务上的 SFT+GRPO 与简单工具 harness 教它“怎么执行”，实现零真机数据的机器人策略并迁移到新目标与新本体。

痛点在于机器人策略既要理解场景与任务意图（what），又要掌握动作执行（how），而真机数据昂贵。ZAP 以小型现成视觉语言模型为基座，先用预训练知识引导世界理解与推理，再在简单仿真任务上做监督微调与 GRPO，并通过工具 harness 输出控制。无任何真机训练数据即在真实任务上达 96.7% 成功率，并在全新物体、目标、策略、语言组合以及未见过的单臂 Franka 本体上展现出组合泛化与跨本体迁移。

> 🔗 **与课题**：直接支撑③能力迁移：论文的核心命题是“预训练提供 what 的语义理解、仿真训练教会 how 的执行”，这正是源域（仿真）→目标域（真机）、本体→本体的能力迁移实证，与 GEE workflow 知识迁移到 QGIS grounding 的“知识可迁移、落地需训练”假设同构；同时给出组合泛化（新物体/新目标/新策略/新语言）上的迁移保持率证据。支撑④工具落地：模型通过简单 tool harness 输出可执行动作，相当于 operator grounding 的接口约束设计。支撑⑥训练：预训练+SFT+GRPO 的三段式配方，明确区分“保留预训练语义”与“后训练注入技能”，可作为我们 DAPT/SFT 与偏好优化阶段的配方参照。支撑⑦评测：用成功率、未见任务泛化、跨本体迁移、成本/时延对比构建多维评测协议，可类比我们的迁移保持率与执行成功率指标。此外其“不依赖显式中间语义层、直接从感知到工具调用”的做法，间接为②⑤的结构化中间表示必要性提供反例对照。

💡 **启发**：可借用其“what 靠预训练、how 靠仿真后训练”的分离式训练配方与 harness 设计：把显式 L3 语义层替换为工具接口约束，并设计“训练分布外的新目标/新本体”测试集来量化迁移保持率。

⚠️ **局限**：任务是机器人连续控制，与 GIS 工作流的离散图结构生成差异较大，其对“结构正确性/依赖闭合”不提供直接证据，迁移结论需谨慎外推。

🚨 **为什么必须重视**：该文与我们的核心假设正面撞车又互为佐证：它证明“预训练知识+仿真后训练”可在无目标域数据下完成跨域、跨本体落地，若我们仍主张依赖显式 L3 语义中间层，将缺乏竞争力。其 harness+SFT+GRPO 配方与分布外迁移评测协议可直接搬用到 GEE→QGIS 的迁移实验设计上。

### 🔥 ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Hierarchical Skill Evolution for Coding Agent
**8/10** · Jun Gao、Yun Peng、Changhai Zhou、Yuhan Zhu、Xiangyu Zeng、Shiyang Zhang Yuhua Zhou · arXiv · 2026-10-26 · [原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35616v1) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.35616v1)
*入选：能力迁移*

> 📄 **摘要**：Skills provide agents with reusable experience to guide future decisions without modifying the underlying model. However, coding trajectories are often entangled with task- and repository-specific contexts, making it challenging to distill procedural skills that generalize across diverse coding environments. To address this challenge, we present Hierarchical Skill Evolution (HSE), which progressively distills procedural knowledge from coding trajectories through hierarchical evidence aggregation. HSE first extracts task-level evidence from individual trajectories, then aggregates recurring evidence within the same repository to form repository-specific skill candidates, and finally consolidates consistent patterns across repositories into transferable skills across repositories. The evolution is driven by an external writer-evaluator loop that generates and assesses skills while keeping the underlying coding model frozen. The loop externally updates the writer and evaluator policies to improve subsequent skill generation and assessment. This hierarchy progressively filters task-specific details while preserving procedures that generalize across diverse coding environments. Successful trajectories yield reusable guidance for future tasks, while failed trajectories reveal recurring failure modes that guide avoidance and recovery. We evaluate HSE on SWE-bench Verified and DeepSWE, using GLM-5.2 across Pi and Claude Code harnesses and GPT-5.5 and Opus 4.8 with Pi. Across both benchmarks, 10 skills evolved solely from SWE-Gym improve coding performance across all evaluated settings, demonstrating transfer across benchmarks, coding models, and agent harnesses. We further evolve the skill library from target-benchmark trajectories without verifier feedback, relying solely on the learned evaluator for candidate assessment and achieving additional gains through benchmark-tailored skills.

**一句话**：HSE 通过“任务级→仓库级→跨仓库级”的分层证据聚合，从编码轨迹中逐步蒸馏出可跨环境迁移的过程性技能，并用外部的 writer-evaluator 循环维护技能库，基础模型全程冻结。

痛点：编码轨迹高度依赖具体任务和仓库语境，模型由此学到的过程性技能难以泛化，因此很难迁移到新的编码环境。方法：HSE 先抽取单条轨迹的任务级证据，再把同一仓库中反复出现的证据聚合成仓库级技能候选，最后把多个仓库间一致的模式固化为可迁移技能；整个进化过程由 writer-evaluator 外部闭环驱动，成功轨迹提供可复用指引，失败轨迹则给出反复出现的失败模式。结果：在 SWE-bench Verified 与 DeepSWE 上，用 GLM-5.2（Pi/Claude Code harness）以及 GPT-5.5、Opus 4.8 验证了分层技能进化的有效性。

> 🔗 **与课题**：主要支撑环节③（能力迁移）：论文的核心命题与课题一致，即能否把在源环境学到的过程性知识迁移到目标环境；对应关系为“仓库级→跨仓库级”的泛化。环节②（Workflow→结构化表示）：它把轨迹提炼为分层的、可复用的过程性技能表示，并按任务、仓库、跨仓库三个层级组织，这是一种显式外部结构化表征。环节⑦（评测）：它用 SWE-bench/DeepSWE 的分环境对比来衡量迁移保持情况，并把失败轨迹系统性地归为反复出现的失败模式。环节⑥（训练/后训练）作为对照：它刻意保持模型冻结、以外部技能库替代参数更新，因此与 DAPT/SFT 形成对照组。注意：它不涉及 GIS 与 DAG/依赖图生成，所以与①④⑤没有直接关系。

💡 **启发**：可以直接借鉴两点：第一，它的分层证据聚合（单轨迹→同环境→跨环境）可以作为衡量“GEE 学到的 workflow 能否跨到 QGIS”的迁移协议模板，关键是统计跨环境一致的模式，而非单例匹配；第二，成功/失败轨迹的双向挖掘可用于构造“幻觉算子率、依赖断裂”的错误模式库，并反过来喂给 writer，逐步积累成技能库。

⚠️ **局限**：技能以外置库形式存在，没有内化为模型参数化能力，且评测只看补丁通过率，缺乏对生成结构正确性、依赖闭合或图可用率的度量。

🚨 **为什么必须重视**：这是与本课题核心科学问题直接竞争的路线：它用“不修改模型，只进化外置技能库”回答同一问题，即过程性知识能否跨环境迁移；这既可能成为强基线，也可能被审稿人质询“为什么不外置技能”。其分层聚合与 writer-evaluator 闭环可以直接迁移到 GEE→QGIS 的迁移保持率实验设计上。

### ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Linear Probes Transfer from Base to Distilled Language Models despite Rotation
**7/10** · Marios Tsatsos · arXiv · 2026-10-26 · [原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35586v1) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.35586v1)
*入选：能力迁移*

> 📄 **摘要**：Linear probes, classifiers that read a language model's internal activations, are widely used to detect harmful requests. They are cheap to train and run, and an auditor who has trained one would like to reuse it when the model is replaced by a successor rather than retrain it for every release. We test how well probes can be reused. For four base models (Qwen2.5-Math-7B, Llama-3.1-8B, Qwen2.5-14B, Qwen2.5-32B) we train a probe on the base model with the XSTest prompt set and apply it, unchanged, to two successors of that base: the DeepSeek-R1 distilled reasoning model and the developer's instruction-tuned chat model. We find that the successors rotate the direction separating unsafe from safe prompts by 17$^\circ$ to 35$^\circ$, yet on XSTest the transferred probe's AUC changes by only $-0.011$ to $+0.027$, and on an independent OR-Bench evaluation, never used for training, by $-0.015$ to $+0.053$. On XSTest, whose safe prompts deliberately contain alarming words, the probes beat a classifier that sees only the prompt text by 0.15 to 0.20 AUC. On the OR-Bench prompts that margin shrinks to 0.03 to 0.20, and the probes flag 29\% to 42\% of prompts that sound harmful but are benign, so part of what they read is the appearance of harm rather than harm itself. A probe trained on a base model can thus be reused, in these families, after distillation and after instruction tuning; how much it reads beyond textual cues depends on the test set.

**一句话**：该论文检验在基座模型上训练的线性探针能否不经重训直接复用到其蒸馏推理模型与指令微调聊天模型上，并量化表征方向旋转与检测性能变化。

痛点：审计者在基座模型上训练的安全探针，一旦模型被后继版本替换，是否必须重训尚不清楚。方法：在四个基座模型（Qwen2.5-Math-7B、Llama-3.1-8B、Qwen2.5-14B、Qwen2.5-32B）上用 XSTest 训练探针，原样迁移至 DeepSeek-R1 蒸馏模型与官方 chat 模型，并在 XSTest 与从未参与训练的 OR-Bench 上评测。结果：安全/不安全提示的分离方向旋转 17°–35°，但 AUC 仅变化 -0.011~+0.027（XSTest）与 -0.015~+0.053（OR-Bench），且优于仅看 prompt 文本的分类器 0.15–0.20 AUC；不过在 OR-Bench 上仍误标 29%–42% 的“听起来有害实则良性”提示，说明探针部分读的是表面有害性。

> 🔗 **与课题**：支撑环节③（能力迁移）与⑦（评测）。③：这是标准的源域（基座模型）→目标域（蒸馏/指令微调后继模型）的内部表征迁移实证研究，给出“方向发生旋转但读出仍可沿用”的量化证据，可类比本课题“GEE 上学到的工作流推理表征迁移到 QGIS grounding 后是否仍线性可分/可读出”。⑦：AUC 差值与旋转角度共同构成一套可复用的 transfer retention（迁移保持率）度量协议。不涉及①②④⑤⑥，与 workflow→processes+links 结构生成、算子落地、训练手段均无直接关系。

💡 **启发**：可直接借用的做法有二：一是“探针冻结、只换模型”的对照设计加分离方向旋转角，用来量化模型内部 workflow 表征在 GEE→QGIS 迁移前后的漂移；二是设置“只看输入文本”的浅层基线做对照，剥离表面线索贡献——可用来判定模型是在做工作流推理，还是只在匹配 GIS/QGIS 术语（对应幻觉算子率的诊断）。

⚠️ **局限**：验证局限于安全/有害请求检测这一探针任务与四个模型家族内部，探针被证明部分只读取表面线索，外推到工作流推理能力迁移时需重新验证任务效度。

### ⭐⭐⭐ Evolve on Graph: Self-Evolving Knowledge Graph Reasoning without Question Annotations
**6/10** · Zongyuan Sun、Shuai Zhao、Ke Shu、Ziyue Zhu、Shangyang Wu、Yuanlin Lin · arXiv · 2026-10-26 · [原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35598v1) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.35598v1)
*入选：RL×LLM方法*

> 📄 **摘要**：Knowledge graphs (KGs) provide reliable external knowledge for multi-hop factual reasoning with large language models (LLMs), yet most KGs in real-world domains such as finance and industry lack question--answer (QA) annotations. Without such annotations, existing methods struggle to improve LLM graph reasoning: training-free agents cannot improve their exploration through interaction; reinforcement learning (RL) agents depend on QA data annotated by humans or synthesized by closed-source models; and training data is typically built offline, so its topology mix is fixed and cannot follow the agent's ability. We propose Evolve on Graph (EoG), a framework that lets an LLM self-evolve its graph reasoning without any QA annotations. Through Graph Self-Play, a single LLM alternates between a Proposer that poses questions and a Solver that answers them. To make questions verifiable, Symbolically Verifiable Topology Synthesis (SVTS) samples reasoning topologies on the KG and executes the corresponding first-order logic queries, yielding topology skeletons with correct and complete answer sets; guided by a skeleton, the Proposer explores the graph to pose a multi-hop question. To let task topologies follow the Solver, a Boundary-Aware Graph Curriculum rewards the Proposer by the Solver's score variance, keeping questions at the Solver's capability boundary so that complex topologies enter training as the Solver improves. On six benchmarks over Freebase, Wikidata, and an industrial KG, EoG consistently outperforms prompting-, retrieval-, and agent-based methods, showing that LLM graph reasoning can improve through self-play without QA annotations. Our code is available at https://github.com/Szy5/EoG

**一句话**：提出 Evolve on Graph (EoG) 框架：让单个 LLM 在没有任何 QA 标注的知识图谱上，通过 Proposer（出题者）与 Solver（解题者）自博弈，自我进化出多跳图推理能力。
