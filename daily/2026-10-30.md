### ⭐⭐⭐ SGTAgent: An Agent Framework for Geospatial Target Localization from Complex Spatial Semantic Queries
**6/10** · Han Wang、Benkui Zhang、Ying Chang、Linlin Liang、Yuanfei Chang、Chengbo Wang · ISPRS International Journal of Geo-Information · 2026-10-28 · [原文](https://doi.org/10.3390/ijgi15110488)
*入选：强命中*

> 📄 **摘要**：Geospatial target localization refers to determining the geographic location of targets from natural language queries, which is essential for applications such as urban management, emergency response, and location-based services. As user queries evolve from address matching toward complex forms incorporating multiple spatial semantic factors, the localization process increasingly demands the coordination of heterogeneous tools and data sources. Automation remains hindered by the lack of a unified structured representation for semantic factors, the difficulty of translating complex spatial constraints into executable operations, and the unreliability of single-round planning and execution. This study proposes SGTAgent, an agent framework for geospatial target localization from complex spatial semantic queries. The parser transforms queries into structured spatial semantic factors, the planner organizes dependent spatial workflows, and the feedback mechanism distinguishes local parameter refinement from workflow restructuring. Experiments show that SGTAgent achieves higher localization accuracy than the evaluated generic baselines across multiple LLM backbones. Using DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash, SGTAgent achieves localization accuracies of 39.86%, 68.76%, and 81.88% at distance thresholds of 100, 300, and 500 m, respectively, outperforming the evaluated agents under the corresponding experimental configurations. On complex queries using GPT-4o, it achieves an LA@500m of 80.47%, compared with 70.12% for ReAct. Ablation experiments show that removing the complete Restate–Replan feedback mechanism reduces the 500 m localization accuracy from 81.88% to 74.83%, demonstrating the effectiveness of the execution feedback mechanism under the evaluated configuration. An independent evaluation of the parser achieves macro-F1 scores of 89.76% on the original benchmark and 89.62% on independently formulated queries, indicating consistent semantic factor extraction capability across different query formulations.

痛点：复杂空间查询缺少统一的结构化语义表示，空间约束难以转译为可执行操作，单轮规划执行不可靠。方法：parser 抽取结构化空间语义因子，planner 组织有依赖关系的工作流，反馈机制区分参数级修正与流程级重构。结果：在 DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash 上 100/300/500 m 阈值定位准确率为 39.86%/68.76%/81.88%，在 GPT-4o 上 LA@500m 达 80.47%，优于所评估的通用基线智能体。

### ⭐⭐ What Survives Distillation? A Mechanistic Interpretability Study of GPT-2 and DistilGPT-2
**5/10** · Nabeelah Faumi、Deshan Sumanathilaka · arXiv (Cornell University) · 2026-10-27 · [原文](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2610.37157)
*入选：能力迁移*

> 📄 **摘要**：Knowledge distillation compresses large language models into compact students that preserve aggregate metrics, yet the mechanistic consequences of depth-reduction distillation remain largely uncharacterized. This work applies linear CKA, logit lens analysis, attention pattern decomposition, sparse autoencoder feature statistics, and activation patching to compare GPT-2 and its six-layer student DistilGPT-2 across two canonical circuits: the Indirect Object Identification (IOI) circuit and the Greater-Than (GT) circuit. The results reveal a compression-collapse dichotomy: the IOI circuit suffers complete mechanistic collapse (DistilGPT-2: 35.2% accuracy vs. 94.8%, zero name-mover preservation), while the GT circuit survives with 87.7% task performance recovery through active remapping of the teacher's layer-9 comparison head into student layer~4 (mean Pearson r = 0.945). These findings demonstrate that circuit compressibility governs mechanistic survival under distillation, and that aggregate metrics are insufficient diagnostics for circuit integrity.

**一句话**：用线性CKA、logit lens、注意力模式分解、稀疏自编码器特征统计与激活修补，机制层面比较GPT-2与六层蒸馏学生DistilGPT-2在IOI与Greater-Than两条电路上的存留情况。

痛点：知识蒸馏只用聚合指标衡量学生模型是否“保真”，但深度压缩对内部电路机制的破坏缺乏刻画。方法：对GPT-2与DistilGPT-2在IOI与GT两套经典电路上做多工具机制分析（CKA、logit lens、注意力分解、SAE特征、activation patching）。结果：呈现“压缩—崩塌”二分：IOI电路完全机制崩塌（35.2% vs 94.8%，name-mover零保留），而GT电路将教师第9层比较头重映射到学生第4层（Pearson r=0.945）实现87.7%任务性能恢复，说明电路可压缩性决定机制存留，聚合指标不足以诊断电路完整性。
